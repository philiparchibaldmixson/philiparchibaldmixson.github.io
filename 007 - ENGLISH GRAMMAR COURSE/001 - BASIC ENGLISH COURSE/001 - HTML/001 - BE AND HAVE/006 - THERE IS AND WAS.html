📍 THERE IS / THERE ARE 📍

Express Existence and Location

🌟 What is "There Is/Are"?

The expressions "there is" and "there are" are used to indicate the existence or presence of something or someone. They are essential structures in English for talking about what exists in a particular place or situation.

These expressions are different from the verb "to be" alone because they specifically introduce new information about the existence or location of things. We use "there is" for singular nouns and "there are" for plural nouns. Understanding this structure will help you describe places, talk about availability, and introduce topics naturally.

📋 Present Tense: There Is / There Are

In the present tense, we choose between "there is" and "there are" based on whether the noun is singular or plural.

Form When to Use Example
There is (singular) One thing or person There is a book on the table.
There is (uncountable) Uncountable nouns There is water in the bottle.
There are (plural) Two or more things/people There are three apples in the basket.
📝 More Examples:

Singular:
• There is a cat in the garden.
• There is a problem with the computer.
• There is milk in the refrigerator.

Plural:
• There are five students in the classroom.
• There are many cars on the street.
• There are two bathrooms in this house.

💡 Contraction

There is → There's (very common in spoken English)
Example: "There's a new restaurant downtown."

Note: "There are" doesn't have a common contraction in standard English.

🎯 Uses of "There Is/Are"

The structure "there is/are" has several important uses in English:

1. Introducing New Information
To tell someone about something they don't know about

• There is a new student in our class.
• There are some cookies in the kitchen.
• There is an important meeting tomorrow.

2. Describing Locations & Places
To say what exists in a particular place

• There is a bank on Main Street.
• There are two parks in this city.
• There is a beautiful beach nearby.

3. Talking About Quantity
To indicate how many or how much of something exists

• There are 30 students in my class.
• There is enough food for everyone.
• There are too many people here.

4. Expressing Availability
To say what is or isn't available

• There is no time to waste.
• There are tickets available for the concert.
• There is space for one more person.

⏰ Past Tense: There Was / There Were

In the past tense, we use "there was" for singular and "there were" for plural, following the same pattern as the verb "to be."

Form When to Use Example
There was (singular) One thing or person (past) There was a party last night.
There was (uncountable) Uncountable nouns (past) There was traffic on the highway.
There were (plural) Two or more things/people (past) There were many people at the event.
📝 Past Tense Examples:

Singular (was):
• There was a storm yesterday.
• There was an accident on the corner.
• There was a movie on TV last night.

Plural (were):
• There were three tests last week.
• There were many tourists in the city.
• There were problems with the system.

💡 Time Expressions

Common time words used with "there was/were":
• Yesterday, last week/month/year
• Ago (two days ago, a year ago)
• In the past, before, when I was young

❌ Negative Forms

To make negative sentences, add "not" or use "no" before the noun.

Present Negative:

Method 1: is/are + not
• There is not a solution to this problem. (There isn't)
• There are not any chairs available. (There aren't)

Method 2: is/are + no (more common)
• There is no solution to this problem.
• There are no chairs available.
• There is no milk in the fridge.
• There are no classes today.

Past Negative:

Method 1: was/were + not
• There was not enough time. (There wasn't)
• There were not any people there. (There weren't)

Method 2: was/were + no (more common)
• There was no time left.
• There were no seats available.
• There was no food at the party.
• There were no problems yesterday.

💡 Common Contractions

• There isn't = There is not
• There aren't = There are not
• There wasn't = There was not
• There weren't = There were not

❓ Question Forms

To form questions, move "is/are" or "was/were" before "there."

Present Questions:

Yes/No Questions:
• Is there a bank near here?
• Are there any questions?
• Is there enough time?
• Are there students in the classroom?

Wh- Questions:
• How many students are there?
• What is there to eat?
• Where is there a good restaurant?
• Why are there so many people?

Past Questions:

Yes/No Questions:
• Was there a meeting yesterday?
• Were there many people at the party?
• Was there a problem?
• Were there any messages for me?

Wh- Questions:
• How many people were there?
• What was there to do?
• Where was there a problem?
• Why were there delays?

💡 Short Answers

Q: Is there a library nearby?
A: Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.

Q: Were there many guests?
A: Yes, there were. / No, there weren't.

🔮 Future Form: There Will Be

For the future tense, we use "there will be" for both singular and plural nouns.

Future Structure
There + will be + noun (singular or plural)

Singular:
• There will be a test next week.
• There will be a party tomorrow.
• There will be time to talk later.

Plural:
• There will be many guests at the wedding.
• There will be new opportunities in the future.
• There will be changes next year.

Future Negative & Questions:

Negative: There will not be / There won't be
• There won't be classes tomorrow.
• There will not be any problems.

Questions: Will there be...?
• Will there be food at the party?
• Will there be enough time?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ There have a book on the table.
✅ There is a book on the table.

❌ There are a problem.
✅ There is a problem.

❌ Is there cats in the garden?
✅ Are there cats in the garden?

❌ There was many people.
✅ There were many people.

❌ It is a book on the table. (when introducing new info)
✅ There is a book on the table.

Complete Overview: All Tenses

Present: There is a party today. / There are guests here.
Past: There was a party yesterday. / There were many guests.
Future: There will be a party tomorrow. / There will be guests.

Negative Present: There is no time. / There are no seats.
Negative Past: There was no time. / There were no seats.
Negative Future: There won't be time. / There won't be seats.
