☀️ PRESENT SIMPLE – AFFIRMATIVE ☀️

Expressing Daily Actions and Facts

🌟 What Is Present Affirmative?

The Present Simple Affirmative is used to describe habits, routines, general truths, and facts. It tells us what people do regularly or what is always true.

In this form, we use the base verb for most subjects, but we add -s or -es for he / she / it.

📋 Structure of Present Affirmative

Subject Verb Form Example
I / You / We / They Base verb I play football.
They work in a bank.
He / She / It Verb + s/es She plays football.
He watches TV.

💡 Spelling Rules

• Add -s: play → plays
• Add -es: watch → watches, go → goes
• Change -y to -ies: study → studies

🎯 Common Uses of Present Affirmative

1. Daily Routines
To describe regular habits

• I wake up at 6 AM.
• She drinks coffee every morning.
• They go to school by bus.

2. General Truths
To state facts or universal truths

• The sun rises in the east.
• Water boils at 100°C.
• Cats hate water.

3. Scheduled Events
To describe timetables or programs

• The train leaves at 8 PM.
• The movie starts at 7.
• School begins at 7:30 AM.

📝 More Examples

👤 I / You / We / They

• I study English.
• You work hard.
• We live in Brazil.
• They play video games.

👥 He / She / It

• He reads books.
• She watches Netflix.
• It rains a lot here.
• My dog sleeps all day.

📌 Verb + s/es Examples

Play → Plays
She plays tennis.
Go → Goes
He goes to work.
Watch → Watches
She watches TV.
Study → Studies
He studies math.
Have → Has
She has a car.

🔄 Time Expressions with Present Simple

Common Time Phrases

• Every day
• Usually
• Always
• Sometimes
• On Mondays
• At night
• In the morning

Example: I usually eat breakfast at 7 AM.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ She play football.
✅ She plays football.

❌ He go to school.
✅ He goes to school.

❌ I watches TV.
✅ I watch TV.

❌ They studies English.
✅ They study English.
