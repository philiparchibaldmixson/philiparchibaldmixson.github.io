🚫 SIMPLE PRESENT – NEGATIVE FORM 🚫

Expressing What Doesn’t Happen in the Present

🌟 What Is the Negative Form?

The negative form of the Simple Present is used to say that something does not happen regularly or is not true in general.

To make a sentence negative, we use do not or does not + the base form of the verb.

📋 Structure of Present Negative

Subject Auxiliary Main Verb Example
I / You / We / They do not (don’t) base verb They don’t like coffee.
He / She / It does not (doesn’t) base verb She doesn’t eat meat.

💡 Important Rule

In negative sentences, do/does takes the tense. The main verb stays in its base form:
❌ She doesn’t eats → ✅ She doesn’t eat

🎯 When to Use the Negative Form

1. Daily Routines
To say what someone doesn’t do regularly

• I don’t drink soda.
• He doesn’t go to the gym.
• They don’t study on weekends.

2. Preferences
To express dislikes or lack of interest

• She doesn’t like chocolate.
• We don’t enjoy horror movies.
• He doesn’t play video games.

3. General Truths
To say what is not true in general

• Water doesn’t burn.
• Cats don’t bark.
• The sun doesn’t rise in the west.

📝 More Examples

👤 I / You / We / They

• I don’t eat meat.
• You don’t work on Sundays.
• We don’t live in Canada.
• They don’t speak French.

👥 He / She / It

• He doesn’t like coffee.
• She doesn’t drive.
• It doesn’t work properly.
• My dog doesn’t bark at night.

📌 Contractions

do not → don’t
I don’t like pizza.
does not → doesn’t
She doesn’t play tennis.

🔄 Time Expressions

Common Time Phrases

• Every day
• On weekends
• At night
• In the morning
• Usually
• Often
• Sometimes

Example: He doesn’t work on weekends.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ She doesn’t eats meat.
✅ She doesn’t eat meat.

❌ They doesn’t like music.
✅ They don’t like music.

❌ I don’t goes to school.
✅ I don’t go to school.

❌ He don’t play soccer.
✅ He doesn’t play soccer.
