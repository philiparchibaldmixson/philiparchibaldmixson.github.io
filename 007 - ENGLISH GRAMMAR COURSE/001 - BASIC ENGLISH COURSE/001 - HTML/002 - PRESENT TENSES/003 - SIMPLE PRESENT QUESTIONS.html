❓ SIMPLE PRESENT – QUESTIONS ❓

Asking About Habits, Routines, and Facts

🌟 What Are Simple Present Questions?

We use Simple Present Questions to ask about routines, habits, general truths, and facts. These questions often begin with Do or Does.

Use Do for I, you, we, they and Does for he, she, it. The main verb always stays in the base form.

📋 Structure of Present Simple Questions

Subject Auxiliary Main Verb Example
I / You / We / They Do Base verb Do you like pizza?
He / She / It Does Base verb Does she play tennis?

💡 Important Rule

In questions, do/does carries the tense. The main verb stays in its base form:
❌ Does she plays? → ✅ Does she play?

🎯 Types of Questions

1. Yes/No Questions
To confirm or check information

• Do you speak English?
• Does he work here?
• Do they live in Brazil?

2. Wh- Questions
To ask for specific information

• What do you do?
• Where does she live?
• When do they arrive?
• Why does he study English?
• How do you travel to school?

📝 More Examples

👤 Do + I / You / We / They

• Do you like music?
• Do they play soccer?
• Do we have class today?
• Do I need a ticket?

👥 Does + He / She / It

• Does he speak Spanish?
• Does she work on weekends?
• Does it rain a lot here?
• Does your dog bark at night?

📌 Short Answers

Do you like coffee?
Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
Does she drive?
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.
Do they study English?
Yes, they do. / No, they don’t.
Does it work?
Yes, it does. / No, it doesn’t.

🔄 Time Expressions

Common Time Phrases

• Every day
• On weekends
• At night
• In the morning
• Usually
• Sometimes
• Once a week

Example: Do you go to the gym on weekends?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ Does she plays tennis?
✅ Does she play tennis?

❌ Do he like pizza?
✅ Does he like pizza?

❌ Do she works here?
✅ Does she work here?

❌ Does it rains a lot?
✅ Does it rain a lot?
