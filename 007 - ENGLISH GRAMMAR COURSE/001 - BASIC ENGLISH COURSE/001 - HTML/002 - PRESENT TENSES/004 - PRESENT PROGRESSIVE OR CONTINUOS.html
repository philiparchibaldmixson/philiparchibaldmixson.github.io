⏳ PRESENT PROGRESSIVE – FORMS ⏳

Describing Actions Happening Now

🌟 What Is the Present Progressive?

The Present Progressive (also called Present Continuous) is used to describe actions that are happening right now, or around the current moment.

It is formed with the verb to be (am/is/are) + verb + ing.

📋 Structure of Present Progressive

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing She is studying now.
Negative Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing They are not working today.
Question Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing? Are you listening?

💡 Quick Tip

I → am
He/She/It → is
You/We/They → are
Then add verb + ing

🎯 Uses of Present Progressive

1. Actions Happening Now
To describe what is happening at this moment

• I am talking to you.
• She is cooking dinner.
• They are playing soccer.

2. Temporary Actions
To describe actions happening around now, but not always

• He is staying with his cousin this week.
• I am working on a new project.
• We are learning Spanish this semester.

3. Changing Situations
To describe things that are developing or changing

• The weather is getting colder.
• Prices are rising.
• Technology is improving fast.

📝 Examples by Subject

👤 Affirmative

• I am studying English.
• You are watching TV.
• He is reading a book.
• She is writing a letter.
• It is raining.
• We are working together.
• They are dancing.

🚫 Negative

• I am not sleeping.
• He is not listening.
• We are not going out.
• They are not playing today.

❓ Questions

• Are you studying?
• Is she cooking?
• Am I doing this right?
• Are they coming now?

📌 Verb + ing Rules

play → playing
He is playing football.
run → running
She is running fast.
write → writing
I am writing a story.
swim → swimming
They are swimming now.
make → making
We are making dinner.

🔄 Time Expressions

Common Time Phrases

• Now
• At the moment
• Today
• This week
• Right now
• Currently

Example: She is studying at the moment.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He is play football.
✅ He is playing football.

❌ They are works now.
✅ They are working now.

❌ She not is cooking.
✅ She is not cooking.

❌ Are she writing?
✅ Is she writing?
