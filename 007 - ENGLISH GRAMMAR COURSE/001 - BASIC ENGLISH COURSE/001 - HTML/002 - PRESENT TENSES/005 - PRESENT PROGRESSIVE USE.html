🎬 PRESENT PROGRESSIVE – USE 🎬

Describing Actions Happening Now and Around Now

🌟 What Is the Present Progressive Used For?

The Present Progressive (also called Present Continuous) is used to describe actions that are happening right now, actions happening around now, and situations that are temporary or changing.

It is formed with am / is / are + verb + ing.

🎯 Main Uses of Present Progressive

1. Actions Happening Now
To describe what is happening at this exact moment

• I am talking to you.
• She is cooking dinner.
• They are playing soccer.
• He is writing a message.
• We are watching a movie.

2. Temporary Actions
To describe actions happening around now, but not always

• I am staying at my cousin’s house this week.
• She is working on a new project.
• We are learning French this semester.
• He is living in São Paulo for a few months.

3. Changing Situations
To describe things that are developing or changing

• The weather is getting colder.
• Prices are rising.
• Technology is improving fast.
• My English is getting better.

4. Future Arrangements
To describe planned future events (with time expressions)

• I am meeting my friend tomorrow.
• She is flying to Salvador next week.
• We are having dinner with the team tonight.
• They are visiting Bahia this weekend.

🕐 Time Expressions with Present Progressive

Now
"I am studying now."
At the moment
"She is working at the moment."
Today / This week
"We are traveling this week."
Right now
"They are playing right now."
Tonight / Tomorrow
"I am meeting her tomorrow."

📌 Verb + ing Rules

run → running
He is running fast.
write → writing
She is writing a story.
swim → swimming
They are swimming now.
make → making
We are making dinner.
study → studying
I am studying English.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He is play football.
✅ He is playing football.

❌ They are works now.
✅ They are working now.

❌ She not is cooking.
✅ She is not cooking.

❌ Are she writing?
✅ Is she writing?
