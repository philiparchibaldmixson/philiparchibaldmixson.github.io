🚫 PRESENT PROGRESSIVE – NEGATIVE FORM 🚫

Expressing What Is Not Happening Now

🌟 What Is the Negative Form?

The negative form of the Present Progressive is used to say that something is not happening right now or is not happening temporarily.

To make a sentence negative, we use am / is / are + not + verb-ing.

📋 Structure of Present Progressive Negative

Subject Verb to Be Negative Structure Example
I am am not + verb-ing I am not working now.
He / She / It is is not (isn’t) + verb-ing She isn’t studying today.
You / We / They are are not (aren’t) + verb-ing They aren’t playing football.

💡 Contractions

is not → isn’t
are not → aren’t
am not has no contraction

🎯 When to Use the Negative Form

1. Actions Not Happening Now
To say what is not happening at this moment

• I am not talking right now.
• She isn’t cooking.
• They aren’t watching TV.
• He isn’t writing a message.

2. Temporary Actions Not Occurring
To describe temporary situations that are not happening

• I am not staying at my cousin’s house this week.
• She isn’t working on that project anymore.
• We aren’t learning French this semester.
• He isn’t living in Salvador now.

3. Future Plans Not Happening
To describe future arrangements that are not happening

• I am not meeting her tomorrow.
• They aren’t traveling this weekend.
• We aren’t having class tonight.
• She isn’t visiting Bahia next week.

📝 More Negative Examples

👤 I / You / We / They

• I am not sleeping.
• You aren’t listening.
• We aren’t studying.
• They aren’t working today.

👥 He / She / It

• He isn’t reading.
• She isn’t writing.
• It isn’t raining.
• My dog isn’t barking.

📌 Verb + ing Review

run → running
She isn’t running now.
write → writing
He isn’t writing a letter.
swim → swimming
They aren’t swimming today.
make → making
We aren’t making dinner.
study → studying
I am not studying now.

🔄 Time Expressions

Common Time Phrases

• Now
• At the moment
• Today
• This week
• Right now
• Tonight / Tomorrow

Example: She isn’t working at the moment.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He not is playing.
✅ He is not playing.

❌ They are not play.
✅ They are not playing.

❌ She isn’t writes.
✅ She isn’t writing.

❌ I amn’t working.
✅ I am not working.
