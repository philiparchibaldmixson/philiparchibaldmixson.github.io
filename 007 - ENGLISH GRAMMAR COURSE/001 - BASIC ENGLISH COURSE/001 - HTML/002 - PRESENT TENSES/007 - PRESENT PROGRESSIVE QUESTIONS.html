❓ PRESENT PROGRESSIVE – QUESTIONS ❓

Asking About Actions Happening Now

🌟 What Are Present Progressive Questions?

We use Present Progressive Questions to ask about actions that are happening right now, temporarily, or around this moment.

To form questions, we use am / is / are + subject + verb-ing.

📋 Structure of Present Progressive Questions

Form Structure Example
Yes/No Question Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing? Are you studying?
Wh- Question Wh-word + am/is/are + subject + verb-ing? What is she doing?

💡 Quick Tip

I → Am I...?
He/She/It → Is he/she/it...?
You/We/They → Are you/we/they...?

🎯 Types of Questions

1. Yes/No Questions
To confirm or check what is happening

• Are you listening?
• Is she cooking?
• Am I doing this right?
• Are they playing soccer?
• Is it raining?

2. Wh- Questions
To ask for specific information

• What are you doing?
• Where is he going?
• Why are they shouting?
• Who is she talking to?
• When are we leaving?
• How is he feeling?

📌 Short Answers

Are you studying?
Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Is she cooking?
Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.
Are they playing?
Yes, they are. / No, they aren’t.
Am I doing this right?
Yes, you are. / No, you aren’t.

🔄 Time Expressions

Common Time Phrases

• Now
• At the moment
• Today
• This week
• Right now
• Tonight / Tomorrow

Example: What are you doing right now?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ Are she cooking?
✅ Is she cooking?

❌ Is they playing?
✅ Are they playing?

❌ What she is doing?
✅ What is she doing?

❌ You are working?
✅ Are you working?
