🔮 FUTURE WITH "WILL" – PREDICTING 🔮

Making Predictions About the Future

🌟 What Is "Will" for Future?

We use "will" to talk about the future when we are making predictions, spontaneous decisions, or promises. In this lesson, we focus on using "will" to predict what we think will happen.

The structure is: Subject + will + base verb

📋 Structure of "Will" for Predictions

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + will + verb She will win the race.
Negative Subject + will not (won’t) + verb It won’t rain tomorrow.
Question Will + subject + verb? Will they arrive on time?

💡 Quick Tip

Will is the same for all subjects:
I will, You will, He will, She will, It will, We will, They will

🎯 When to Use "Will" for Predictions

1. Predictions Without Evidence
To say what we think will happen in the future

• I think it will snow next week.
• She will be a great teacher.
• They will win the game.
• You will love this movie.

2. Beliefs About the Future
To express opinions or beliefs about what will happen

• People will live on Mars one day.
• Technology will change everything.
• The world will be different in 50 years.
• Students will study online more often.

📝 More Examples

✅ Affirmative

• I will call you later.
• He will pass the exam.
• We will travel to Europe.
• They will enjoy the concert.

🚫 Negative

• I won’t forget your birthday.
• She won’t be late.
• We won’t stay long.
• It won’t be easy.

❓ Questions

• Will you come to the party?
• Will it be cold tomorrow?
• Will she like the gift?
• What will happen next?

📌 Time Expressions with "Will"

Tomorrow
"I will call you tomorrow."
Next week/month/year
"She will start college next year."
Soon
"They will arrive soon."
In the future
"People will drive electric cars in the future."
Later
"We will talk later."

🔄 "Will" vs "Going To"

Comparison

Will: Use for predictions, promises, or decisions made at the moment
• "I think it will rain."
• "I’ll help you with that."

Going to: Use for plans or predictions with evidence
• "I’m going to visit my grandma." (plan)
• "Look at the sky! It’s going to rain." (evidence)

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He will goes to school.
✅ He will go to school.

❌ Will comes she?
✅ Will she come?

❌ I no will do it.
✅ I won’t do it.

❌ They will to travel.
✅ They will travel.
