🤝 FUTURE WITH "WILL" – DECIDING, REFUSING, PROMISING 🤝

Expressing Intentions and Reactions in the Future

🌟 What Is "Will" Used For?

We use "will" not only to make predictions, but also to express decisions, refusals, and promises. These uses show how we react or commit to something in the moment.

The structure is: Subject + will + base verb

📋 Structure of "Will" for Intentions

Function Structure Example
Deciding Subject + will + verb I’ll help you with that.
Refusing Subject + will not (won’t) + verb He won’t go to the party.
Promising Subject + will + verb We’ll never forget this moment.

💡 Quick Tip

Will is used for spontaneous reactions and commitments. It’s perfect for expressing what you decide or promise in the moment.

🎯 Uses of "Will" for Decisions, Refusals, and Promises

1. Making Decisions
To decide something at the moment of speaking

• I’ll answer the phone.
• We’ll take the bus.
• She’ll talk to him now.
• They’ll order pizza.

2. Refusing to Do Something
To say you won’t do something

• I won’t lie to you.
• He won’t clean his room.
• We won’t accept that offer.
• They won’t come tonight.

3. Making Promises
To commit to something in the future

• I’ll always support you.
• She’ll be there for you.
• We’ll help you move.
• They’ll never forget this day.

🔄 "Will" vs "Going To"

Comparison

Will: Use for decisions, promises, refusals, and spontaneous reactions
• "I’ll help you." (decision)
• "We won’t do that." (refusal)
• "I’ll always love you." (promise)

Going to: Use for plans and predictions with evidence
• "I’m going to visit my aunt." (plan)
• "Look! It’s going to rain." (evidence)

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I will to help you.
✅ I will help you.

❌ Will goes she?
✅ Will she go?

❌ I no will do it.
✅ I won’t do it.

❌ They will helps you.
✅ They will help you.
