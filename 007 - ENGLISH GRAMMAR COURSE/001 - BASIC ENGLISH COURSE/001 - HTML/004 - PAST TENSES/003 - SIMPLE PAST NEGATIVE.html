🚫 SIMPLE PAST – NEGATIVE FORM 🚫

Expressing What Didn’t Happen in the Past

🌟 What Is the Negative Form?

The negative form of the Simple Past is used to say that something did not happen in the past. It’s useful for correcting information, expressing absence of action, or denying past events.

To make a sentence negative, we use did not (didn’t) + base verb.

📋 Structure of Simple Past Negative

Subject Auxiliary Main Verb Example
I / You / He / She / It / We / They did not (didn’t) base verb She didn’t go to school.

💡 Important Rule

In negative sentences, did takes the past tense. The main verb stays in its base form:
❌ She didn’t went → ✅ She didn’t go

🎯 When to Use the Negative Form

1. Denying Past Actions
To say something didn’t happen

• I didn’t eat breakfast.
• He didn’t call me.
• They didn’t arrive on time.
• We didn’t go to the party.

2. Correcting Information
To fix a wrong assumption

• She didn’t travel last week.
• I didn’t see that movie.
• He didn’t win the game.
• We didn’t meet yesterday.

3. Expressing Absence
To say something was missing or didn’t occur

• There wasn’t any food.
• I didn’t hear anything.
• She didn’t feel well.
• They didn’t show up.

📝 More Negative Examples

👤 I / You / We / They

• I didn’t study.
• You didn’t call.
• We didn’t finish the project.
• They didn’t play soccer.

👥 He / She / It

• He didn’t sleep well.
• She didn’t come to class.
• It didn’t work yesterday.
• My dog didn’t bark.

📌 Common Verbs in Negative Form

go → didn’t go
She didn’t go to school.
eat → didn’t eat
I didn’t eat lunch.
see → didn’t see
We didn’t see the movie.
do → didn’t do
He didn’t do his homework.
have → didn’t have
They didn’t have time.

🔄 Time Expressions with Simple Past

Common Time Phrases

• Yesterday
• Last night
• Two days ago
• In 2020
• This morning
• When I was a child

Example: I didn’t go to school yesterday.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ She didn’t went to school.
✅ She didn’t go to school.

❌ I didn’t saw the movie.
✅ I didn’t see the movie.

❌ They didn’t played soccer.
✅ They didn’t play soccer.

❌ He didn’t was happy.
✅ He wasn’t happy.
