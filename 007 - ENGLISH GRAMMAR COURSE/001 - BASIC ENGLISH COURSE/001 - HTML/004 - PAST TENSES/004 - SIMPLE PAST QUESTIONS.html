❓ SIMPLE PAST – QUESTIONS ❓

Asking About Actions That Happened in the Past

🌟 What Are Simple Past Questions?

We use Simple Past Questions to ask about actions or events that happened and finished in the past. These questions help us get information about past experiences, routines, or facts.

To form questions, we use Did + subject + base verb.

📋 Structure of Simple Past Questions

Form Structure Example
Yes/No Question Did + subject + base verb? Did you study last night?
Wh- Question Wh-word + did + subject + base verb? Where did she go?

💡 Quick Tip

Use did for all subjects. The main verb stays in its base form:
❌ Did she went? → ✅ Did she go?

🎯 Types of Questions

1. Yes/No Questions
To confirm or check past actions

• Did you go to school?
• Did he call you?
• Did they arrive on time?
• Did she eat breakfast?

2. Wh- Questions
To ask for specific information

• What did you do yesterday?
• Where did she go?
• When did they leave?
• Why did he cry?
• How did you get here?

📌 Common Verbs in Questions

go → Did you go?
"Did you go to the party?"
eat → Did she eat?
"Did she eat lunch?"
see → Did they see?
"Did they see the movie?"
do → Did he do?
"Did he do his homework?"
have → Did we have?
"Did we have class yesterday?"

🔄 Time Expressions with Simple Past

Common Time Phrases

• Yesterday
• Last night
• Two days ago
• In 2020
• This morning
• When I was a child

Example: What did you do yesterday?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ Did she went to school?
✅ Did she go to school?

❌ Did you studied?
✅ Did you study?

❌ What did he saw?
✅ What did he see?

❌ Did they was happy?
✅ Were they happy?
