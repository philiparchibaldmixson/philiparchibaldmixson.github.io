⏳ PAST PROGRESSIVE ⏳

Describing Actions in Progress in the Past

🌟 What Is the Past Progressive?

The Past Progressive (also called Past Continuous) is used to describe actions that were in progress at a specific moment in the past. It often sets the scene or shows that something was happening when another action occurred.

It is formed with was / were + verb-ing.

📋 Structure of Past Progressive

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + was/were + verb-ing She was studying at 8 PM.
Negative Subject + was/were + not + verb-ing They weren’t working yesterday.
Question Was/Were + subject + verb-ing? Were you sleeping?

💡 Quick Tip

I / He / She / It → was
You / We / They → were
Then add verb + ing

🎯 Uses of Past Progressive

1. Action in Progress at a Specific Time
To describe what was happening at a moment in the past

• I was watching TV at 9 PM.
• She was cooking dinner when I arrived.
• They were playing soccer yesterday afternoon.
• We were studying all night.

2. Interrupted Action
To show that one action was interrupted by another

• I was sleeping when the phone rang.
• He was walking when it started to rain.
• They were talking when the teacher came in.
• She was driving when the car broke down.

3. Two Actions Happening at the Same Time
To describe simultaneous actions in the past

• I was reading while she was writing.
• They were dancing while the band was playing.
• We were studying while it was raining.
• He was cooking while I was cleaning.

📝 More Examples

✅ Affirmative

• I was studying.
• You were working.
• He was sleeping.
• We were traveling.
• They were laughing.

🚫 Negative

• I wasn’t studying.
• She wasn’t listening.
• We weren’t playing.
• They weren’t watching TV.

❓ Questions

• Were you studying?
• Was he sleeping?
• Were they working?
• What were you doing?
• Why was she crying?

📌 Verb + ing Rules

run → running
He was running fast.
write → writing
She was writing a story.
swim → swimming
They were swimming in the river.
make → making
We were making dinner.
study → studying
I was studying English.

🔄 Time Expressions with Past Progressive

Common Time Phrases

• At 7 PM
• Yesterday at noon
• While
• When
• All night
• During the class

Example: I was reading when the lights went out.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He were sleeping.
✅ He was sleeping.

❌ They was playing.
✅ They were playing.

❌ Was you working?
✅ Were you working?

❌ She wasn’t listened.
✅ She wasn’t listening.
