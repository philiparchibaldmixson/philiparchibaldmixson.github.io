⏳ SIMPLE PAST OR PAST PROGRESSIVE? ⏳

Understanding the Difference Between Two Past Tenses

🌟 What’s the Difference?

Both Simple Past and Past Progressive describe actions in the past, but they are used in different ways:

  • Simple Past: Describes a completed action in the past.
  • Past Progressive: Describes an action in progress at a specific time in the past.

📋 Structure Comparison

Tense Structure Example
Simple Past Subject + past verb She watched a movie.
Past Progressive Subject + was/were + verb-ing She was watching a movie.

💡 Quick Tip

Use Simple Past for short, finished actions. Use Past Progressive for longer actions or background situations.

🎯 When to Use Each Tense

1. Completed vs. Ongoing Actions
Use Simple Past for finished actions. Use Past Progressive for actions in progress.

• I watched TV last night. (Simple Past)
• I was watching TV when you called. (Past Progressive)

2. Interrupted Actions
Use Past Progressive for the long action, Simple Past for the interruption.

• She was cooking when the phone rang.
• They were walking when it started to rain.

3. Parallel Actions
Use Past Progressive for two actions happening at the same time.

• I was reading while she was writing.
• They were dancing while the band was playing.

📝 More Examples

✅ Simple Past

• I studied English.
• He arrived late.
• They played soccer.
• We watched a movie.

✅ Past Progressive

• I was studying English.
• He was arriving when the bell rang.
• They were playing soccer at 4 PM.
• We were watching a movie when the lights went out.

📌 Time Expressions

Simple Past
Yesterday, last night, two days ago, in 2020
Past Progressive
At 7 PM, while, when, all afternoon

🔄 Mixed Sentences

Combining Both Tenses

• I was walking to school when I saw a cat.
• She was studying when the power went out.
• They were playing outside when it started to rain.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I was go to school.
✅ I was going to school.

❌ She was cook dinner.
✅ She was cooking dinner.

❌ They played when I was sleep.
✅ They played when I was sleeping.

❌ He was watched TV.
✅ He was watching TV.

Use Simple Past for Finished Actions and Past Progressive for Actions in Progress!

Mix both tenses to tell rich and clear stories about the past!
