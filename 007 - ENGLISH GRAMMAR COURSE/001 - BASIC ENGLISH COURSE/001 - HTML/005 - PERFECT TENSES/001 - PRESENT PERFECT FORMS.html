✅ PRESENT PERFECT – FORMS ✅

Talking About Experiences and Unfinished Time

🌟 What Is the Present Perfect?

The Present Perfect is used to talk about actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past or started in the past and continue now. It connects the past with the present.

It is formed with have / has + past participle.

📋 Structure of Present Perfect

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + have/has + past participle She has visited Salvador.
Negative Subject + have/has + not + past participle They haven’t finished the project.
Question Have/Has + subject + past participle? Have you seen that movie?

💡 Quick Tip

I / You / We / They → have
He / She / It → has
Then add past participle (3rd verb form)

🎯 When to Use the Present Perfect

1. Life Experiences
To talk about things you’ve done (no specific time)

• I’ve visited Rio de Janeiro.
• She has eaten sushi.
• We’ve traveled to Europe.
• They’ve met famous people.

2. Unfinished Time
To describe actions in a time period that’s still happening

• I’ve studied a lot this week.
• He has worked hard today.
• We’ve had three classes so far.
• She has read five books this month.

3. Actions with Present Results
To show that a past action affects the present

• I’ve lost my keys. (I don’t have them now)
• She has broken her phone. (It doesn’t work)
• We’ve finished the homework. (It’s done)
• He has cleaned the room. (It’s clean now)

📝 More Examples

✅ Affirmative

• I’ve seen that movie.
• He has visited Bahia.
• We’ve learned a lot.
• They’ve arrived.

🚫 Negative

• I haven’t seen that movie.
• She hasn’t visited Bahia.
• We haven’t learned much.
• They haven’t arrived yet.

❓ Questions

• Have you seen that movie?
• Has she visited Bahia?
• Have we learned enough?
• Have they arrived?

📌 Common Past Participles

go → gone
She has gone to the store.
eat → eaten
I’ve eaten lunch already.
see → seen
We’ve seen that show.
do → done
He has done his homework.
write → written
They’ve written a letter.

🔄 Time Expressions with Present Perfect

Common Time Phrases

• Ever
• Never
• Already
• Yet
• Just
• Since
• For

Example: I’ve never been to São Paulo.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I have saw that movie.
✅ I have seen that movie.

❌ She has went to school.
✅ She has gone to school.

❌ We haven’t ate yet.
✅ We haven’t eaten yet.

❌ Has you finished?
✅ Have you finished?
