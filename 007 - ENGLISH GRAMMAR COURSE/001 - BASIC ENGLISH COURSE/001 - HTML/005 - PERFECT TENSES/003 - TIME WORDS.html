⏰ TIME WORDS: PRESENT PERFECT OR SIMPLE PAST? ⏰

Choosing the Right Tense Based on Time Expressions

🌟 Why Do Time Words Matter?

Some time expressions are used with the Present Perfect, and others with the Simple Past. Choosing the correct tense depends on whether the time is finished or unfinished.

  • Simple Past: Use with finished time (yesterday, last week, in 2020...)
  • Present Perfect: Use with unfinished time (today, this week, ever, never...)

📋 Time Word Comparison

Time Word Use With Example
Yesterday Simple Past I saw him yesterday.
This week Present Perfect I’ve studied a lot this week.
Last year Simple Past She traveled to Bahia last year.
Ever / Never Present Perfect Have you ever been to Salvador?
In 2020 Simple Past We moved to Itamaraju in 2020.
Already / Yet Present Perfect I’ve already finished. / Have you finished yet?

💡 Quick Tip

If the time is finished, use Simple Past.
If the time is still happening or unspecified, use Present Perfect.

🎯 Time Words with Each Tense

1. Present Perfect Time Words
Used with unfinished or unspecified time

• Ever / Never
• Already / Yet
• Just / Recently
• So far / Until now
• Today / This week / This month
• Since / For

2. Simple Past Time Words
Used with finished time

• Yesterday
• Last night / Last week / Last year
• Two days ago
• In 2020 / In June
• On Monday
• When I was a child

📝 More Examples

✅ Present Perfect

• I’ve never eaten sushi.
• She has already left.
• We’ve studied a lot this week.
• Have you finished yet?

✅ Simple Past

• I ate sushi yesterday.
• She left at 8 AM.
• We studied last night.
• Did you finish the homework?

📌 Mixed Sentences

Present Perfect
"I’ve read three books this month."
Simple Past
"I read that book last month."
Present Perfect
"She has already arrived."
Simple Past
"She arrived at 7 PM."

🔄 Common Mistakes

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I’ve seen him yesterday.
✅ I saw him yesterday.

❌ She has gone to Bahia last year.
✅ She went to Bahia last year.

❌ Did you ever visit Itacaré?
✅ Have you ever visited Itacaré?

❌ We’ve finished the project two days ago.
✅ We finished the project two days ago.

Use Time Words to Choose the Right Tense!

Finished time → Simple Past. Unfinished or unspecified time → Present Perfect.
