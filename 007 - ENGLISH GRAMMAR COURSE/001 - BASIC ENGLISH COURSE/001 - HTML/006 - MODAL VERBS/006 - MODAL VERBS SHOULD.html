💬 MODAL VERB: SHOULD 💬

Giving Advice, Suggestions, and Opinions

🌟 What Does "Should" Mean?

SHOULD is a modal verb used to express advice, recommendations, opinions, and expectations. It’s softer than “must” and often used to help or guide someone.

It is followed by the base form of the verb and does not change with the subject.

📋 Structure with "Should"

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + should + base verb You should study more.
Negative Subject + should not (shouldn’t) + base verb He shouldn’t eat so much sugar.
Question Should + subject + base verb? Should we call her?

💡 Quick Tip

Use should to give advice or share your opinion. It’s polite and helpful.

🎯 Common Uses of "Should"

1. Advice
To help someone make a good decision

• You should sleep early.
• He should talk to a doctor.
• We should study for the test.
• They should be more careful.

2. Opinion
To express what you think is right

• I think you should try again.
• She should apologize.
• We should help them.
• They should listen to the teacher.

3. Expectation
To say what is likely or expected

• The train should arrive soon.
• He should be here by now.
• We should finish by 5 PM.
• It should be sunny tomorrow.

📝 More Examples

✅ Affirmative

• I should call my mom.
• You should eat more fruit.
• We should clean the room.
• They should arrive soon.

🚫 Negative

• I shouldn’t be late.
• He shouldn’t drive so fast.
• We shouldn’t skip class.
• They shouldn’t argue.

❓ Questions

• Should I bring my notebook?
• Should we wait here?
• Should she talk to him?
• What should they do?

📌 Related Expressions

Ought to
"You ought to study." (similar meaning)
Had better
"You’d better be careful." (stronger advice)
Could
"You could try again." (suggestion)

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using "Should" in Context

• You should drink more water.
• I should finish my homework.
• We shouldn’t forget the tickets.
• Should I talk to the teacher?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He should to go.
✅ He should go.

❌ You shoulds study.
✅ You should study.

❌ Should I to call her?
✅ Should I call her?

❌ We should going now.
✅ We should go now.
