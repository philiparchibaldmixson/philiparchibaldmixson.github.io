🧠 MODAL VERBS: COULD & BE ABLE TO 🧠

Talking About Ability in the Past, Present, and Future

🌟 What Do They Mean?

COULD and BE ABLE TO are used to express ability. They are similar to CAN, but used in different tenses and situations.

  • COULD: General ability in the past or polite requests.
  • BE ABLE TO: Ability in different tenses (past, present, future).

📋 Structure Comparison

Expression Structure Example
Could Subject + could + base verb I could swim when I was 5.
Be able to Subject + be (am/is/are/was/were/will be) + able to + base verb She is able to speak French.

💡 Quick Tip

Use could for general past ability. Use was/were able to for specific successful actions.

🎯 Common Uses

1. General Past Ability – "Could"
To describe what someone was able to do in the past

• I could read when I was 4.
• She could play the piano.
• We could run very fast.
• They could understand Spanish.

2. Specific Past Success – "Was/Were Able To"
To describe a specific achievement

• I was able to fix the computer.
• She was able to pass the test.
• We were able to arrive on time.
• They were able to win the game.

3. Present and Future Ability – "Be Able To"
To talk about ability now or in the future

• I am able to help you.
• She is able to speak Japanese.
• We will be able to travel next year.
• They are able to solve the problem.

4. Polite Requests – "Could"
To ask for something politely

• Could you help me?
• Could I borrow your pen?
• Could we leave early?
• Could she join us?

📝 More Examples

✅ COULD

• I could swim when I was young.
• He could read at age 3.
• Could you open the window?
• We couldn’t find the answer.

✅ BE ABLE TO

• I am able to help now.
• She was able to solve the puzzle.
• We will be able to finish tomorrow.
• They weren’t able to come.

📌 Related Expressions

Can
"I can swim." (present ability)
Could
"I could swim when I was 5." (past ability)
Be able to
"We will be able to help." (future ability)

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Both in Context

• I could play the guitar when I was younger.
• She was able to pass the test after studying hard.
• We are able to speak English fluently.
• They will be able to join us next week.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He could to swim.
✅ He could swim.

❌ She was able speak.
✅ She was able to speak.

❌ We will able to go.
✅ We will be able to go.

❌ Could you to help me?
✅ Could you help me?

Use "Could" for Past Ability and Polite Requests. Use "Be Able To" for All Tenses!

Practice with different subjects and time expressions to build fluency!
