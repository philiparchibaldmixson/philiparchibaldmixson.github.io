🛑 MODAL VERBS: PERMISSION – CAN, COULD, MAY 🛑

Asking for and Giving Permission in English

🌟 What Do They Mean?

CAN, COULD, and MAY are modal verbs used to talk about permission. They help us ask for permission, give permission, or talk about what is allowed.

  • CAN: Informal and common in everyday speech.
  • COULD: Polite and more formal than “can”.
  • MAY: Very formal and often used in official or academic contexts.

📋 Structure Comparison

Modal Structure Example
Can Can + subject + base verb? Can I go outside?
Could Could + subject + base verb? Could we leave early?
May May + subject + base verb? May I ask a question?

💡 Quick Tip

Use can with friends, could to be polite, and may in formal situations.

🎯 Asking for Permission

1. Using "Can"
Informal and friendly

• Can I borrow your pen?
• Can we go now?
• Can she use the computer?
• Can they come with us?

2. Using "Could"
Polite and respectful

• Could I leave a bit earlier?
• Could we sit here?
• Could you help me with this?
• Could they join the meeting?

3. Using "May"
Very formal and official

• May I speak now?
• May we enter the room?
• May she take the exam?
• May I use your phone?

📝 Giving Permission

✅ CAN

• Yes, you can go.
• You can use my laptop.
• She can stay here.
• They can play outside.

✅ COULD

• You could leave early today.
• He could take a break.
• We could use the room.
• They could join us.

✅ MAY

• You may begin the test.
• She may enter.
• Students may leave after the bell.
• You may use your notes.

📌 Related Expressions

Allow
"They allow phones in class."
Let
"She let me borrow her book."
Permission
"Do you have permission to leave?"

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using All Three in Context

• Can I open the window? (informal)
• Could I open the window? (polite)
• May I open the window? (formal)

• You can leave now.
• You could leave if you want.
• You may leave after the meeting ends.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ Can I to go?
✅ Can I go?

❌ Could you to help me?
✅ Could you help me?

❌ May I to speak?
✅ May I speak?

❌ She cans come.
✅ She can come.
