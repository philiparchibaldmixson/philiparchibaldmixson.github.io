🔄 PASSIVE VOICE – INTRODUCTION 🔄

Focus on the Action, Not the Doer

🌟 What Is the Passive Voice?

The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action or the receiver of the action, not on who performed it. It’s common in formal writing, news, and scientific reports.

It is formed with be + past participle.

📋 Structure of Passive Voice

Tense Active Passive
Present Simple They clean the room. The room is cleaned.
Past Simple She wrote the book. The book was written.
Future Simple He will deliver the package. The package will be delivered.
Present Perfect They have built a house. A house has been built.

💡 Quick Tip

Use passive voice when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from context.

🎯 When to Use the Passive Voice

1. Unknown Agent
We don’t know who did the action

• The window was broken.
• The wallet was found on the street.

2. Unimportant Agent
The doer doesn’t matter

• The streets are cleaned every day.
• The homework was corrected.

3. Obvious Agent
The doer is clear from context

• The thief was arrested.
• The cake was baked this morning.

📝 More Examples

✅ Present Simple

• English is spoken here.
• The documents are printed every morning.

✅ Past Simple

• The letter was sent yesterday.
• The car was washed last weekend.

✅ Future Simple

• The results will be announced soon.
• The project will be completed next month.

✅ Present Perfect

• The work has been finished.
• The emails have been answered.

📌 Passive vs Active

Active
"The chef cooked the meal."
Passive
"The meal was cooked by the chef."
Active
"They built the house."
Passive
"The house was built."

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Passive Voice in Context

• The report was written by Maria.
• The room is cleaned every morning.
• The documents will be sent • The homework has been corrected.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ The cake baked.
✅ The cake was baked.

❌ The letter is send.
✅ The letter is sent.

❌ The homework has corrected.
✅ The homework has been corrected.

❌ The room will cleaned.
✅ The room will be cleaned.
