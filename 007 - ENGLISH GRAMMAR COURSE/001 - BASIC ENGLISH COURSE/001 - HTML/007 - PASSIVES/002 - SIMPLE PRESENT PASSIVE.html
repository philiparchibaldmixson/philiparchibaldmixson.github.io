📘 SIMPLE PRESENT – PASSIVE VOICE 📘

Focus on the Action, Not the Doer

🌟 What Is the Passive Voice in Simple Present?

The Simple Present Passive is used when the action is more important than the person who does it. It’s common in instructions, news, and formal writing.

It is formed with am / is / are + past participle.

📋 Structure of Simple Present Passive

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + am/is/are + past participle The room is cleaned every day.
Negative Subject + am/is/are + not + past participle The windows aren’t opened in the morning.
Question Am/Is/Are + subject + past participle? Is the homework checked?

💡 Quick Tip

Use passive voice when the doer is unknown, unimportant, or obvious from context.

🎯 When to Use the Simple Present Passive

1. Instructions
To describe how things are done

• The form is filled out online.
• The documents are sent by email.

2. News and Reports
To report facts or events

• A new law is passed.
• The president is elected every four years.

3. General Truths
To describe things that are always true

• English is spoken in many countries.
• The Earth is surrounded by atmosphere.

📝 More Examples

✅ Affirmative

• The house is cleaned on Fridays.
• Emails are answered quickly.
• The food is served at noon.
• Homework is collected every Monday.

🚫 Negative

• The trash isn’t taken out at night.
• The reports aren’t printed on time.
• The lights aren’t turned off.
• The books aren’t returned to the shelf.

❓ Questions

• Is the room cleaned every day?
• Are the tests corrected by the teacher?
• Is lunch served at 1 PM?
• Are the windows closed at night?

📌 Active vs Passive

Active
"They clean the room."
Passive
"The room is cleaned."
Active
"She writes the reports."
Passive
"The reports are written."

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Simple Present Passive in Context

• The classroom is cleaned every morning.
• The packages are delivered on time.
• The homework is checked by the teacher.
• The lights are turned off at night.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ The room cleaned every day.
✅ The room is cleaned every day.

❌ The reports is written.
✅ The reports are written.

❌ Is cleaned the floor?
✅ Is the floor cleaned?

❌ They are clean the windows.
✅ The windows are cleaned.
