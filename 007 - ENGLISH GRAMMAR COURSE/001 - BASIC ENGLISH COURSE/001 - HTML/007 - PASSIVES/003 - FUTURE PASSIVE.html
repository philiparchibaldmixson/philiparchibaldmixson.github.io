🔮 FUTURE SIMPLE – PASSIVE VOICE 🔮

Talking About Future Actions Without Focusing on the Doer

🌟 What Is the Future Passive?

The Future Passive Voice is used to describe actions that will happen in the future, but the doer is unknown, unimportant, or obvious.

It is formed with will be + past participle.

📋 Structure of Future Passive

Form Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + will be + past participle The report will be sent tomorrow.
Negative Subject + will not be + past participle The package won’t be delivered today.
Question Will + subject + be + past participle? Will the room be cleaned?

💡 Quick Tip

Use the future passive to focus on what will happen, not who will do it.

🎯 When to Use the Future Passive

1. Formal Announcements
To describe future plans or events

• The results will be announced next week.
• A new policy will be introduced soon.

2. Instructions and Schedules
To explain what will be done

• The documents will be printed by noon.
• Lunch will be served at 1 PM.

3. Predictions
To describe what is expected to happen

• The project will be completed on time.
• The stadium will be built by 2026.

📝 More Examples

✅ Affirmative

• The homework will be corrected tomorrow.
• The invitations will be sent soon.
• The room will be decorated for the party.
• The files will be uploaded later.

🚫 Negative

• The meeting won’t be recorded.
• The documents won’t be signed today.
• The emails won’t be answered until Monday.
• The test won’t be given this week.

❓ Questions

• Will the homework be checked?
• Will the food be delivered on time?
• Will the room be cleaned before lunch?
• Will the results be published online?

📌 Active vs Passive

Active
"They will clean the room."
Passive
"The room will be cleaned."
Active
"She will send the email."
Passive
"The email will be sent."

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Future Passive in Context

• The classroom will be cleaned before the exam.
• The results will be posted • The packages will be delivered • The homework will be reviewed

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ The report will send tomorrow.
✅ The report will be sent tomorrow.

❌ The room will cleaned.
✅ The room will be cleaned.

❌ Will be the homework corrected?
✅ Will the homework be corrected?

❌ The emails will answered.
✅ The emails will be answered.
