❓ YES / NO QUESTIONS ❓

Ask Questions That Can Be Answered With “Yes” or “No”

🌟 What Are Yes/No Questions?

Yes/No Questions are questions that can be answered with a simple “Yes” or “No”. They usually begin with an auxiliary verb like do, does, did, is, are, was, were, will, can, have, has.

  • Do you like pizza? → Yes, I do.
  • Is she at home? → No, she isn’t.
  • Can they swim? → Yes, they can.

📋 Structure of Yes/No Questions

Tense Structure Example
Present Simple Do/Does + subject + base verb Do you study English?
Past Simple Did + subject + base verb Did she call you?
Present Continuous Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing Is he working?
Future Simple Will + subject + base verb Will they arrive soon?
Present Perfect Have/Has + subject + past participle Have you finished?

💡 Quick Tip

Start with the auxiliary verb, then add the subject and main verb. Don’t forget the question mark!

🎯 Common Auxiliary Verbs

1. DO / DOES
Present Simple

• Do you like music?
• Does she play guitar?
• Do they live here?

2. DID
Past Simple

• Did you go to the party?
• Did he call you?
• Did they arrive late?

3. IS / ARE / AM
Present Continuous

• Is she studying?
• Are you listening?
• Am I late?

4. WILL
Future Simple

• Will you come tomorrow?
• Will it rain?
• Will they help us?

5. CAN / COULD
Ability / Permission

• Can you swim?
• Could I borrow your pen?
• Can they speak French?

📌 Short Answers

Yes
Yes, I do. / Yes, she is. / Yes, they have.
No
No, I don’t. / No, he isn’t. / No, we haven’t.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ You like pizza?
✅ Do you like pizza?

❌ She is your sister?
✅ Is she your sister?

❌ You went to school?
✅ Did you go to school?

❌ You will help?
✅ Will you help?
