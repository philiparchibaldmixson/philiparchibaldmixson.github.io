❓ Question Words in English ❓

WH-Questions and How to Use Them

🌟 What are Question Words?

Question words, also called WH-words or interrogatives, are special words we use to ask for specific information. Unlike yes/no questions, these questions require a detailed answer that provides information about time, place, person, reason, manner, or other details.

Most question words in English begin with the letters "WH" (what, where, when, why, which, who, whom, whose), plus "how" which is also a question word. Mastering these words is essential for having conversations, gathering information, and expressing curiosity in English.

📚 WHAT - Asking About Things

WHAT
Meaning: Qué / Cuál
Use: To ask about things, actions, information, or to request descriptions
📝 Examples:
What is your name? → My name is Sarah.
What are you doing? → I'm reading a book.
What do you want for dinner? → I want pizza.
What time is it? → It's 3 o'clock.
What color is your car? → It's blue.
What happened yesterday? → I had a meeting with my boss.

💡 Common Combinations with WHAT

What time: What time does the movie start?
What kind/type: What kind of music do you like?
What color: What color are your eyes?
What day: What day is today?
What about: What about going to the park?

📍 WHERE - Asking About Places

WHERE
Meaning: Dónde
Use: To ask about location, position, or place
📝 Examples:
Where do you live? → I live in New York.
Where is the bathroom? → It's on the second floor.
Where are you from? → I'm from Brazil.
Where did you go last weekend? → I went to the beach.
Where can I buy tickets? → You can buy them online.
Where will you be tomorrow? → I'll be at the office.

💡 Common Phrases with WHERE

Where are you? (asking current location)
Where from: Where are you from?
Where to: Where are you going?
Where else: Where else did you look?

⏰ WHEN - Asking About Time

WHEN
Meaning: Cuándo
Use: To ask about time, dates, or occasions
📝 Examples:
When is your birthday? → It's on June 15th.
When did you arrive? → I arrived yesterday.
When will the meeting start? → It will start at 2 PM.
When do you usually wake up? → I wake up at 7 AM.
When was the last time you saw her? → Last week.
When can you come over? → I can come over tonight.

💡 Time Answers for WHEN

Common answers include: today, tomorrow, yesterday, next week, last month, in the morning, at night, on Monday, in 2025, soon, later, never, always

❓ WHY - Asking About Reasons

WHY
Meaning: Por qué
Use: To ask about reasons, causes, or purposes
📝 Examples:
Why are you late? → Because I missed the bus.
Why did you choose this job? → Because I love working with people.
Why is the sky blue? → Because of the way light scatters.
Why don't you like coffee? → Because it's too bitter for me.
Why should I believe you? → Because I've never lied to you.
Why can't we go to the park? → Because it's raining.

💡 Answering WHY Questions

Use "because" to answer why questions:
Why are you happy? → Because I got a promotion.
You can also use: "to + verb", "for + noun", "due to", "since", "as"

🎯 HOW - Asking About Manner

HOW
Meaning: Cómo
Use: To ask about manner, way, condition, or degree
📝 Examples:
How are you? → I'm fine, thank you.
How do you make coffee? → First, you heat the water...
How did you learn English? → I took classes and practiced daily.
How can I help you? → I need directions to the station.
How was your day? → It was great!
How does this machine work? → You press this button to start.
HOW + Adjective/Adverb Combinations:

How old: How old are you? → I'm 25 years old.
How tall: How tall is he? → He's 6 feet tall.
How long: How long does it take? → About 30 minutes.
How far: How far is the airport? → It's 20 miles away.
How much: How much does it cost? → It costs $50.
How many: How many students are there? → There are 30.
How often: How often do you exercise? → Three times a week.
How fast: How fast can you run? → Pretty fast!

👤 WHO - Asking About People

WHO
Meaning: Quién
Use: To ask about people's identity (subject or object)
📝 Examples:
Who is that person? → That's my teacher.
Who called you? → My mom called.
Who did you meet yesterday? → I met an old friend.
Who wants pizza? → I do!
Who are you waiting for? → I'm waiting for my sister.
Who won the game? → Our team won!
WHO vs. WHOM:

WHO: Used as the subject (who does the action)
→ Who loves chocolate? (who is the subject)

WHOM: Used as the object (formal, less common)
→ Whom did you invite? (whom receives the action)
→ In modern English, "who" is commonly used for both

🔍 WHICH - Asking for Choice

WHICH
Meaning: Cuál / Qué
Use: To ask about a specific choice from a limited number of options
📝 Examples:
Which color do you prefer, red or blue? → I prefer blue.
Which book is yours? → The one on the left.
Which way should we go? → Let's go this way.
Which restaurant do you recommend? → I recommend the Italian one.
Which is better, coffee or tea? → It depends on your taste.
WHICH vs. WHAT:

WHICH: Limited, specific choices
→ Which dress should I wear? (choosing from a few options)

WHAT: Unlimited, general options
→ What dress are you wearing? (could be anything)

🏠 WHOSE - Asking About Possession

WHOSE
Meaning: De quién
Use: To ask about ownership or possession
📝 Examples:
Whose phone is this? → It's mine / It's John's.
Whose car are we taking? → We're taking Sarah's car.
Whose turn is it? → It's my turn.
Whose idea was this? → It was Tom's idea.
Whose keys did you find? → I found my neighbor's keys.

💡 Don't Confuse!

WHOSE = possession (whose book?)
WHO'S = who is / who has (who's there? = who is there?)

💬 Question Structure and Word Order

Question Word + Auxiliary/Be + Subject + Main Verb + ...?
📝 Structure Examples:

What + do + you + want? → What do you want?
Where + is + she + going? → Where is she going?
When + did + they + arrive? → When did they arrive?
Why + can't + we + leave? → Why can't we leave?
How + does + it + work? → How does it work?
Who + called + you? → Who called you? (who is subject)
Which + book + do + you + like? → Which book do you like?

💡 Special Case: WHO as Subject

When WHO is the subject, we don't use auxiliary verbs:
✅ Who likes pizza? (not "Who does like pizza?")
✅ Who broke the window?
✅ Who wants ice cream?

🗣️ Real Conversations

Conversation 1: Meeting Someone New

Person A: Hi! What's your name?
Person B: My name is Maria. What about you?
Person A: I'm John. Where are you from?
Person B: I'm from Spain. And you?
Person A: I'm from Canada. How long have you been here?
Person B: About three months. Why did you come here?
Person A: For work. What do you do?
Person B: I'm a teacher. How about you?

Conversation 2: Making Plans

Sarah: What are you doing this weekend?
Tom: Nothing special. Why?
Sarah: Would you like to go to the movies?
Tom: Sure! When do you want to go?
Sarah: How about Saturday evening?
Tom: Perfect
