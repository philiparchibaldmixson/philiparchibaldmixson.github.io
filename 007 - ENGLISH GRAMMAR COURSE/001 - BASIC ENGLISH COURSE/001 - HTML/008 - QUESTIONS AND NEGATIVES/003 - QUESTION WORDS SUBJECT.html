🧠 QUESTION WORDS: SUBJECT 🧠

When the Question Word Is the Subject

🌟 What Are Subject Questions?

In subject questions, the question word is the subject of the sentence. These questions do not use auxiliary verbs like do/does/did. The word order is the same as in affirmative sentences.

Examples:

  • Who called you?
  • What happened?
  • Which student got the best grade?

📋 Structure of Subject Questions

Question Word Verb Object (optional) Example
Who called you Who called you?
What happened — What happened?
Which student won the prize Which student won the prize?

💡 Quick Tip

If the question word is the subject, don’t use do/does/did. Just use the verb directly.

🎯 Common Subject Question Words

1. WHO
Used for people

• Who made this cake?
• Who knows the answer?
• Who called you last night?

2. WHAT
Used for things or actions

• What caused the problem?
• What made you laugh?
• What happened?

3. WHICH
Used to choose between options

• Which student got the highest score?
• Which book won the award?
• Which team won the match?

📝 More Examples

✅ Present Simple

• Who teaches this class?
• What makes you happy?
• Which singer sings this song?

✅ Past Simple

• Who broke the window?
• What caused the fire?
• Which student answered first?

✅ Future

• Who will lead the team?
• What will happen next?
• Which player will win?

📌 Subject vs Object Questions

Subject Question
Who invited you?
Object Question
Who did you invite?
Subject Question
What made you laugh?
Object Question
What did you make?

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Subject Questions in Context

• Who opened the door?
• What started the argument?
• Which student asked the question?
• Who fixed the computer?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ Who did call you?
✅ Who called you?

❌ What did happen?
✅ What happened?

❌ Which student did win?
✅ Which student won?

Use Subject Questions to Ask Who or What Did Something!

Remember: No auxiliary verbs needed — just the question word + verb.
