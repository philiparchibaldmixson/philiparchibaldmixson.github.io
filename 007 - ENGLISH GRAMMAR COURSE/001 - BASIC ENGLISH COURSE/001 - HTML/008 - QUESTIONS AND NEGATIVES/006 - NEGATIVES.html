🚫 NEGATIVES IN ENGLISH 🚫

How to Say That Something Is Not True

🌟 What Are Negative Sentences?

Negative sentences are used to say that something is not true, did not happen, or is not happening. In English, we usually use not with auxiliary verbs like do, does, did, is, are, was, were, have, has, will, can.

Examples:

  • I don’t like coffee.
  • She isn’t at home.
  • They haven’t finished the project.

📋 Structure of Negative Sentences

Tense Structure Example
Present Simple Subject + do/does + not + base verb He doesn’t play soccer.
Past Simple Subject + did + not + base verb We didn’t go to school.
Present Continuous Subject + is/are/am + not + verb-ing I’m not studying now.
Present Perfect Subject + have/has + not + past participle They haven’t eaten yet.
Future Simple Subject + will + not + base verb She won’t come tomorrow.

💡 Quick Tip

Use contractions like don’t, doesn’t, didn’t, isn’t, aren’t, won’t in spoken and informal English.

🎯 Common Negative Forms

1. DO / DOES + NOT
Present Simple

• I don’t like pizza.
• He doesn’t speak French.
• They don’t live here.

2. DID + NOT
Past Simple

• I didn’t see the movie.
• She didn’t call me.
• We didn’t go out.

3. IS / ARE / AM + NOT
Present Continuous

• He isn’t working.
• They aren’t listening.
• I’m not sleeping.

4. HAVE / HAS + NOT
Present Perfect

• I haven’t finished yet.
• She hasn’t arrived.
• We haven’t eaten.

5. WILL + NOT
Future Simple

• I won’t go.
• He won’t help.
• They won’t come.

📌 Negative Contractions

do not → don’t
I don’t know.
does not → doesn’t
She doesn’t like it.
did not → didn’t
We didn’t go.
will not → won’t
He won’t come.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Negatives in Context

• I don’t understand this exercise.
• She isn’t feeling well today.
• We didn’t finish the homework.
• They won’t be here tomorrow.
• He hasn’t called me yet.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ He don’t like it.
✅ He doesn’t like it.

❌ I didn’t went.
✅ I didn’t go.

❌ She not is here.
✅ She isn’t here.

❌ We haven’t ate.
✅ We haven’t eaten.
