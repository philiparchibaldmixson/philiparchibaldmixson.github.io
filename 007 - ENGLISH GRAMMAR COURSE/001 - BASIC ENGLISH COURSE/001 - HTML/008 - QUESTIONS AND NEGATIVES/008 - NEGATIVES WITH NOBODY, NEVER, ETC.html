🚫 NEGATIVE WORDS: NOBODY, NEVER, NOTHING, NOWHERE 🚫

How to Use Negative Words Correctly in English

🌟 What Are Negative Words?

Negative words like nobody, never, nothing, and nowhere are used to express the idea of no one, no time, no thing, or no place. They already carry a negative meaning, so we don’t use “not” with them.

Examples:

  • Nobody came to the meeting.
  • I have never been to Paris.
  • There is nothing in the fridge.
  • She went nowhere last weekend.

📋 Common Negative Words

Word Meaning Example
nobody no person Nobody answered the phone.
nothing no thing There’s nothing to do here.
never at no time I never eat fast food.
nowhere no place We went nowhere special.
no one no person (same as nobody) No one knew the answer.

💡 Quick Tip

Don’t use “not” with these words. Say “Nobody came,” not “Not nobody came.”

🎯 Examples by Category

1. NOBODY / NO ONE
Used for people

• Nobody was at home.
• No one called me.
• Nobody wants to leave.

2. NOTHING
Used for things

• I saw nothing.
• There’s nothing in the bag.
• Nothing happened.

3. NEVER
Used for time

• I never smoke.
• She has never been to London.
• We never agree on anything.

4. NOWHERE
Used for places

• He went nowhere.
• There’s nowhere to sit.
• I have nowhere to go.

📌 Negative Words vs NOT + ANY

Negative Word
Nobody came.
NOT + ANY
Not anybody came.
Negative Word
I saw nothing.
NOT + ANY
I didn’t see anything.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Negative Words in Context

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I don’t want nothing.
✅ I don’t want anything. / I want nothing.

❌ She didn’t say nobody.
✅ She didn’t say anybody. / She said nobody.

❌ There isn’t nothing here.
✅ There isn’t anything here. / There’s nothing here.

Use negative words like “nobody” and “never” carefully — they already mean “not.”

Don’t use “not” with them. Practice with examples to sound natural and correct!
