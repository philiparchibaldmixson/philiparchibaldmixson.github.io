📘 INFINITIVES WITH “TO” 📘

How to Use “to” + Base Verb in English

🌟 What Is an Infinitive?

An infinitive is the base form of a verb, usually preceded by “to”. It can act like a noun, adjective, or adverb in a sentence.

Examples:

  • I want to learn English.
  • She needs to study.
  • It’s important to practice.

📋 Structure of Infinitives

Use Structure Example
After certain verbs Verb + to + base verb We hope to travel soon.
To express purpose To + base verb I exercise to stay healthy.
As subject To + base verb + verb To learn is exciting.
After adjectives Adjective + to + base verb It’s easy to understand.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “to” + verb after verbs like want, need, hope, plan, decide, learn, and promise.

🎯 Common Verbs Followed by “to”

1. WANT
Desire or intention

• I want to sleep.
• She wants to visit Bahia.
• They want to learn English.

2. NEED
Requirement

• You need to study.
• We need to leave now.
• He needs to rest.

3. PLAN / HOPE / DECIDE
Future intentions

• I plan to travel next year.
• They hope to pass the test.
• She decided to stay.

4. LEARN / TRY / PROMISE
Effort or commitment

• He’s learning to cook.
• I’ll try to help.
• She promised to call.

📝 More Examples

✅ Purpose

• I study to improve my skills.
• She runs to stay fit.
• We work to earn

✅ After Adjectives

• It’s hard to explain.
• He’s happy to help.
• It’s important to listen.

✅ As Subject

• To travel is exciting.
• To speak English well takes practice.
• To be kind is powerful.

📌 Infinitive vs Gerund

Infinitive
I want to learn.
Gerund
I enjoy learning.
Infinitive
She needs to study.
Gerund
She likes studying.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

Using Infinitives in Context

• I want to speak English fluently.
• He promised to help with the homework.
• We need to leave early.
• It’s nice to meet new people.
• To learn is a lifelong journey.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I want learning.
✅ I want to learn.

❌ She needs go.
✅ She needs to go.

❌ We hope pass.
✅ We hope to pass.

❌ He decided stay.
✅ He decided to stay.

Use “to” + base verb to express actions, intentions, and purposes!
