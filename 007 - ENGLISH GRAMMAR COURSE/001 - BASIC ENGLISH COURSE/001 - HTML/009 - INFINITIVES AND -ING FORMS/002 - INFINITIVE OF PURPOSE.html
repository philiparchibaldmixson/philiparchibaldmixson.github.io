🎯 INFINITIVE OF PURPOSE 🎯

Using “to” + Verb to Show Purpose

🌟 What Is an Infinitive of Purpose?

An infinitive of purpose is a phrase that uses “to” + base verb to explain why someone does something — the goal or reason behind the action.

Examples:

  • I study to learn English.
  • She runs to stay healthy.
  • We practice to improve our skills.

📋 Structure of Infinitive of Purpose

Action Purpose Full Sentence
study to learn I study to learn English.
exercise to stay fit She exercises to stay fit.
save money to travel We save money to travel abroad.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “to” + verb after an action to explain the reason or goal. It’s short and natural!

🎯 Common Verbs with Purpose

1. STUDY / READ / PRACTICE
Learning goals

• I study to pass the test.
• She reads to improve her vocabulary.
• We practice to get better.

2. WORK / SAVE / PLAN
Future goals

• He works to earn • They save to buy • I plan to travel

3. EXERCISE / EAT / SLEEP
Health goals

• She exercises to stay • He eats well to feel • I sleep early to wake up

4. HELP / TEACH / GIVE
Helping others

• I teach to help • She gives advice to support • We volunteer to make

📝 More Examples

✅ Purpose Sentences

📌 Infinitive vs “for” + noun

Infinitive
I study to learn
For + noun
I study for the test.
Infinitive
She runs to stay
For + noun
She runs for her health.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Wrong → ✅ Correct

❌ I study for learn.
✅ I study to learn.

❌ She runs for stay healthy.
✅ She runs to stay healthy.

❌ We practice for improve.
✅ We practice to improve.

❌ He works for earn money.
✅ He works to earn money.

Use “to” + verb to show purpose clearly and naturally!
