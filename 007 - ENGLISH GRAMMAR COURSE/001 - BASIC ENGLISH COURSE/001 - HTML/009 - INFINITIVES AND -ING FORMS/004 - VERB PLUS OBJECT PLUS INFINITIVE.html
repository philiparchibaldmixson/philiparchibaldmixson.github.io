🗣️ VERB + OBJECT + INFINITIVE 🗣️

Using “to” + Verb After an Object

🌟 What Is Verb + Object + Infinitive?

Some verbs are followed by an object and then an infinitive (to + base verb). This structure is used to tell, ask, advise, or allow someone to do something.

  • She told me to study.
  • They asked us to help.
  • I want you to listen.

📋 Structure of Verb + Object + Infinitive

Verb Object Infinitive Full Sentence
tell me to study She told me to study.
ask us to help They asked us to help.
want you to listen I want you to listen.
advise him to rest The doctor advised him to rest.

💡 Quick Tip

Use this structure when one person causes or requests another person to do something.

🎯 Common Verbs That Use This Structure

  • tell / ask / order → She told me to wait.
  • want / expect / need → I want you to come.
  • advise / encourage / remind → He reminded me to call.
  • allow / permit / help → They allowed us to enter.

📝 More Examples

📌 Verb + Infinitive vs Verb + Object + Infinitive

Form Example
Verb + Infinitive I want to go.
Verb + Object + Infinitive I want you to go.
Verb + Infinitive She decided to stay.
Verb + Object + Infinitive She told me to stay.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

• My mom told me to clean my room.
• The teacher asked us to be quiet.
• I want you to understand this.
• He encouraged her to apply for the job.
• They allowed us to leave early.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I want that you go.
✅ I want you to go.

❌ She told to me study.
✅ She told me to study.

❌ They asked to us help.
✅ They asked us to help.

❌ He advised to me rest.
✅ He advised me to rest.

Use Verb + Object + Infinitive to express instructions, requests, and intentions clearly!
