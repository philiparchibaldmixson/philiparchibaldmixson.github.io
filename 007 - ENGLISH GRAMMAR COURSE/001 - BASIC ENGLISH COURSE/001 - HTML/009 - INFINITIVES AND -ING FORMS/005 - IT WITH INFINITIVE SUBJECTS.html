Using “It” + Infinitive to Start Sentences

🌟 What Is “It” + Infinitive?

We often use “it” as a dummy subject followed by an infinitive (to + verb) to express opinions, importance, or necessity. This structure is common in English and sounds natural.

Examples:

  • It’s important to study.
  • It’s hard to explain.
  • It’s nice to meet you.

📋 Structure of “It” + Infinitive

It Be Verb Adjective Infinitive Full Sentence
It is important to study It is important to study.
It was difficult to hear It was difficult to hear.
It is easy to understand It is easy to understand.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “It” + be + adjective + to + verb to express opinions or describe actions.

🎯 Common Adjectives Used with “It” + Infinitive

1. IMPORTANT / NECESSARY / ESSENTIAL
Expressing importance

• It’s important to study.
• It’s necessary to sleep well.
• It’s essential to drink water.

2. EASY / HARD / DIFFICULT
Describing difficulty

• It’s easy to learn.
• It’s hard to explain.
• It’s difficult to hear.

3. NICE / GOOD / FUN
Describing feelings

• It’s nice to meet you.
• It’s good to relax.
• It’s fun to play soccer.

4. POSSIBLE / IMPOSSIBLE / SAFE
Describing possibility or safety

• It’s possible to improve.
• It’s impossible to finish now.
• It’s safe to cross here.

📝 More Examples

✅ Present

• It’s important to study every day.
• It’s easy to understand this topic.
• It’s fun to learn English.

✅ Past

• It was hard to hear the speaker.
• It was nice to meet you.
• It was difficult to find the place.

✅ Future

• It will be important to arrive early.
• It will be easy to solve this.
• It will be fun to travel together.

📌 Compare with Normal Infinitive Subjects

Form Example
Infinitive Subject To study is important.
It + Infinitive It is important to study.
Infinitive Subject To sleep early is healthy.
It + Infinitive It is healthy to sleep early.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

• It’s important to speak clearly.
• It’s difficult to understand fast English.
• It’s nice to help others.
• It’s possible to learn online.
• It’s fun to study with friends.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Is important to study.
✅ It is important to study.

❌ Is easy to learn.
✅ It is easy to learn.

❌ Was nice meet you.
✅ It was nice to meet you.

❌ Will be fun travel.
✅ It will be fun to travel.

Use “It” + infinitive to express opinions, feelings, and importance in a natural way!
