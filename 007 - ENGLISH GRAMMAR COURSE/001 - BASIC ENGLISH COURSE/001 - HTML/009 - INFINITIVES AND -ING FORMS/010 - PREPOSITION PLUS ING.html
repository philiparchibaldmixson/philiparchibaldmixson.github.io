🔗 PREPOSITION + -ING FORM 🔗

Using Verbs in -ING Form After Prepositions

🌟 What Is This Structure?

When a verb comes after a preposition, we usually use the -ING form (also called a gerund). This structure is very common in English and sounds natural.

  • I’m good at speaking English.
  • She’s interested in learning Spanish.
  • We talked about going to the beach.

📋 Structure

Preposition -ING Form Full Sentence
at speaking I’m good at speaking English.
in learning She’s interested in learning Spanish.
about going We talked about going to the beach.
for helping Thanks for helping me.
without asking He left without asking.

💡 Quick Tip

After a preposition, always use a verb in the -ING form. Never use the base form or infinitive!

🎯 Common Prepositions Followed by -ING

  • about → We talked about going out.
  • in → She’s interested in traveling.
  • at → He’s great at drawing.
  • for → Thank you for coming.
  • without → He left without saying goodbye.
  • before → Wash your hands before eating.
  • after → We rested after walking all day.

📝 More Examples

✅ Present

• I’m afraid of flying.
• She’s good at cooking.
• They’re excited about meeting new people.

✅ Past

• He apologized for being late.
• We talked about moving to another city.
• She succeeded in passing the test.

✅ Future

• I look forward to seeing you.
• They plan on traveling next month.
• We’ll think about changing the schedule.

📌 Compare with Other Structures

Form Example
Preposition + -ING She’s interested in learning.
Verb + Infinitive She wants to learn.
Adjective + Infinitive It’s easy to learn.
-ING as Subject Learning is important.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I’m tired of waiting.
  • She’s afraid of speaking in public.
  • We’re thinking about moving.
  • He succeeded in fixing the car.
  • They’re responsible for organizing the event.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I’m good at speak.
✅ I’m good at speaking.

❌ She’s interested in learn.
✅ She’s interested in learning.

❌ We talked about go.
✅ We talked about going.

❌ He left without ask.
✅ He left without asking.

Use preposition + -ING to sound natural and fluent in English!
