🎬 VERB + -ING FORM 🎬

Using Verbs Followed by -ING

🌟 What Is This Structure?

Some verbs in English are followed by another verb in the -ING form (called a gerund). This structure is used to talk about activities, habits, preferences, and actions.

  • I enjoy reading.
  • She stopped talking.
  • We finished studying.

📋 Structure

Main Verb -ING Verb Full Sentence
enjoy reading I enjoy reading books.
avoid driving He avoids driving at night.
finish studying We finished studying early.
suggest going She suggested going to the park.

💡 Quick Tip

Use -ING after verbs like enjoy, avoid, finish, suggest, keep, stop, admit, deny, and mind.

🎯 Common Verbs Followed by -ING

  • enjoy → I enjoy listening to music.
  • avoid → She avoids eating sugar.
  • finish → We finished cleaning the house.
  • suggest → He suggested watching a movie.
  • keep → They keep talking during class.
  • stop → I stopped smoking.
  • admit → She admitted cheating on the test.
  • deny → He denied breaking the window.
  • mind → Do you mind waiting a moment?

📝 More Examples

✅ Present

• I enjoy learning English.
• She avoids driving in traffic.
• We keep practicing every day.

✅ Past

• He admitted lying.
• They finished working late.
• I stopped using social media.

✅ Future

• I will suggest trying a new method.
• She might avoid traveling next week.
• We plan to keep studying.

📌 Compare with Verb + Infinitive

Form Example
Verb + -ING I enjoy reading.
Verb + Infinitive I want to read.
Verb + -ING She suggested going.
Verb + Infinitive She decided to go.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I enjoy listening to podcasts.
  • She keeps forgetting her keys.
  • We finished painting the room.
  • He admitted stealing the money.
  • They suggested meeting earlier.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I enjoy to read.
✅ I enjoy reading.

❌ She suggested to go.
✅ She suggested going.

❌ We finished to study.
✅ We finished studying.

❌ He admitted to cheat.
✅ He admitted cheating.

Use verb + -ING to talk about actions, habits, and preferences!
