⚡ STRUCTURES WITH “GET” ⚡

Using “get” in Different English Expressions

🌟 What Does “Get” Mean?

The verb get is very flexible in English. It can mean receive, become, arrive, understand, or be used in passive and causative structures. It’s used in many everyday expressions.

  • I got a gift. (receive)
  • She got tired. (become)
  • We got home late. (arrive)
  • Did you get it? (understand)

📋 Common Structures with “Get”

Structure Meaning Example
get + adjective become He got sick last week.
get + noun receive / obtain I got a message from her.
get + place arrive They got to school at 8.
get + past participle passive voice The window got broken.
get + object + past participle causative I got my car fixed.

💡 Quick Tip

“Get” is informal and common in spoken English. It replaces “become”, “receive”, or “have something done”.

🎯 Examples by Category

1. GET + ADJECTIVE
To show change or condition

• I got tired after work.
• She got angry at the news.
• We got lost in the city.

2. GET + NOUN
To receive or obtain

• He got a promotion.
• I got a call from my friend.
• They got a new job.

3. GET + PLACE
To arrive somewhere

• We got home late.
• She got to the airport early.
• What time did you get there?

4. GET + PAST PARTICIPLE
Passive voice

• The cake got burned.
• My phone got stolen.
• The door got locked.

5. GET + OBJECT + PAST PARTICIPLE
Causative structure

• I got my hair cut.
• He got his car washed.
• We got the house painted.

📝 More Examples

✅ Present

• I get tired after lunch.

✅ Past

✅ Future

📌 Compare with “Be” Structures

Structure Example
be + adjective She is tired.
get + adjective She got tired.
be + past participle The phone was stolen.
get + past participle The phone got stolen.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I got a message from João.
  • She got married last year.
  • We got stuck in traffic.
  • He got his bike repaired.
  • The kids got excited about the trip.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I get to tired.
✅ I get tired.

❌ She got marry.
✅ She got married.

❌ We got to home.
✅ We got home.

❌ He got his car fix.
✅ He got his car fixed.

Use “get” to express change, arrival, reception, passive actions, and causative ideas!
