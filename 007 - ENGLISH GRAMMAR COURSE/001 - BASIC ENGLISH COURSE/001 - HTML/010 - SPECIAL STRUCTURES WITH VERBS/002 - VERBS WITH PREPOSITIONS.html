🔗 VERBS WITH PREPOSITIONS 🔗

Common Verb + Preposition Combinations in English

🌟 What Are Verbs with Prepositions?

Some verbs in English are followed by specific prepositions. These combinations are called prepositional verbs. The preposition is part of the verb’s meaning and is followed by a noun, pronoun, or -ING form.

  • She looks at the painting.
  • I listen to music every day.
  • We depend on our team.

📋 Structure

Verb Preposition Object Full Sentence
look at the sky She looked at the sky.
listen to music I listen to music.
depend on you We depend on you.
talk about the problem He talked about the problem.

💡 Quick Tip

Don’t forget the preposition! It’s part of the verb’s meaning and must be used correctly.

🎯 Common Verb + Preposition Combinations

  • look at → She looked at me.
  • listen to → I listen to podcasts.
  • talk about → We talked about the movie.
  • depend on → They depend on support.
  • belong to → This book belongs to Ana.
  • agree with → I agree with you.
  • apologize for → He apologized for being late.
  • wait for → We waited for the bus.
  • worry about → Don’t worry about it.

📌 Compare with Verbs Without Prepositions

With Preposition Without Preposition
I listen to music. I hear music.
She looks at the photo. She sees the photo.
We talk about the issue. We discuss the issue.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I listen to English songs to improve my vocabulary.
  • She talked about her experience abroad.
  • We waited for the results all day.
  • He apologized for forgetting the meeting.
  • They depend on technology to work remotely.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I listen music.
✅ I listen to music.

❌ She looked the photo.
✅ She looked at the photo.

❌ We talked the problem.
✅ We talked about the problem.

❌ He apologized the delay.
✅ He apologized for the delay.
