🛠️ HAVE SOMETHING DONE 🛠️

Using “have” to show someone does something for you

🌟 What Is “Have Something Done”?

We use have + object + past participle to say that someone arranges for another person to do something. It’s called the causative form.

  • I had my car repaired. (someone repaired it for me)
  • She had her hair cut. (a hairdresser cut it)
  • We had the house painted. (painters did it)

📋 Structure

Subject Have Object Past Participle Full Sentence
I had my car repaired I had my car repaired.
She has her nails done She has her nails done every week.
We had the house cleaned We had the house cleaned.
They will have the documents printed They will have the documents printed.

💡 Quick Tip

Use this structure when you don’t do the action yourself, but arrange for someone else to do it.

🎯 Common Uses of “Have Something Done”

  • Haircuts → I had my hair cut.
  • Repairs → She had her phone fixed.
  • Cleaning → We had the room cleaned.
  • Documents → He had the report printed.
  • Medical → She had her eyes checked.
  • Photos → They had their pictures taken.

📝 More Examples

✅ Present

• I have my shirts ironed every week.
• She has her nails done at the salon.
• We have the office cleaned daily.

✅ Past

• He had his car washed yesterday.
• I had my laptop repaired last month.
• They had the windows replaced.

✅ Future

• I will have my passport renewed soon.
• She will have her hair styled for the party.
• We’ll have the documents signed tomorrow.

📌 Compare with Active Voice

Active Causative
The mechanic repaired my car. I had my car repaired.
The cleaner cleaned the house. We had the house cleaned.
The stylist cut her hair. She had her hair cut.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I had my phone repaired at the store.
  • She has her eyebrows shaped professionally.
  • We had the garden cleaned before the party.
  • He had his suit dry-cleaned.
  • They will have their wedding photos taken next week.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I had cut my hair.
✅ I had my hair cut.

❌ She had do her nails.
✅ She had her nails done.

❌ We had clean the house.
✅ We had the house cleaned.

❌ He had fix his car.
✅ He had his car fixed.

Use “have something done” to talk about services or actions done by others for you!
