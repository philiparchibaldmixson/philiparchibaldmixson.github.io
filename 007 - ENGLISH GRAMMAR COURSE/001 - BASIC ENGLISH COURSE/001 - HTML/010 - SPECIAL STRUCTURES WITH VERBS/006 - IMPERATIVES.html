🗣️ IMPERATIVES 🗣️

Giving Commands, Instructions, and Advice

🌟 What Are Imperatives?

An imperative is a verb form used to give orders, instructions, advice, or requests. It usually starts with the base form of the verb and doesn’t use a subject.

  • Close the door.
  • Be quiet.
  • Don’t touch that!

📋 Structure

Type Structure Example
Positive Verb (base form) Open the window.
Negative Don’t + verb Don’t forget your keys.
With “please” Please + verb Please wait here.
Be + adjective Be + adjective Be careful with that.

💡 Quick Tip

Imperatives are direct. Use “please” to sound more polite. Use “don’t” to make it negative.

🎯 Common Imperatives

  • Stop talking.
  • Listen carefully.
  • Turn off the lights.
  • Don’t forget your homework.
  • Be quiet.
  • Please sit down.
  • Don’t be late.
  • Take a deep breath.
  • Follow the instructions.

📝 More Examples

✅ Positive

• Close the window.
• Take a break.
• Read the instructions.

✅ Negative

• Don’t touch that.
• Don’t be rude.
• Don’t forget your ID.

✅ Polite

• Please wait here.
• Please help me.
• Please be patient.

📌 Imperatives vs Regular Sentences

Imperative Regular Sentence
Turn off the TV. You should turn off the TV.
Don’t be late. You must not be late.
Be careful. You need to be careful.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • Watch your step!
  • Don’t forget to call me.
  • Be kind to others.
  • Take your time.
  • Please turn off your phone.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ You close the door.
✅ Close the door.

❌ Don’t to be late.
✅ Don’t be late.

❌ Please you help me.
✅ Please help me.

❌ Be to careful.
✅ Be careful.

Use imperatives to give clear instructions, advice, or commands!

Practice with positive, negative, and polite forms to sound natural and confident!
