📘 ARTICLES: A & AN 📘

Using Indefinite Articles in English

🌟 What Are “A” and “An”?

A and an are indefinite articles. We use them to talk about something general or not specific. They come before singular countable nouns.

  • A → before words that begin with a consonant sound
  • An → before words that begin with a vowel sound

📋 Structure

Article Used Before Example
A Consonant sound A dog, a book, a car
An Vowel sound An apple, an hour, an idea

💡 Quick Tip

Use “a” or “an” depending on the sound, not the spelling. Example: an hour (silent “h”).

🎯 Common Examples

✅ A

  • A cat
  • A university (starts with “you” sound)
  • A house
  • A teacher
  • A banana

✅ An

  • An elephant
  • An umbrella
  • An honest person (silent “h”)
  • An hour
  • An idea

📝 More Sentences

✅ Using “A”

• I saw a bird in the tree.
• She bought a new phone.
• He needs a pen to write.

✅ Using “An”

• I ate an orange for breakfast.
• She is an artist.
• He waited for an hour.

📌 Compare: A vs An

Form Example Why?
A A university “University” starts with a “you” sound (consonant)
An An hour “Hour” starts with a vowel sound (silent “h”)
A A banana “Banana” starts with a consonant sound
An An elephant “Elephant” starts with a vowel sound

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I saw a dog chasing an old man.
  • She wants to buy a car and an apartment.
  • He gave me a book and an envelope.
  • We need a plan and an idea.
  • They found a solution and an opportunity.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I saw an dog.
✅ I saw a dog.

❌ She is a artist.
✅ She is an artist.

❌ He waited for a hour.
✅ He waited for an hour.

❌ I need an pen.
✅ I need a pen.

Use “a” before consonant sounds and “an” before vowel sounds!

Listen carefully to the first sound of the word, and practice with real examples to build fluency!
