🧍 A & AN: DESCRIBING PEOPLE 🧍

Using Indefinite Articles to Talk About People

🌟 What Are “A” and “An”?

A and an are indefinite articles used before singular countable nouns. When describing people, we use them before professions, roles, personality traits, and more.

  • A → before words that begin with a consonant sound
  • An → before words that begin with a vowel sound

📋 Structure

Article Used Before Example
A Consonant sound A doctor, a teacher, a kind person
An Vowel sound An actor, an engineer, an honest woman

💡 Quick Tip

Use “a” or “an” depending on the sound, not the spelling. Example: an honest man (silent “h”).

🎯 Common Descriptions

✅ Using “A”

  • A teacher
  • A friendly man
  • A police officer
  • A smart student
  • A young boy

✅ Using “An”

  • An artist
  • An intelligent woman
  • An honest person
  • An old man
  • An engineer

📝 More Sentences

✅ Using “A”

• She is a nurse.
• He’s a brave firefighter.
• Maria is a good friend.

✅ Using “An”

• He’s an excellent musician.
• She is an honest leader.
• João is an amazing athlete.

📌 Compare: A vs An with People

Form Example Why?
A A university student “University” starts with a “you” sound (consonant)
An An honest man “Honest” starts with a vowel sound (silent “h”)
A A helpful neighbor “Helpful” starts with a consonant sound
An An ambitious student “Ambitious” starts with a vowel sound

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • She’s a talented dancer and an inspiring person.
  • He became a doctor after years of study.
  • Maria is an excellent teacher.
  • We met a kind woman and an energetic child.
  • He’s an honest man with a strong character.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ She is an teacher.
✅ She is a teacher.

❌ He is a engineer.
✅ He is an engineer.

❌ Maria is an good friend.
✅ Maria is a good friend.

❌ He is a honest man.
✅ He is an honest man.

Use “a” before consonant sounds and “an” before vowel sounds when describing people!

Practice with professions, traits, and roles to sound natural and accurate in English!
