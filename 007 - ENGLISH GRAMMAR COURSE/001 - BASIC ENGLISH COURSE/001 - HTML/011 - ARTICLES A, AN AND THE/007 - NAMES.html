🌎 ARTICLES & NAMES 🌎

Using “The” or No Article with Proper Names

🌟 What’s the Rule?

We usually don’t use articles with most proper names (people, countries, cities). But we do use “the” with names that include words like republic, kingdom, ocean, river, mountain range, or plural names.

  • ✅ I live in Brazil. (no article)
  • ✅ She visited the United States. (with “the”)
  • ✅ We climbed Mount Everest. (no article)
  • ✅ They swam in the Amazon River. (with “the”)

📋 Structure

Type of Name Use “the”? Example
Countries (single word) No Brazil, France, Japan
Countries (with “kingdom”, “states”, etc.) Yes The United Kingdom, The United States
Rivers, Oceans, Seas Yes The Amazon, The Pacific Ocean
Mountains (single peak) No Mount Everest, Mount Fuji
Mountain ranges Yes The Andes, The Alps
Buildings, Landmarks Yes The Eiffel Tower, The Empire State Building
People’s names No John, Maria, Mr. Smith

💡 Quick Tip

Use “the” with plural names, geographical features, and official titles. Don’t use “the” with most personal names or countries.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ No Article

  • Brazil is a beautiful country.
  • Mount Everest is the highest mountain.
  • London is the capital of England.
  • Maria is my best friend.
  • Asia is the largest continent.

✅ With “The”

  • The United States is very large.
  • The Amazon flows through South America.
  • The Alps are in Europe.
  • The Eiffel Tower is in Paris.
  • The Nile is a famous river.

📝 More Sentences

✅ No Article

• I live in Canada.
• She visited Rome last summer.
• We climbed Mount Kilimanjaro.
• John is a doctor.

✅ With “The”

• They traveled across the Sahara Desert.
• The United Nations held a meeting.
• We saw the Statue of Liberty.
• The Philippines is made up of many islands.

📌 Compare: With and Without “The”

Without “The” With “The”
Brazil is in South America. The United States is in North America.
Mount Everest is famous. The Himalayas are beautiful.
Asia is huge. The Pacific Ocean is deep.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • France is known for its food.
  • The Netherlands has many canals.
  • Mount Fuji is in Japan.
  • The Amazon Rainforest is full of wildlife.
  • John lives in New York.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ The Brazil is beautiful.
✅ Brazil is beautiful.

❌ I visited the Mount Everest.
✅ I visited Mount Everest.

❌ She lives in the London.
✅ She lives in London.

❌ The Maria is my friend.
✅ Maria is my friend.

Use “the” with plural names, geographical features, and official titles — but not with most personal names or countries!
