🧠 ARTICLES: SPECIAL CASES 🧠

Tricky Situations with “A”, “An” and “The”

🌟 What Are Special Cases?

Sometimes articles follow special patterns in English. We omit or always use “the” in certain expressions, places, and activities. These don’t follow the usual rules!

  • ✅ I go to school every day. (no article)
  • ✅ He plays the piano. (with “the”)
  • ✅ We arrived on the same day. (fixed expression)

📋 Common Patterns

Expression Article? Example
Go to school / church / bed / work No I go to school at 8.
Play the + instrument Yes She plays the guitar.
In the morning / afternoon / evening Yes We’ll meet in the afternoon.
At night No He studies at night.
The same / the next / the last Yes We arrived on the next day.
By car / bus / train No She came by bus.
Go to the cinema / the bank / the doctor Yes I went to the doctor.

💡 Quick Tip

Some places and expressions never use articles, while others always need “the”. Learn them as fixed phrases!

🎯 Common Expressions

✅ No Article

  • Go to school / college / work
  • Be in hospital / prison / bed
  • Travel by car / plane / train
  • At night

✅ With “The”

  • Go to the bank / the cinema / the supermarket
  • Play the piano / the violin / the drums
  • In the morning / the evening / the afternoon
  • The same / the next / the last

📝 More Sentences

✅ No Article

• She goes to school every day.
• He’s in hospital after the accident.
• We traveled by train.
• I sleep at night.

✅ With “The”

• I went to the supermarket.
• He plays the drums.
• We’ll meet in the morning.
• She wore the same dress as yesterday.

📌 Compare: With and Without “The”

Without “The” With “The”
He goes to school. He went to the school to fix the lights.
She’s in hospital. She visited the hospital as a tourist.
We traveled by car. We cleaned the car before traveling.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • He goes to church every Sunday.
  • We met in the afternoon.
  • She came by taxi.
  • I went to the cinema last night.
  • He plays the violin beautifully.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I go to the school every day. (general)
✅ I go to school every day.

❌ She is in the hospital. (as a patient)
✅ She is in hospital.

❌ He plays piano.
✅ He plays the piano.

❌ We traveled in the car.
✅ We traveled by car.

Learn special cases as fixed expressions — some need “the”, others never use it!
