👈 DETERMINERS: THIS, THAT, THESE, THOSE 👉

Using Demonstratives to Point to Things

🌟 What Are Demonstrative Determiners?

This, that, these, and those are used to point to people or things. They show distance (near or far) and number (singular or plural).

  • This → singular & near
  • That → singular & far
  • These → plural & near
  • Those → plural & far

📋 Structure

Determiner Number Distance Example
This Singular Near This book is interesting.
That Singular Far That car is fast.
These Plural Near These shoes are new.
Those Plural Far Those houses are old.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “this/these” for things close to you, and “that/those” for things farther away.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ This

  • This pen is mine.
  • This is my friend.
  • This chair is comfortable.

✅ That

  • That mountain is beautiful.
  • That restaurant is expensive.
  • That dog looks friendly.

✅ These

  • These apples are fresh.
  • These are my keys.
  • These students are smart.

✅ Those

  • Those shoes are dirty.
  • Those are my cousins.
  • Those buildings are old.

📝 More Sentences

✅ Singular

• This phone is new.
• That book belongs to Ana.
• This is delicious.
• That looks amazing.

✅ Plural

• These cookies are homemade.
• Those cars are expensive.
• These are my glasses.
• Those were great moments.

📌 Compare: Near vs Far

Near Far
This laptop is mine. That laptop is yours.
These books are interesting. Those books are boring.
This is my seat. That is your seat.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • This is my first time here.
  • That was a great movie.
  • These are my favorite jeans.
  • Those people are waiting in line.
  • This coffee tastes amazing.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ This books are good.
✅ These books are good.

❌ That apples are fresh.
✅ Those apples are fresh.

❌ These is my phone.
✅ This is my phone.

❌ Those is my bag.
✅ That is my bag.

Use “this/these” for things near you, and “that/those” for things farther away!
