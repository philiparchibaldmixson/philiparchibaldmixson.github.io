🔍 DETERMINERS: SOMEBODY, ANYTHING & NOWHERE 🔍

Using Indefinite Words to Talk About People, Things, and Places

🌟 What Are These Words?

Words like somebody, anything, and nowhere are called indefinite pronouns. We use them when we don’t say exactly who, what, or where we’re talking about.

  • Some- → used in positive sentences and offers
  • Any- → used in questions and negatives
  • No- → used to express absence or zero

📋 Structure

Type Form Example
People somebody / anybody / nobody Somebody is at the door.
Things something / anything / nothing I didn’t see anything.
Places somewhere / anywhere / nowhere We went nowhere last weekend.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “some-” in positive sentences, “any-” in questions/negatives, and “no-” to mean zero or none.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Somebody / Someone

  • Somebody called you.
  • Someone is waiting outside.

✅ Anybody / Anyone

  • Did anybody see my phone?
  • I don’t know anyone here.

✅ Nobody / No one

  • Nobody answered the phone.
  • No one was in the room.

✅ Something / Anything / Nothing

  • I heard something strange.
  • Do you want anything to drink?
  • There’s nothing in the fridge.

✅ Somewhere / Anywhere / Nowhere

  • Let’s go somewhere fun.
  • Is there anywhere open now?
  • We went nowhere yesterday.

📝 More Sentences

✅ Positive

• Somebody left their bag here.
• I want to eat something sweet.
• Let’s go somewhere quiet.

✅ Negative

• I didn’t see anybody at the station.
• She doesn’t want anything right now.
• We have nowhere to stay.

✅ Questions

• Did anyone call me?
• Is there anything I can do?
• Are you going anywhere this weekend?

📌 Compare: Some / Any / No

Positive Negative Zero
Somebody is at the door. I didn’t see anybody. Nobody was there.
She wants something to eat. She doesn’t want anything. She wants nothing.
We went somewhere nice. We didn’t go anywhere. We went nowhere.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • Somebody knocked on the door.
  • I didn’t hear anything.
  • We went nowhere because of the rain.
  • Is anyone home?
  • There’s nothing to worry about.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I didn’t see nobody.
✅ I didn’t see anybody.

❌ She doesn’t want nothing.
✅ She doesn’t want anything.

❌ We didn’t go nowhere.
✅ We didn’t go anywhere.

❌ Is there nothing I can do?
✅ Is there anything I can do?
