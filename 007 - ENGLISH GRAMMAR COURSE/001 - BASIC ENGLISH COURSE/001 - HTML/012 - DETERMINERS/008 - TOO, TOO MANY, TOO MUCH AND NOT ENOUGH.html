🚫 DETERMINERS: TOO, TOO MUCH, TOO MANY & NOT ENOUGH 🚫

Talking About Excess and Lack in English

🌟 What Do They Mean?

We use too, too much, too many, and not enough to talk about problems with quantity — either too much or not enough.

  • Too + adjective/adverb → It’s too hot.
  • Too much + uncountable noun → Too much sugar is unhealthy.
  • Too many + countable noun → Too many people were there.
  • Not enough + noun → We have not enough time.

📋 Structure

Form Used With Example
Too + adjective/adverb Describing quality This box is too heavy.
Too much + uncountable noun Describing excess He ate too much rice.
Too many + countable noun Describing excess There are too many cars.
Not enough + noun Describing lack We have not enough chairs.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “too” for problems of excess, and “not enough” for problems of lack. Watch the noun type: countable vs uncountable!

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Too + Adjective

  • This soup is too salty.
  • He’s too tired to work.
  • It’s too late to call.
  • The bag is too heavy.
  • She’s too shy to speak.

✅ Too Much

  • There’s too much noise.
  • He drank too much coffee.
  • We have too much homework.
  • She added too much salt.
  • I spent too much money.

✅ Too Many

  • There are too many people here.
  • We took too many photos.
  • She has too many clothes.
  • He asked too many questions.
  • I saw too many ads.

✅ Not Enough

  • We have not enough time.
  • There’s not enough food.
  • She didn’t get enough sleep.
  • He has not enough money.
  • There aren’t enough chairs.

📝 More Sentences

✅ Too / Too Much / Too Many

• This movie is too long.
• I ate too much cake.
• There are too many emails to read.

✅ Not Enough

• We don’t have enough time to finish.
• She didn’t bring enough clothes.
• There’s not enough light in the room.

📌 Compare: Too vs Not Enough

Too / Too Much / Too Many Not Enough
This coffee is too hot. This coffee isn’t hot enough.
We have too much work. We don’t have enough help.
There are too many people. There aren’t enough seats.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • It’s too cold to swim today.
  • We had too much food at the party.
  • There were too many cars on the road.
  • I didn’t have enough time to study.
  • She didn’t bring enough water for the hike.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I have too money.
✅ I have too much money.

❌ There are too much people.
✅ There are too many people.

❌ We don’t have enough chairs enough.
✅ We don’t have enough chairs.

❌ She is enough tall.
✅ She is tall enough.

Use “too” for excess and “not enough” for lack — and match the noun type correctly!
