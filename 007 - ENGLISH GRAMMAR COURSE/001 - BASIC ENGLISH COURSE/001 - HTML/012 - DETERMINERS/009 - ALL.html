🌐 DETERMINER: ALL 🌐

Using “All” to Talk About Entire Groups or Quantities

🌟 What Does “All” Mean?

All means every part or the whole amount. It can be used with plural countable nouns, uncountable nouns, and pronouns.

  • All + plural noun → All students passed the test.
  • All + uncountable noun → All water was filtered.
  • All of + the/my/this + noun → All of the money is gone.

📋 Structure

Form Used With Example
All + plural noun Countable (people/things) All students arrived early.
All + uncountable noun Mass nouns All information was correct.
All of + the/this/my + noun Specific reference All of the cake was eaten.
All of + pronoun Us / them / you All of us are ready.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “all” to refer to the complete group or quantity. Add “of” when followed by a determiner or pronoun.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ All + Plural Noun

  • All employees must attend.
  • All children love to play.
  • All books are on the shelf.
  • All cars were parked outside.
  • All students passed the exam.

✅ All + Uncountable Noun

  • All water is clean now.
  • All music was classical.
  • All sugar was used.
  • All money was donated.
  • All information is confidential.

✅ All of + Determiner

  • All of the students are here.
  • All of my clothes are clean.
  • All of this food looks delicious.
  • All of that noise was unnecessary.
  • All of the water spilled.

✅ All of + Pronoun

  • All of us agree.
  • All of them are invited.
  • All of you did well.
  • All of it was true.
  • All of me wants all of you. 🎵

📝 More Sentences

✅ General Use

• All students must register.
• All of the chairs are broken.
• All sugar was gone.
• All of us were surprised.
• All of this is yours.

📌 Compare: All vs All of

All All of
All students are present. All of the students are present.
All water is clean. All of the water is clean.
All books are new. All of my books are new.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • All of us went to the party.
  • All the lights are off.
  • All of the cake was eaten.
  • All students must wear uniforms.
  • All of this is important.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ All of students are here.
✅ All students are here.

❌ All my of books are new.
✅ All of my books are new.

❌ All of sugar was used.
✅ All sugar was used.

❌ All of the them are ready.
✅ All of them are ready.

Use “all” to refer to the whole group or quantity. Add “of” when followed by a determiner or pronoun!
