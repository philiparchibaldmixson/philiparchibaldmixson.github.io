🔢 DETERMINERS: ALL, EVERY & EACH 🔢

Talking About Groups and Individuals in English

🌟 What Do They Mean?

All, every, and each are determiners used to talk about groups or individuals within a group. They help us express totality or individuality.

  • All → the whole group or quantity
  • Every → all members of a group, seen individually
  • Each → every single item/person, one by one

📋 Structure

Determiner Used With Example
All Plural countable or uncountable nouns All students passed the test.
Every Singular countable nouns Every student got a certificate.
Each Singular countable nouns Each student received a gift.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “all” for the whole group, “every” for general individual focus, and “each” for specific individual attention.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ All

  • All children love to play.
  • All the water was clean.
  • All of us are ready.
  • All books are on the shelf.
  • All of them were invited.

✅ Every

  • Every day is a new chance.
  • Every student must register.
  • Every person matters.
  • Every question was answered.
  • Every country has its own culture.

✅ Each

  • Each child received a toy.
  • Each person has a role.
  • Each answer was correct.
  • Each student got a different book.
  • Each of them smiled.

📝 More Sentences

✅ All

• All employees must attend.
• All of the cake was eaten.
• All music was classical.

✅ Every

• Every morning I drink coffee.
• Every child deserves love.
• Every room was cleaned.

✅ Each

• Each guest received a gift.
• Each of us has a story.
• Each page has a number.

📌 Compare: All vs Every vs Each

All Every Each
All students passed. Every student passed. Each student passed individually.
All books are new. Every book is new. Each book is new and unique.
All of us agree. — Each of us has a different opinion.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • All students must wear uniforms.
  • Every person has rights.
  • Each employee has a task.
  • All of the lights are off.
  • Each of them smiled at me.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Every students are here.
✅ All students are here.
✅ Every student is here.

❌ Each students got a gift.
✅ Each student got a gift.

❌ All of student passed.
✅ All students passed.
✅ All of the students passed.

Use “all” for total groups, “every” for general individuals, and “each” for specific individuals!
