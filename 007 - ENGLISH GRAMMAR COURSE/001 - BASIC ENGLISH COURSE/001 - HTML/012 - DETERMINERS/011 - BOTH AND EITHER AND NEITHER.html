🧭 DETERMINERS: BOTH, EITHER & NEITHER 🧭

Talking About Two Options in English

🌟 What Do They Mean?

Both, either, and neither are determiners used when we talk about two people, things, or options.

  • Both → refers to two things together
  • Either → refers to one or the other (but not both)
  • Neither → refers to not one and not the other

📋 Structure

Determiner Meaning Example
Both Two together Both options are good.
Either One or the other Either answer is correct.
Neither Not one and not the other Neither person came.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “both” for two together, “either” for one of two, and “neither” for none of two.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Both

  • Both teams played well.
  • Both books are interesting.
  • Both of them are ready.
  • Both answers are correct.
  • Both my parents are teachers.

✅ Either

  • Either road leads to the city.
  • Either option is fine.
  • Either of them can help you.
  • Either hand works.
  • Either movie is good.

✅ Neither

  • Neither answer is correct.
  • Neither person showed up.
  • Neither of them agreed.
  • Neither road is safe.
  • Neither choice is ideal.

📝 More Sentences

✅ Both

• Both phones are expensive.
• Both of the shirts fit well.
• Both candidates gave speeches.

✅ Either

• You can take either bus.
• Either of the keys will open the door.
• Either solution works.

✅ Neither

• Neither answer helped me.
• Neither of the doors was unlocked.
• Neither student passed the test.

📌 Compare: Both vs Either vs Neither

Both Either Neither
Both books are interesting. Either book is fine. Neither book is useful.
Both of them agreed. Either of them can help. Neither of them came.
Both roads are open. Either road is okay. Neither road is safe.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • Both of us are tired.
  • Either of the restaurants is good.
  • Neither of the answers made sense.
  • Both shoes are dirty.
  • Neither option is perfect.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Both of student passed.
✅ Both students passed.
✅ Both of the students passed.

❌ Either students are correct.
✅ Either student is correct.

❌ Neither students came.
✅ Neither student came.

Use “both” for two together, “either” for one of two, and “neither” for none of two!
