📚 DETERMINERS + OF 📚

Using “of” with Determiners in English

🌟 What Does “of” Do?

We use of after determiners like some, all, each, none, many, few, both, neither, and either when followed by a pronoun or a determiner + noun.

  • Some of the people left early.
  • Each of them got a prize.
  • All of the cake was eaten.

📋 Structure

Form Used With Example
Determiner + of + the/my/this + noun Specific reference Some of the students are absent.
Determiner + of + pronoun Us / them / you / it Each of them is ready.
Determiner + noun General reference Some people are missing.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “of” when the determiner is followed by a pronoun or a specific noun with a determiner (like “the”, “my”, “this”).

🎯 Common Examples

✅ With “of”

  • All of the students passed.
  • Some of my friends are here.
  • None of the cake was left.
  • Each of us has a role.
  • Many of them agreed.
  • Few of the answers were correct.
  • Both of the options are valid.
  • Neither of the doors was open.
  • Either of the shirts fits.

✅ Without “of”

  • All students passed.
  • Some people are missing.
  • None came.
  • Each child received a gift.
  • Many countries joined.

📝 More Sentences

✅ With “of”

• Some of the water spilled.
• Each of the books is unique.
• None of them answered.
• Few of my classmates were present.
• All of it was true.

✅ Without “of”

• Some water spilled.
• Each book is unique.
• Few classmates were present.
• All truth matters.

📌 Compare: With vs Without “of”

With “of” Without “of”
All of the students are here. All students are here.
Some of my friends called me. Some friends called me.
Each of them smiled. Each person smiled.
None of the answers were correct. None were correct.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • All of us went to the party.
  • Some of the food was spicy.
  • None of the lights were working.
  • Each of the students got a medal.
  • Few of the ideas were accepted.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ All of students are here.
✅ All students are here.
✅ All of the students are here.

❌ Each of child got a gift.
✅ Each child got a gift.
✅ Each of the children got a gift.

❌ Some of water spilled.
✅ Some water spilled.
✅ Some of the water spilled.

Use “of” when the determiner is followed by a pronoun or a specific noun with a determiner!
