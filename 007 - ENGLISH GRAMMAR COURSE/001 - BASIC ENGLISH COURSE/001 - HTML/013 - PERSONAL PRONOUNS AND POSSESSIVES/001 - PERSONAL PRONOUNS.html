🙋 PERSONAL PRONOUNS 🙋

Talking About People and Things in English

🌟 What Are Personal Pronouns?

Personal pronouns are words we use to replace nouns and refer to people, animals, or things. They show who is doing something or who something is happening to.

  • Subject pronouns → I, you, he, she, it, we, they
  • Object pronouns → me, you, him, her, it, us, them

📋 Pronoun Chart

Person Subject Object Example
1st singular I me I love music. / She called me.
2nd singular you you You are kind. / I saw you.
3rd singular (male) he him He is tall. / I met him.
3rd singular (female) she her She is smart. / I helped her.
3rd singular (thing) it it It is broken. / I fixed it.
1st plural we us We are ready. / They saw us.
2nd plural you you You are welcome. / We invited you.
3rd plural they them They are here. / I called them.

💡 Quick Tip

Use subject pronouns before the verb. Use object pronouns after the verb or preposition.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Subject Pronouns

  • I am learning English.
  • You look happy today.
  • He plays football.
  • She loves chocolate.
  • It is raining.
  • We are friends.
  • They live in Brazil.

✅ Object Pronouns

  • She called me.
  • I saw you yesterday.
  • We invited him to the party.
  • He helped her with homework.
  • I fixed it this morning.
  • They met us at the park.
  • We saw them at the concert.

📝 More Sentences

✅ Subject Pronouns

• I like pizza.
• He is my brother.
• We study together.
• They are coming soon.

✅ Object Pronouns

• She invited me.
• I saw him at school.
• We called them last night.
• He gave us a gift.

📌 Compare: Subject vs Object

Subject Pronoun Object Pronoun
She loves music. Music loves her.
They called us. We called them.
I saw him. He saw me.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I am from Bahia.
  • She works at a hospital.
  • He gave me a ride.
  • We are going to the beach.
  • They invited us to dinner.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Me am happy.
✅ I am happy.

❌ Us are here.
✅ We are here.

❌ She loves I.
✅ She loves me.

❌ Them called us.
✅ They called us.

Use subject pronouns to say who does the action. Use object pronouns to say who receives it!
