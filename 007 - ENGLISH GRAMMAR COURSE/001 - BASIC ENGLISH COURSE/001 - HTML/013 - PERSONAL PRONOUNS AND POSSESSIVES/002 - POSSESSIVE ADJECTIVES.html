🎒 POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES 🎒

Describing What Belongs to Whom

🌟 What Are Possessive Adjectives?

Possessive adjectives are words we use to show ownership. They come before a noun and tell us who something belongs to.

  • my → belongs to me
  • your → belongs to you
  • his → belongs to him
  • her → belongs to her
  • its → belongs to it
  • our → belongs to us
  • their → belongs to them

📋 Adjective Chart

Person Possessive Adjective Example
1st singular my This is my bag.
2nd singular/plural your Is that your phone?
3rd singular (male) his This is his car.
3rd singular (female) her That is her notebook.
3rd singular (thing) its The dog wagged its tail.
1st plural our This is our house.
3rd plural their Those are their books.

💡 Quick Tip

Possessive adjectives are always followed by a noun. They do not stand alone!

🎯 Common Examples

  • This is my pencil.
  • That is your seat.
  • He forgot his keys.
  • She loves her dog.
  • The cat licked its paw.
  • We painted our room.
  • They brought their instruments.

📌 Compare: Possessive Adjective vs Possessive Pronoun

Possessive Adjective Possessive Pronoun
This is my book. This book is mine.
That is your car. That car is yours.
It’s our idea. The idea is ours.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • My favorite color is blue.
  • Your coffee is on the table.
  • His jacket is new.
  • Her bag is heavy.
  • Its name is Luna.
  • Our school is big.
  • Their apartment is downtown.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ This is mine book.
✅ This is my book.

❌ That is hers phone.
✅ That is her phone.

❌ Theirs house is big.
✅ Their house is big.

❌ Its a nice day.
✅ It’s a nice day. (It’s = it is)

Use possessive adjectives before nouns to show who something belongs to!
