🔁 REFLEXIVE PRONOUNS 🔁

Talking About Actions Done to Ourselves

🌟 What Are Reflexive Pronouns?

Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and object of a sentence are the same person. They show that someone does something to themselves.

  • myself → I did it to me
  • yourself → you did it to you
  • himself → he did it to him
  • herself → she did it to her
  • itself → it did it to it
  • ourselves → we did it to us
  • yourselves → you (plural) did it to you
  • themselves → they did it to them

📋 Pronoun Chart

Subject Reflexive Pronoun Example
I myself I made this cake myself.
you (singular) yourself You should be proud of yourself.
he himself He hurt himself playing football.
she herself She taught herself to play piano.
it itself The cat cleaned itself.
we ourselves We prepared ourselves for the test.
you (plural) yourselves You all should enjoy yourselves.
they themselves They introduced themselves to the group.

💡 Quick Tip

Use reflexive pronouns when the subject and object are the same. They often follow verbs like “cut”, “hurt”, “teach”, “enjoy”, “prepare”.

🎯 Common Examples

  • I looked at myself in the mirror.
  • Did you do this yourself?
  • He blamed himself for the mistake.
  • She introduced herself to the class.
  • The dog cleaned itself.
  • We taught ourselves how to code.
  • You should be proud of yourselves.
  • They organized the event themselves.

📝 More Sentences

• I made this dress myself.
• She cut herself while cooking.
• We enjoyed ourselves at the party.
• He taught himself French.
• They found themselves in trouble.
• You should treat yourself sometimes.

📌 Compare: Reflexive vs Regular Pronoun

Regular Pronoun Reflexive Pronoun
I saw him. He saw himself.
She helped me. She helped herself.
We called them. They called themselves.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I introduced myself to the teacher.
  • He blamed himself for the accident.
  • She taught herself to paint.
  • We prepared ourselves for the challenge.
  • They found themselves lost in the forest.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ He saw him in the mirror.
✅ He saw himself in the mirror.

❌ She hurt her while running.
✅ She hurt herself while running.

❌ We taught us Spanish.
✅ We taught ourselves Spanish.

❌ They introduced them to the group.
✅ They introduced themselves to the group.

Use reflexive pronouns when the subject and object are the same person or group!
