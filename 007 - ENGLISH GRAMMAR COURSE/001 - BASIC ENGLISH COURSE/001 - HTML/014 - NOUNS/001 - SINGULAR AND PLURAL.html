📚 NOUNS: SINGULAR & PLURAL 📚

Understanding One vs More in English

🌟 What Are Singular and Plural Nouns?

Nouns name people, places, things, or ideas. They can be singular (one) or plural (more than one).

  • Singular → refers to one item (e.g., book, child)
  • Plural → refers to more than one (e.g., books, children)

📋 Basic Rules

Rule Singular Plural
Add “-s” car cars
Add “-es” box boxes
Change “y” to “ies” baby babies
Irregular plural child children
No change sheep sheep

💡 Quick Tip

Most nouns add “-s” to become plural, but watch out for irregular forms!

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Regular Plurals

  • book → books
  • table → tables
  • pen → pens
  • apple → apples
  • student → students

✅ “-es” Endings

  • box → boxes
  • bus → buses
  • watch → watches
  • dish → dishes
  • class → classes

✅ “y” to “ies”

  • baby → babies
  • city → cities
  • party → parties
  • story → stories
  • puppy → puppies

✅ Irregular Plurals

  • man → men
  • woman → women
  • child → children
  • foot → feet
  • tooth → teeth

📝 More Sentences

• I have one book.
• She bought three books.
• The child is playing.
• The children are laughing.
• A man entered the room.
• Two men were waiting outside.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • The student is reading.
  • The students are writing.
  • I saw a fox in the forest.
  • We saw two foxes yesterday.
  • She has a puppy.
  • They have three puppies.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I have two book.
✅ I have two books.

❌ The childs are playing.
✅ The children are playing.

❌ She has many foots.
✅ She has many feet.

❌ There is three cat.
✅ There are three cats.

Use singular nouns for one item and plural nouns for more than one!
