🧱 NOUN + NOUN STRUCTURE 🧱

Combining Nouns to Create New Ideas

🌟 What Is “Noun + Noun”?

Sometimes we use two nouns together to create a new idea. The first noun acts like an adjective and gives more information about the second noun.

  • chicken soup → soup made with chicken
  • car door → the door of a car
  • student card → a card for a student

📋 Structure

Form Meaning Example
noun + noun Type or purpose coffee cup → a cup for coffee
noun + noun Material or content paper bag → a bag made of paper
noun + noun Owner or user teacher desk → a desk used by a teacher
noun + noun Location or part kitchen window → a window in the kitchen

💡 Quick Tip

The first noun usually describes the second noun. It tells us what kind, what for, or what material.

📝 More Sentences

• I need a new coffee mug.
• She forgot her student card.
• The car engine is broken.
• We bought a wood table.
• He opened the kitchen window.

📌 Compare: Noun + Noun vs Adjective + Noun

Noun + Noun Adjective + Noun
chicken soup → soup made with chicken hot soup → soup that is hot
glass door → door made of glass clean door → door that is clean
car key → key for a car small key → key that is small

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I lost my car keys.
  • She bought a coffee table.
  • We need a passport photo.
  • The garden wall is very high.
  • This glass bottle is recyclable.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I need a soup chicken.
✅ I need chicken soup.

❌ She has a card student.
✅ She has a student card.

❌ The door car is open.
✅ The car door is open.

❌ He bought a table wood.
✅ He bought a wood table.

Use “noun + noun” to describe type, purpose, material, location, or user!
