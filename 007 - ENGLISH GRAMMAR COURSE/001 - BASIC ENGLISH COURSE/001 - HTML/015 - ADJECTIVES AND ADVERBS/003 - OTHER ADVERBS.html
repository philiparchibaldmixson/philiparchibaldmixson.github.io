🧭 OTHER ADVERBS 🧭

Describing Time, Place, Frequency, Degree, and Certainty

🌟 What Are Adverbs?

Adverbs are words that modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. They give us more information about how, when, where, how often, or to what extent something happens.

  • Time → now, soon, yesterday
  • Place → here, there, outside
  • Frequency → always, often, never
  • Degree → very, quite, too
  • Certainty → probably, definitely, maybe

📋 Structure

Type Function Example
Time When something happens She arrived yesterday.
Place Where something happens The kids are playing outside.
Frequency How often something happens I always drink coffee in the morning.
Degree To what extent She is very tired.
Certainty How sure we are He will probably come later.

💡 Quick Tip

Adverbs can appear at the beginning, middle, or end of a sentence depending on their type.

📌 Compare: Adverb vs Adjective

Adjective Adverb
She is happy. She smiled happily.
He is quiet. He spoke quietly.
The dog is angry. The dog barked angrily.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I go always to school.
✅ I always go to school.

❌ She sings beautiful.
✅ She sings beautifully.

❌ He is very much tired.
✅ He is very tired.

❌ They play in outside.
✅ They play outside.

Use adverbs to describe time, place, frequency, degree, and certainty. They make your sentences more complete!
