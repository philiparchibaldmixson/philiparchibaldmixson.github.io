🚀 ADVERBS WITH VERBS 🚀

Describing How Actions Are Performed

🌟 What Are Adverbs with Verbs?

Adverbs with verbs describe how, when, where, or how often an action happens. They give more detail to the verb in the sentence.

  • She sings beautifully. → how
  • He arrived late. → when
  • They play outside. → where
  • I always study at night. → how often

📋 Structure

Position Example
After the verb She speaks clearly.
Before the verb He quickly opened the door.
Between auxiliary and main verb They have always worked hard.
At the beginning or end of the sentence Yesterday, we arrived late. / We arrived late yesterday.

💡 Quick Tip

Adverbs often end in “-ly” and can move around in the sentence depending on what they describe.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Adverbs of Manner (How)

  • run quickly
  • drive carefully
  • sing loudly
  • write neatly
  • work hard

✅ Adverbs of Time (When)

  • arrive early
  • leave soon
  • start now
  • finish later
  • come yesterday

✅ Adverbs of Place (Where)

  • go outside
  • sit here
  • stand there
  • travel abroad
  • walk inside

✅ Adverbs of Frequency (How Often)

  • study always
  • exercise often
  • eat sometimes
  • forget rarely
  • complain never

📝 More Sentences

• He runs fast every morning.
• She usually arrives on time.
• They danced gracefully.
• I never eat meat.
• We met outside the café.

📌 Compare: Verb Alone vs Verb + Adverb

Verb Only Verb + Adverb
She speaks. She speaks clearly.
He drives. He drives carefully.
They arrived. They arrived late.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • She answered politely.
  • He quickly closed the window.
  • We always eat together.
  • The baby slept peacefully.
  • I rarely watch TV.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ He runs quick.
✅ He runs quickly.

❌ She sings beautiful.
✅ She sings beautifully.

❌ I go always to school.
✅ I always go to school.

❌ They play in outside.
✅ They play outside.

Use adverbs with verbs to describe how, when, where, or how often actions happen!
