📊 COMPARATIVE OR SUPERLATIVE? 📊

Choosing the Right Form to Compare

🌟 What’s the Difference?

We use comparative adjectives to compare two things. We use superlative adjectives to compare three or more things and show the highest or lowest degree.

  • Comparative → taller, more expensive, better
  • Superlative → tallest, most expensive, best

📋 When to Use Each

Use Comparative Superlative
Number of items 2 3 or more
Structure adjective + “-er” or “more” + adjective “the” + adjective + “-est” or “most” + adjective
Example My car is faster than yours. This is the fastest car in the world.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “than” with comparatives. Use “the” with superlatives.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Comparative

  • This book is more interesting than that one.
  • She is taller than her sister.
  • My phone is better than yours.
  • Today is colder than yesterday.
  • This sofa is more comfortable than the old one.

✅ Superlative

  • This is the most interesting book I’ve read.
  • He is the tallest player on the team.
  • That was the best movie ever.
  • It’s the coldest day of the year.
  • This is the most comfortable chair in the house.

📝 More Sentences

• My car is faster than yours.
• This is the fastest car I’ve ever driven.
• She’s more confident now than before.
• He’s the most confident speaker in the group.
• That exam was easier than the last one.
• It was the easiest question on the test.

📌 Compare: Two vs Many

Comparative (2 items) Superlative (3+ items)
John is taller than Mike. John is the tallest in the class.
This sofa is more comfortable than that one. This is the most comfortable sofa here.
She is better than him. She is the best player on the team.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • This pizza is better than the last one.
  • He’s the most intelligent student in the class.
  • My house is bigger than yours.
  • That was the worst experience ever.
  • She’s more confident now than before.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ She is more tall than him.
✅ She is taller than him.

❌ This is the most cold day.
✅ This is the coldest day.

❌ He is the better student.
✅ He is the best student.

❌ That car is most fast.
✅ That car is the fastest.

Use comparatives to compare two things. Use superlatives to show the highest or lowest among many!
