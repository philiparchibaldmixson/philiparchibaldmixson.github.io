🏆 SUPERLATIVES 🏆

Describing the Highest or Lowest Degree

🌟 What Are Superlatives?

Superlatives are adjectives used to show the highest or lowest degree of a quality. We use them to compare three or more things.

  • the tallest → the most tall of all
  • the most expensive → costs more than all others
  • the best → highest quality

📋 How to Form Superlatives

Adjective Type Superlative Form Example
Short (1 syllable) +est tall → the tallest
Ends in “y” change “y” to “i” + est happy → the happiest
Long (2+ syllables) most + adjective beautiful → the most beautiful
Irregular varies good → the best

💡 Quick Tip

Always use “the” before a superlative adjective.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Regular Superlatives

  • small → the smallest
  • cold → the coldest
  • easy → the easiest
  • nice → the nicest
  • hot → the hottest

✅ Long Adjectives

  • expensive → the most expensive
  • comfortable → the most comfortable
  • important → the most important
  • interesting → the most interesting
  • dangerous → the most dangerous

✅ Irregular Superlatives

  • good → the best
  • bad → the worst
  • far → the farthest / the furthest

📝 More Sentences

• This is the most exciting game I’ve ever played.
• She is the smartest student in the class.
• That was the worst day of my life.
• This is the biggest pizza I’ve ever seen.
• He’s the most talented musician here.

📌 Compare: Superlative vs Comparative

Comparative (2 items) Superlative (3+ items)
John is taller than Mike. John is the tallest in the class.
This sofa is more comfortable than that one. This is the most comfortable sofa here.
She is better than him. She is the best player on the team.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • This is the best pizza in town.
  • He’s the most polite person I know.
  • My bag is the lightest of all.
  • This movie is the most exciting of the year.
  • She’s the most creative in her class.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ She is most tall in the group.
✅ She is the tallest in the group.

❌ This is best pizza.
✅ This is the best pizza.

❌ He is most fast.
✅ He is the fastest.

❌ That car is more cheap.
✅ That car is the cheapest.

Use superlatives to show the highest or lowest degree among three or more things!
