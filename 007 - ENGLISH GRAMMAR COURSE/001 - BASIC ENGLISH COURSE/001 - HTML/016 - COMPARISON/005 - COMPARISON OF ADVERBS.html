⚖️ COMPARISON OF ADVERBS ⚖️

Comparing How Actions Are Performed

🌟 What Is Adverb Comparison?

We use comparative and superlative forms of adverbs to compare how actions are done. They show differences in speed, manner, frequency, or degree.

  • Comparative → more quickly, better, less carefully
  • Superlative → most quickly, best, least carefully

📋 How to Form Comparatives and Superlatives

Adverb Type Comparative Superlative Example
Regular (-ly) more + adverb most + adverb quickly → more quickly → most quickly
Irregular varies varies well → better → best
Short adverbs +er +est fast → faster → fastest
Negative comparison less + adverb least + adverb carefully → less carefully → least carefully

💡 Quick Tip

Use “than” with comparatives. Use “the” with superlatives. Irregular forms must be memorized!

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Comparative Adverbs

  • She runs faster than me.
  • He speaks more clearly than before.
  • I work better under pressure.
  • They arrived more quickly than expected.
  • She listens less patiently than her sister.

✅ Superlative Adverbs

  • He drives the fastest of all.
  • She answered the most politely.
  • This team performed the best in the competition.
  • He reacted the least calmly.
  • They arrived the earliest.

📝 More Sentences

• She sings better than her brother.
• He works more efficiently than before.
• I study less frequently during holidays.
• They responded the most quickly.
• He speaks the most confidently in meetings.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ He speaks good.
✅ He speaks well.

❌ She runs more fast.
✅ She runs faster.

❌ He works most hard.
✅ He works the hardest.

❌ I study more frequent.
✅ I study more frequently.

Use comparative adverbs to compare two actions. Use superlative adverbs to show the highest or lowest degree!
