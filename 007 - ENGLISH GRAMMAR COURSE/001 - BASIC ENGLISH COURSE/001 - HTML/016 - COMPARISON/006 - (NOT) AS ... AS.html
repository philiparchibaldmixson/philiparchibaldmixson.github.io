⚖️ (NOT) AS ... AS ⚖️

Expressing Equality and Inequality

🌟 What Does “As ... As” Mean?

We use as + adjective/adverb + as to compare two things that are equal in some way. We use not as ... as to show that they are not equal.

  • as tall as → same height
  • not as fast as → slower
  • as carefully as → same level of care

📋 Structure

Form Use Example
as + adjective + as Equality She is as tall as her brother.
not as + adjective + as Inequality This car is not as fast as mine.
as + adverb + as Equality in action He runs as quickly as his friend.
not as + adverb + as Inequality in action She doesn’t speak as clearly as the teacher.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “as ... as” to show similarity. Use “not as ... as” to show difference.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Equality

  • My phone is as good as yours.
  • She is as smart as her sister.
  • This chair is as comfortable as the sofa.
  • He works as hard as anyone.
  • They arrived as early as we did.

✅ Inequality

  • This movie is not as interesting as the last one.
  • He isn’t as tall as his father.
  • She doesn’t drive as carefully as her brother.
  • My bag isn’t as heavy as yours.
  • We didn’t finish as quickly as expected.

📝 More Sentences

• This test is as difficult as the last one.
• He’s not as friendly as he seems.
• The second book wasn’t as exciting as the first.
• She sings as beautifully as a professional.
• I’m not as tired as I was yesterday.

📌 Compare: As ... As vs More/Less Than

As ... As Comparative
He is as fast as his brother. He is faster than his brother.
This room is not as big as the other. This room is smaller than the other.
She works as hard as anyone. She works harder than most people.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • This phone isn’t as expensive as it looks.
  • He’s as talented as his idol.
  • We didn’t arrive as early as we hoped.
  • Her dress is as elegant as the designer’s.
  • That explanation wasn’t as clear as the first one.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ He is as taller as me.
✅ He is as tall as me.

❌ She is not so smart as her sister.
✅ She is not as smart as her sister.

❌ This is as more expensive as that.
✅ This is as expensive as that.

❌ He runs as fast than me.
✅ He runs as fast as me.

Use “as ... as” to show equality. Use “not as ... as” to show difference. It’s simple and powerful!
