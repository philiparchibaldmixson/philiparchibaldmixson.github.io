🔗 CONJUNCTIONS: INTRODUCTION 🔗

Connecting Words, Ideas, and Sentences

🌟 What Are Conjunctions?

Conjunctions are words that connect other words, phrases, or clauses. They help us build complex and meaningful sentences.

  • and → adds ideas
  • but → shows contrast
  • because → shows reason
  • if → shows condition
  • although → shows contradiction

📋 Types of Conjunctions

Type Function Examples
Coordinating Connect equal parts and, but, or, so, yet
Subordinating Connect dependent clauses because, although, if, when, while
Correlative Used in pairs either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also

💡 Quick Tip

Use conjunctions to avoid short, choppy sentences. They make your writing smoother and clearer.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Coordinating Conjunctions

  • I like tea and coffee.
  • She is tired, but she keeps working.
  • You can choose pizza or pasta.
  • He was late, so we started without him.
  • It’s cold, yet sunny.

✅ Subordinating Conjunctions

  • I stayed home because it was raining.
  • Although she was tired, she finished the task.
  • If you study, you’ll pass.
  • When I arrived, they were leaving.
  • While he cooked, I cleaned.

✅ Correlative Conjunctions

  • Either you come, or I go.
  • Neither John nor Mary was there.
  • Not only is she smart, but also kind.

📝 More Sentences

• I want to go out, but it’s raining.
• We’ll leave when the movie ends.
• Although he tried hard, he didn’t win.
• If you call me, I’ll answer.
• She sings and dances beautifully.

📌 Compare: Without vs With Conjunctions

Without Conjunction With Conjunction
I was tired. I worked. I was tired, but I worked.
He studied. He passed. He passed because he studied.
We waited. They arrived. We waited until they arrived.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • She didn’t come because she was sick.
  • I’ll go if you go.
  • He’s smart and funny.
  • Although it’s late, I’m still working.
  • Not only did he win, but also broke the record.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I like apples but oranges.
✅ I like apples and oranges.

❌ He is tired, because he works.
✅ He is tired because he works.

❌ I go if you go.
✅ I’ll go if you go.

❌ She is not only smart but also.
✅ She is not only smart but also kind.

Use conjunctions to connect ideas and make your sentences flow naturally!
