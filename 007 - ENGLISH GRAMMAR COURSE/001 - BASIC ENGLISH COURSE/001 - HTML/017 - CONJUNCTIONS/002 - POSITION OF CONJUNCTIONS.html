📍 POSITION OF CONJUNCTIONS 📍

Where Conjunctions Appear in Sentences

🌟 What Is Conjunction Position?

Conjunctions can appear at the beginning, middle, or end of a sentence depending on their type and function. They help connect ideas smoothly.

  • Coordinating conjunctions → usually in the middle
  • Subordinating conjunctions → beginning or middle
  • Correlative conjunctions → paired in different parts

📋 Position by Type

Type Position Example
Coordinating Middle I was tired, but I kept working.
Subordinating Beginning or Middle Although it was late, I stayed. / I stayed although it was late.
Correlative Split Not only did he win, but also broke the record.

💡 Quick Tip

Subordinating conjunctions can start a sentence. Coordinating ones usually connect two ideas in the middle.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Coordinating Conjunctions (Middle)

  • I like tea, and I like coffee.
  • She is tired, but she keeps going.
  • You can stay here, or you can leave.
  • He was late, so we started without him.

✅ Subordinating Conjunctions (Beginning or Middle)

  • Because it was raining, we stayed home.
  • We stayed home because it was raining.
  • If you study, you’ll pass.
  • You’ll pass if you study.
  • Although he was tired, he worked.
  • He worked although he was tired.

✅ Correlative Conjunctions (Split)

  • Either you come, or I go.
  • Neither John nor Mary was there.
  • Not only is she smart, but also kind.

📝 More Sentences

• Although it was cold, we went outside.
• I’ll help you if I have time.
• She sings and dances.
• Because he was late, he missed the bus.
• Not only did she win, but also broke the record.

📌 Compare: Beginning vs Middle

Beginning Middle
Although it was raining, we went out. We went out although it was raining.
If you call me, I’ll answer. I’ll answer if you call me.
Because he studied, he passed. He passed because he studied.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • Although it’s late, I’m still working.
  • I’ll go if you go.
  • She didn’t come because she was sick.
  • He’s smart and funny.
  • Not only did he win, but also inspired others.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Because he was late. He missed the bus.
✅ Because he was late, he missed the bus.

❌ I go. If you go.
✅ I’ll go if you go.

❌ She is not only smart. But also kind.
✅ She is not only smart but also kind.

❌ Although he was tired. He worked.
✅ Although he was tired, he worked.

Use conjunctions in the right position to connect ideas clearly and naturally!
