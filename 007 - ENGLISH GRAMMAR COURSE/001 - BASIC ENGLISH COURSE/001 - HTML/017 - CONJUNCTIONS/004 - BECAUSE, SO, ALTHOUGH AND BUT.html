🔗 CONJUNCTIONS: BECAUSE, SO, ALTHOUGH, BUT 🔗

Connecting Ideas with Reason, Result, and Contrast

🌟 What Do These Conjunctions Do?

These conjunctions help us connect ideas and show cause, result, or contrast between actions or thoughts.

  • because → shows reason
  • so → shows result
  • although → shows contradiction
  • but → shows contrast

📋 Structure and Meaning

Conjunction Function Example
because Gives a reason I stayed home because it was raining.
so Shows a result It was raining, so I stayed home.
although Shows contradiction Although it was raining, I went out.
but Shows contrast It was raining, but I went out.

💡 Quick Tip

Use because for reasons, so for results, and although/but for contrast.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Using “because”

  • She’s happy because she passed the test.
  • I’m tired because I didn’t sleep well.

✅ Using “so”

  • I didn’t sleep well, so I’m tired.
  • He studied hard, so he passed the test.

✅ Using “although”

  • Although it was cold, we went swimming.
  • Although she was tired, she kept working.

✅ Using “but”

  • It was cold, but we went swimming.
  • She was tired, but she kept working.

📝 More Sentences

• I didn’t go out because I was sick.
• He was late, so we started without him.
• Although I was nervous, I gave the presentation.
• She’s shy, but she’s very friendly.
• We missed the bus because we left late.

📌 Compare: Reason vs Result vs Contrast

Reason (because) Result (so) Contrast (although/but)
I stayed home because I was sick. I was sick, so I stayed home. Although I was sick, I went out. / I was sick, but I went out.
He left early because he was tired. He was tired, so he left early. Although he was tired, he stayed. / He was tired, but he stayed.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I didn’t eat lunch because I was busy.
  • She was busy, so she didn’t eat lunch.
  • Although he was hungry, he didn’t eat.
  • He was hungry, but he didn’t eat.
  • We were tired, so we went home early.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I didn’t go because I was tired so.
✅ I didn’t go because I was tired.

❌ Although it was raining, but we went out.
✅ Although it was raining, we went out.

❌ He was late because so we left.
✅ He was late, so we left.

❌ I was tired, although I slept well.
✅ I was tired although I had slept well.

Use “because” for reasons, “so” for results, and “although/but” for contrast. They make your sentences clear and connected!
