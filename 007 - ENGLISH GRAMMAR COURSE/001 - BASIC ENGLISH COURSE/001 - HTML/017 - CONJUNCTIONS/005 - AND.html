➕ CONJUNCTIONS: AND ➕

Adding Ideas Together

🌟 What Does “AND” Do?

The conjunction and is used to connect words, phrases, or clauses that are similar or related. It adds one idea to another.

  • Connects nouns: apples and oranges
  • Connects verbs: run and jump
  • Connects adjectives: tall and strong
  • Connects clauses: I called her and she answered

📋 Structure and Use

Use Example
Connect nouns I like tea and coffee.
Connect verbs He reads and writes every day.
Connect adjectives She is kind and smart.
Connect clauses I called her and she answered.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “and” to add information. It shows that both parts are true or happening together.

🎯 Common Examples

  • I like music and movies.
  • He runs and swims every morning.
  • She is funny and friendly.
  • We ate dinner and watched a movie.
  • They studied hard and passed the exam.

📝 More Sentences

• I bought bread and cheese.
• He opened the door and walked in.
• She is young and talented.
• We laughed and cried during the movie.
• I need paper and pens for class.

📌 Compare: Without vs With “AND”

Without “and” With “and”
I like apples. I like bananas. I like apples and bananas.
She sings. She dances. She sings and dances.
He is tall. He is strong. He is tall and strong.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • We talked and laughed for hours.
  • She cooked dinner and cleaned the kitchen.
  • I need milk and eggs from the store.
  • He is smart and creative.
  • They played soccer and had fun.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I like and apples.
✅ I like apples and bananas.

❌ She is and beautiful.
✅ She is smart and beautiful.

❌ He runs and.
✅ He runs and swims.

❌ We ate and.
✅ We ate and drank together.

Use “and” to connect ideas, actions, and descriptions. It makes your sentences richer and clearer!

Practice with real examples to speak and write naturally in English!
