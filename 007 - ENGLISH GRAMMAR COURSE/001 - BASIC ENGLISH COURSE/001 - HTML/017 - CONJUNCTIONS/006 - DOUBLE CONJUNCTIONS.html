🧩 DOUBLE CONJUNCTIONS 🧩

Connecting Ideas in Pairs

🌟 What Are Double Conjunctions?

Double conjunctions are pairs of words that work together to connect ideas and show choice, combination, or contrast. They make sentences more expressive and balanced.

  • either ... or → choice between two options
  • neither ... nor → negative of both options
  • both ... and → combination of two ideas
  • not only ... but also → emphasis and addition

📋 Structure and Meaning

Double Conjunction Function Example
either ... or Choose one Either you come, or I go.
neither ... nor Reject both Neither John nor Mary was there.
both ... and Include both Both the teacher and the students were excited.
not only ... but also Emphasize addition Not only is she smart, but also kind.

💡 Quick Tip

Keep the sentence balanced. What comes after the first part should match the second part.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Either ... Or

  • Either you study, or you fail.
  • We can either walk or take a taxi.

✅ Neither ... Nor

  • Neither the manager nor the assistant was available.
  • I neither smoke nor drink.

✅ Both ... And

  • Both my mom and dad love music.
  • She is both talented and hardworking.

✅ Not Only ... But Also

  • Not only did he win, but also broke the record.
  • She’s not only beautiful but also intelligent.

📝 More Sentences

• Either you apologize, or I leave.
• Neither the book nor the movie impressed me.
• Both the cake and the cookies were delicious.
• Not only was the concert amazing, but also unforgettable.
• She not only speaks English, but also French.

📌 Compare: Double Conjunctions in Action

Conjunction Meaning Example
either ... or One of two Either he goes, or I go.
neither ... nor None of two Neither the teacher nor the student knew the answer.
both ... and Two together Both the cat and the dog are sleeping.
not only ... but also Extra emphasis Not only did she sing, but also danced.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • Either you help me, or I’ll do it alone.
  • Neither my phone nor my laptop is working.
  • Both the movie and the soundtrack were amazing.
  • Not only did he arrive early, but also brought breakfast.
  • She not only teaches English, but also Spanish.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Either you come or.
✅ Either you come, or I go.

❌ Neither John or Mary was there.
✅ Neither John nor Mary was there.

❌ Both she and is smart.
✅ Both she and he are smart.

❌ Not only she sings but also.
✅ Not only does she sing, but also plays guitar.

Use double conjunctions to express choice, combination, or contrast with clarity and style!
