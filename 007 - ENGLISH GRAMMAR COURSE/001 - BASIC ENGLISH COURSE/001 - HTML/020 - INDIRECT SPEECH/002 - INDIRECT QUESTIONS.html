❓ INDIRECT SPEECH: INDIRECT QUESTIONS ❓

Reporting Questions Politely and Correctly

🌟 What Is an Indirect Question?

Indirect questions are used to report a question someone asked, without using the exact words. We don’t use question word order and don’t use a question mark.

  • Direct: “Where does she live?”
  • Indirect: He asked where she lived.

📋 Structure

Type Form Example
Yes/No question ask + if / whether + subject + verb She asked if I was busy.
Wh-question ask + question word + subject + verb He asked where I lived.
Polite question Can you tell me + question word + subject + verb Can you tell me what time it starts?

💡 Quick Tip

In indirect questions, use statement word order (subject + verb), not question word order.

🎯 Common Examples

✅ Yes/No Questions

  • He asked if I liked coffee.
  • She asked whether I was free on Friday.
  • They asked if we had arrived.

✅ Wh-Questions

  • I asked where she lived.
  • He asked what I wanted.
  • She asked why we were late.

📝 More Sentences

• She asked how old I was.
• He asked when the train left.
• I asked why she was crying.
• They asked if I had finished the project.
• He asked what I was doing at that moment.

📌 Compare: Direct vs Indirect Questions

Direct Question Indirect Question
“Where do you live?” She asked where I lived.
“Are you busy?” He asked if I was busy.
“What time does it start?” Can you tell me what time it starts?

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • She asked if I had a pen.
  • He asked where I was going.
  • I asked what her name was.
  • They asked if we could help.
  • Can you tell me how much it costs?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ He asked where do I live.
✅ He asked where I lived.

❌ She asked what are you doing.
✅ She asked what I was doing.

❌ I asked if did she like it.
✅ I asked if she liked it.

❌ Can you tell me what time does it start?
✅ Can you tell me what time it starts?

Use indirect questions to report speech politely and clearly. Remember to change the word order!
