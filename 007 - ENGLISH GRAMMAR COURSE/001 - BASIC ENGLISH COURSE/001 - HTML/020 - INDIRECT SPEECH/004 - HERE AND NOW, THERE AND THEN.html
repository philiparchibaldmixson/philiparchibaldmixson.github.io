🕰️ INDIRECT SPEECH: HERE AND NOW → THERE AND THEN 🕰️

Changing Time and Place in Reported Speech

🌟 What Does It Mean?

When we report speech, we often need to change expressions of time and place. Words like here and now become there and then to reflect the shift in context.

  • Direct: “I’m staying here now.”
  • Indirect: He said he was staying there then.

📋 Common Changes

Direct Speech Indirect Speech
now then
today that day
tonight that night
tomorrow the next day
yesterday the day before
here there
this that
these those

💡 Quick Tip

Always adjust time and place expressions to match the new context of the speaker and listener.

🎯 Common Examples

  • She said, “I’m busy now.” → She said she was busy then.
  • He said, “I’ll come tomorrow.” → He said he would come the next day.
  • They said, “We were here yesterday.” → They said they had been there the day before.
  • She said, “I’ll do it tonight.” → She said she would do it that night.
  • He said, “I like this book.” → He said he liked that book.

📝 More Sentences

• “I’m staying here.” → He said he was staying there.

• “We met yesterday.” → They said they had met the day before.
• “I’ll finish it tomorrow.” → He said he would finish it the next day.

📌 Compare: Direct vs Indirect

Direct Speech Indirect Speech

“I’ll be here tomorrow.” He said he would be there the next day.
“We arrived yesterday.” They said they had arrived the day before.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • She said she was staying there that day.
  • He said he would call me that night.
  • They said they had been there the day before.
  • She said she liked that movie.
  • He said he would arrive the next day.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ She said she was here. (wrong context)
✅ She said she was there.

❌ He said he will come tomorrow.
✅ He said he would come the next day.

❌ They said they met yesterday.
✅ They said they had met the day before.

❌ She said she likes this book.
✅ She said she liked that book.

In indirect speech, “here and now” becomes “there and then”. Adjust time and place to match the new context!
