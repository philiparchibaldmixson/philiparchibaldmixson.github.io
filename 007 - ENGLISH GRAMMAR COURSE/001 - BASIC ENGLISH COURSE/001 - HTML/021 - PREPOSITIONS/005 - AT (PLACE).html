📌 PREPOSITION OF PLACE: AT 📌

Using “at” to describe specific locations

🌟 What Does “AT” Mean?

We use at to talk about specific points or locations. It doesn’t describe the inside or surface of a place — just the position.

  • at the bus stop
  • at the door
  • at school
  • at home
  • at the airport

📋 Structure

Preposition Used For Example
at Specific point or location She’s at the station.
at Events or addresses We met at the party.
at General places He’s at work.

💡 Quick Tip

Use “at” when you refer to a point, not the inside or surface of something.

🎯 Common Examples

  • I’m at the door.
  • She’s at the hospital.
  • We arrived at the airport.
  • He’s at the top of the stairs.
  • They’re at the restaurant.

📝 More Sentences

• I’m waiting at the bus stop.
• She’s at the front desk.
• We met at the concert.
• He’s at home today.
• They’re at school right now.
• The car is parked at the corner.

📌 Compare: IN vs ON vs AT

IN ON AT
She’s in the room. The painting is on the wall. He’s at the door.
We’re in the building. The phone is on the desk. I’m at the office.
They’re in the car. The keys are on the car. We’re at the parking lot.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • She’s at the reception desk.
  • I’m at the entrance.
  • We’re at the cinema.
  • He’s at the top of the hill.
  • They’re at the party.
  • The taxi is waiting at the corner.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I’m in the door.
✅ I’m at the door.

❌ She’s on the hospital.
✅ She’s at the hospital.

❌ We met in the party.
✅ We met at the party.

❌ He’s on home.
✅ He’s at home.

Use “at” to describe specific points or general locations. It helps you express place clearly!
