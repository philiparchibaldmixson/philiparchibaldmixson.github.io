🗣️ SPOKEN GRAMMAR: QUESTION TAGS 🗣️

Confirming, Checking, and Reacting in Conversation

🌟 What Are Question Tags?

Question tags are short questions added to the end of a sentence. In spoken English, they’re used to check information, confirm understanding, or encourage a response.

  • Positive sentence → Negative tag
  • Negative sentence → Positive tag
  • Use the same auxiliary/modal verb as the sentence

📋 Structure

Statement Type Tag Type Example
Positive Negative You’re coming, aren’t you?
Negative Positive She isn’t here, is she?
Present Simple do/does He plays guitar, doesn’t he?
Past Simple did You saw that, didn’t you?
Modal Verbs can/will/would/etc. They can help, can’t they?

💡 Quick Tip

In informal speech, intonation matters: rising for real questions, falling for confirmation.

🎯 Common Examples

  • It’s hot today, isn’t it?
  • You live in Itamaraju, don’t you?
  • She can speak English, can’t she?
  • We’re meeting at 5, aren’t we?
  • They didn’t call, did they?
  • He won the match, didn’t he?
  • You’ve finished, haven’t you?
  • She won’t mind, will she?

📝 More Sentences

• You’re tired, aren’t you?
• He doesn’t eat meat, does he?
• We should leave now, shouldn’t we?
• They weren’t late, were they?
• She’s coming with us, isn’t she?
• You didn’t forget, did you?

📌 Spoken vs Written

Spoken English Written English
Nice weather, isn’t it? It is nice weather today.
You’re coming, aren’t you? He confirmed you are coming.
She didn’t say that, did she? She did not say that.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • You’re ready, aren’t you?
  • He won’t be late, will he?
  • We’ve met before, haven’t we?
  • She doesn’t like coffee, does she?
  • They were here yesterday, weren’t they?
  • You can help me, can’t you?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ You are coming, isn’t you?
✅ You are coming, aren’t you?

❌ She don’t like it, do she?
✅ She doesn’t like it, does she?

❌ They didn’t arrive, didn’t they?
✅ They didn’t arrive, did they?

❌ He can speak, can he?
✅ He can speak, can’t he?

Use question tags to sound natural and interactive in conversation. They help confirm and connect!
