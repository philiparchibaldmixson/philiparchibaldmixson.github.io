🗣️ SPOKEN GRAMMAR: SHORT ANSWERS 🗣️

Answering Clearly and Naturally in Conversation

🌟 What Are Short Answers?

Short answers are used in spoken English to respond quickly and politely to yes/no questions. They follow a clear pattern and sound more natural than just saying “yes” or “no”.

  • Use Yes/No + subject + auxiliary/modal verb.
  • Repeat the verb from the question (do, does, did, is, are, was, were, have, will, etc.).
  • Don’t repeat the full sentence — keep it short!

📋 Structure

Question Type Short Answer (Yes) Short Answer (No)
Present Simple Yes, I do. No, I don’t.
Present Continuous Yes, she is. No, she isn’t.
Past Simple Yes, they did. No, they didn’t.
Present Perfect Yes, we have. No, we haven’t.
Future (will) Yes, he will. No, he won’t.
Modal Verbs Yes, I can. No, I can’t.

💡 Quick Tip

Always match the verb and subject from the question. Don’t add extra words!

🎯 Common Examples

  • Do you like pizza? → Yes, I do.
  • Is she coming? → No, she isn’t.
  • Did they call you? → Yes, they did.
  • Have you finished? → No, I haven’t.
  • Will he be there? → Yes, he will.
  • Can you help me? → No, I can’t.

📝 More Sentences

• Are you ready? → Yes, I am.
• Do they speak English? → No, they don’t.
• Was it expensive? → Yes, it was.
• Have you seen it? → No, I haven’t.
• Will you come? → Yes, I will.
• Should we wait? → No, we shouldn’t.

📌 Spoken vs Written

Spoken English Written English
Yes, I do. I like pizza.
No, she isn’t. She is not coming.
Yes, they did. They called me yesterday.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • Do you study English? → Yes, I do.
  • Did she go to the party? → No, she didn’t.
  • Can they come today? → Yes, they can.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Yes, I am do.
✅ Yes, I do.

❌ No, she not.
✅ No, she isn’t.

❌ Yes, they does.
✅ Yes, they do.

❌ No, I haven’t seen.
✅ No, I haven’t.
