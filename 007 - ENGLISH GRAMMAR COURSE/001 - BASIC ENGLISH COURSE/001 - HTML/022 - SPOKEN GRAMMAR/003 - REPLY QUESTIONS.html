🗣️ SPOKEN GRAMMAR: REPLY QUESTIONS 🗣️

Keeping the Conversation Going

🌟 What Are Reply Questions?

Reply questions are short questions we use in conversation to show interest, react, or keep the conversation going. They repeat the auxiliary or modal verb from the previous sentence.

  • They’re common in spoken English.
  • They often sound like surprise, curiosity, or agreement.
  • They use the same verb as the original sentence.

📋 Structure

Original Statement Reply Question
I love chocolate. Do you?
She’s coming later. Is she?
They didn’t call. Did they?
We’ve finished the project. Have you?
He can speak French. Can he?
She won’t be late. Won’t she?

💡 Quick Tip

Use reply questions to sound engaged and natural. They’re great for showing interest!

🎯 Common Examples

  • I live in Itamaraju. → Do you?
  • She’s a teacher. → Is she?
  • They’ve already left. → Have they?
  • He didn’t like it. → Did he?
  • You can drive. → Can you?
  • We’re going now. → Are you?
  • She won’t come. → Won’t she?

📝 More Sentences

• I’ve seen that movie. → Have you?
• He works at night. → Does he?
• They weren’t invited. → Weren’t they?
• She likes sushi. → Does she?
• We’re meeting later. → Are we?
• I didn’t know that. → Did you?

📌 Spoken vs Written

Spoken English Written English
I love music. Do you? I also enjoy music.
She’s moving to São Paulo. Is she? She plans to relocate to São Paulo.
They’ve finished already. Have they? They have already completed the task.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I’m learning English. → Are you?
  • She got a new job. → Did she?
  • We’ve been there before. → Have you?
  • He doesn’t like coffee. → Does he?
  • You’ll be late. → Will I?
  • They’re coming tomorrow. → Are they?

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I like pizza. Is you?
✅ I like pizza. Do you?

❌ She’s here. Does she?
✅ She’s here. Is she?

❌ They didn’t go. Didn’t they?
✅ They didn’t go. Did they?

❌ He can swim. Can he swim?
✅ He can swim. Can he?

Use reply questions to sound curious, friendly, and natural in conversation. They help you connect!
