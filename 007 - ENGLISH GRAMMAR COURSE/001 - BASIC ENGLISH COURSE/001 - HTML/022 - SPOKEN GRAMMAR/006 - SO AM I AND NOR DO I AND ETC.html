🗣️ SPOKEN GRAMMAR: SO AM I • NEITHER DO I 🗣️

Agreeing Naturally in Conversation

🌟 What Are These Expressions?

We use So / Neither / Nor + auxiliary verb + subject to agree with someone in spoken English. These are short, polite ways to say “Me too” or “Me neither” — but with correct grammar!

  • So + auxiliary + subject → to agree with a positive statement
  • Neither / Nor + auxiliary + subject → to agree with a negative statement
  • Use the same auxiliary/modal verb as the original sentence

📋 Structure

Original Statement Agreement
I’m tired. So am I.
She can swim. So can I.
We’ve finished. So have we.
I don’t like coffee. Neither do I.
He hasn’t arrived. Nor have I.
They won’t come. Neither will we.

💡 Quick Tip

“So” = agree with positive. “Neither” / “Nor” = agree with negative. Always match the verb!

🎯 Common Examples

  • I’m hungry. → So am I.
  • She likes music. → So do I.
  • We’ve been there. → So have we.
  • I don’t eat meat. → Neither do I.
  • He didn’t call. → Nor did I.
  • They won’t stay. → Neither will we.

📝 More Sentences

• I’m excited. → So am I.
• She can speak French. → So can I.
• We’ve finished our homework. → So have we.
• I don’t like horror movies. → Neither do I.
• He hasn’t arrived yet. → Nor have I.
• They won’t join us. → Neither will we.

📌 Spoken vs Written

Spoken Written
I love pizza. So do I. I also love pizza.
I don’t like traffic. Neither do I. I dislike traffic too.
She’s tired. So am I. I am also tired.

🔄 Real-Life Sentences

  • I’m from Bahia. → So am I.
  • He likes soccer. → So do I.
  • We’ve seen that movie. → So have we.
  • I don’t drink soda. → Neither do I.
  • She hasn’t finished. → Nor have I.
  • They won’t travel this year. → Neither will we.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I’m tired. So do I.
✅ So am I.

❌ She can swim. So am I.
✅ So can I.

❌ I don’t like it. So don’t I.
✅ Neither do I.

❌ He hasn’t arrived. Neither I have.
✅ Nor have I.

Use “So + verb + subject” to agree with positive statements, and “Neither/Nor + verb + subject” for negative ones!
