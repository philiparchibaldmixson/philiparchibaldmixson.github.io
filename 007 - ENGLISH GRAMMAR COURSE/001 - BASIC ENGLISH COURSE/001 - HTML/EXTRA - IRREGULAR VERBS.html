📚 Irregular Verbs in English 📚

Master the Most Important Irregular Verbs

🌟 What are Irregular Verbs?

Irregular verbs are verbs that do not follow the regular pattern of adding "-ed" to form the past tense and past participle. Instead, they change in unpredictable ways. While regular verbs are consistent and easy to learn, irregular verbs must be memorized individually because each one has its own unique forms.

There are approximately 200 irregular verbs in English, but the most common 50-100 are used frequently in everyday conversation. Mastering these verbs is essential for speaking and writing English correctly. The three forms you need to know are: Base Form (infinitive), Past Simple, and Past Participle.

Regular vs. Irregular Verbs:

✅ Regular Verb: WALK

Base: walk
Past: walked
Past Participle: walked

All regular verbs add -ed

⚠️ Irregular Verb: GO

Base: go
Past: went
Past Participle: gone

Each irregular verb is unique!

✍️ Using Irregular Verbs in Different Tenses

Understanding when to use each form is crucial for correct English grammar.

1. BASE FORM - Used with:
  • Present Simple (I/you/we/they): I go to work.
  • Infinitive (to + verb): I want to go home.
  • Imperative (commands): Go now!
2. PAST SIMPLE - Used for:
  • Completed actions in the past: She went to the store yesterday.
  • Specific time in the past: They saw a movie last night.
3. PAST PARTICIPLE - Used with:
  • Present Perfect: I have gone to that restaurant.
  • Past Perfect: She had gone before I arrived.
  • Passive Voice: The book was written by a famous author.
