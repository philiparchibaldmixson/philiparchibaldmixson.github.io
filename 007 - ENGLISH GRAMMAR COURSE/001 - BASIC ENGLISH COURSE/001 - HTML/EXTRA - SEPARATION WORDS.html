Master the art of connecting and separating ideas in English

➡️ Transition Words

Words and phrases that help separate and connect different ideas, creating smooth transitions between sentences and paragraphs.
However
Shows contrast between ideas
Furthermore
Adds additional information
Nevertheless
Introduces opposing point
Moreover
Emphasizes additional point
Therefore
Shows logical conclusion
Meanwhile
Indicates simultaneous action

⚡ Contrast & Opposition Words

Words that create separation by showing contrast or opposition between ideas.
Although / Though
Although it rained, we went hiking.
Despite / In spite of
Despite the traffic, we arrived on time.
Whereas / While
She likes tea, whereas he prefers coffee.
On the other hand
It's expensive. On the other hand, it's high quality.
Conversely
He loves summer; conversely, she prefers winter.
Nonetheless
It was risky; nonetheless, they succeeded.

⏰ Time Separation Words

Words that separate events by indicating different time periods or sequences.
Before / After
Before sunrise / After the meeting
Subsequently
Subsequently, new evidence emerged.
Previously
Previously, this was impossible.
Eventually
Eventually, they found the solution.
Immediately
Immediately after, things changed.
Later / Earlier
Later that day / Earlier this morning

🔗 Coordinating Conjunctions

Simple conjunctions that separate and connect independent clauses (remember FANBOYS).
For
I stayed home, for I was tired.
And
I bought bread and milk.
Nor
I don't like it, nor do I want it.
But
It's raining, but I'll go anyway.
Or
Coffee or tea?
Yet
Small, yet powerful.
So
I was hungry, so I ate.

📝 Putting It All Together

Without separation words: I woke up late. I missed the bus. I was late for work. I had an important meeting.
With separation words: I woke up late. Therefore, I missed the bus and was late for work. However, despite this setback, I made it to the important meeting eventually.
