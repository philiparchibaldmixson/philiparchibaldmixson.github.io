🧠 Basic Verb Structures – DO/DID as Auxiliary

We use do, does, and did as auxiliary verbs to form questions and negatives in the present and past simple tenses. They help us build correct grammar when the main verb is not “be.”

✅ Structure

  • Do/Does + subject + base verb → Present Simple Question
  • Did + subject + base verb → Past Simple Question
  • Subject + do/does/did + not + base verb → Negative Sentence

✅ Examples

Examples:
Present Simple – Question:
Do you like pizza?
Present Simple – Negative:
She does not play football.
Past Simple – Question:
Did they visit the museum?
Past Simple – Negative:
He did not go to school yesterday.

  • __________ he speak English? (does)

  • (Does / Did) he call you yesterday?
