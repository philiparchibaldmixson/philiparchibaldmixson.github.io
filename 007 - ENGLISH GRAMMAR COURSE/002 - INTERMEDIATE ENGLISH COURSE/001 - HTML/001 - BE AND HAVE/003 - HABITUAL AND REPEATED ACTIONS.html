🔁 BE and HAVE – Habitual and Repeated Actions

In English, we often use BE and HAVE in the present simple tense to describe habits, routines, and repeated actions. These verbs help us talk about things that happen regularly or are generally true.

🧍 Using BE for Habits and Routines

The verb BE is used to describe someone's usual state, job, or role. It is also used with adjectives to describe regular feelings or conditions.

Examples:
Routine:
I am always tired in the morning.
Job:
She is a nurse. He is a teacher.
Personality:
They are very friendly.

👜 Using HAVE for Habits and Routines

The verb HAVE is used to describe possessions, relationships, and regular activities like meals, meetings, or responsibilities.

Examples:
Daily Activity:
We have lunch at 12:30 every day.
Responsibility:
He has a lot of homework every week.
Possession:
I have a meeting every Monday.
