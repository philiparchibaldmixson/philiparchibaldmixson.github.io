🍽️ HAVE for Actions

The verb HAVE is not only used for possession—it’s also used to describe common actions and experiences. These include meals, conversations, showers, meetings, and more. It’s a very useful verb in everyday English!

🧠 Common Action Phrases with HAVE

We use HAVE with many nouns to describe actions. These combinations are called collocations. They are fixed expressions that native speakers use naturally.

Examples:
Meals:
I have breakfast at 7 a.m. / We have dinner together.
Conversations:
Let’s have a talk. / They have a meeting every Monday.
Health & Hygiene:
She has a shower before work. / He has a headache.
Fun & Experiences:
We have a great time at the beach. / I have fun with my friends.
