🔄 Present Progressive for Changes

The Present Progressive tense (also called Present Continuous) is used to describe actions happening now. But it’s also used to talk about changes, developments, and trends—things that are in progress or evolving over time.

📈 Structure

Subject + BE + Verb-ing

Examples: I am learning, She is changing, They are improving

Examples of Changes:
Personal Growth:
I am becoming more confident every day.
Trends:
More people are using electric cars.
Technology:
Artificial intelligence is changing the way we work.
Environment:
The climate is getting warmer.
