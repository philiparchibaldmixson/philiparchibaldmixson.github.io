📝 Simple Present – Instructions, Routines & Facts

The Simple Present tense is used to describe instructions, routines, habits, and general truths. It is one of the most common tenses in English and is used in everyday communication.

🔧 Instructions and Directions

We use the simple present to give instructions, directions, or commands. These are often found in manuals, recipes, and guides.

Examples:
Instructions:
Turn on the computer. Click the icon. Enter your password.
Recipes:
Add the eggs. Mix the flour. Bake for 20 minutes.

🔁 Habits and Routines

We use the simple present to talk about things that happen regularly or repeatedly.

Examples:
Daily Routine:
She wakes up at 6 a.m. / I go to work every day.
Habits:
He drinks coffee every morning. / We walk to school.

🌍 General Truths and Facts

The simple present is also used to express facts that are always true or scientific truths.

Examples:
Facts:
Water boils at 100°C. / The sun rises in the east.
Universal Truths:
People need air to breathe. / Birds fly.
