🧠 Present Tenses – Non-Progressive Verbs

Some verbs are not usually used in the progressive (continuous) form. These are called non-progressive verbs or stative verbs. They describe states, feelings, thoughts, and possessions—not actions in progress.

📌 Common Non-Progressive Verbs

These verbs are typically used in the simple present tense, even when talking about something happening now.

Examples:
Mental States:
I know the answer. / She believes in magic.
Emotions:
He loves chocolate. / We hate waiting.
Possession:
They have a new car. / I own a laptop.
Senses:
This cake tastes delicious. / The flower smells nice.

🚫 Avoid Using ING with These Verbs

We do not usually say: I am knowing, She is loving, They are having (unless it's an action like "having lunch").
