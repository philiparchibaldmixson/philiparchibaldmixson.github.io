🔮 Future with WILL – Decisions, Promises & More

The modal verb WILL is used to talk about the future. It expresses spontaneous decisions, promises, offers, and predictions. It is one of the most common ways to speak about future actions in English.

🧠 Structure

Subject + will + base verb

Examples: I will help, She will call, They will arrive

Examples of Usage:
Decision:
I will take the bus. (decided now)
Promise:
I will always support you.
Offer:
I will help you with your homework.
Prediction:
It will rain tomorrow.
