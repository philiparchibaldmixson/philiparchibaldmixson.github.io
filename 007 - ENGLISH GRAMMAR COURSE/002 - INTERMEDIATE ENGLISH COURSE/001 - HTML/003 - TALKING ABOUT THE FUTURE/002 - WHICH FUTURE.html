⏳ Future Forms – Which Future?

In English, we use different forms to talk about the future depending on the situation. The three most common are:

🔮 WILL – Decisions, Promises, Predictions

WILL is used for spontaneous decisions, promises, offers, and predictions.

Examples:
Decision:
I will call you later.
Promise:
I will help you with your homework.
Prediction:
It will rain tomorrow.

📌 GOING TO – Plans and Intentions

GOING TO is used when we already decided or planned something before speaking.

Examples:
Plan:
We are going to visit grandma next weekend.
Intention:
She is going to study medicine.
Prediction (based on evidence):
Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.

📅 PRESENT PROGRESSIVE – Arrangements

PRESENT PROGRESSIVE is used for future arrangements that are already scheduled.

Examples:
Arrangement:
I am meeting my friend at 5 p.m.
Scheduled Plan:
They are flying to Paris tomorrow.
