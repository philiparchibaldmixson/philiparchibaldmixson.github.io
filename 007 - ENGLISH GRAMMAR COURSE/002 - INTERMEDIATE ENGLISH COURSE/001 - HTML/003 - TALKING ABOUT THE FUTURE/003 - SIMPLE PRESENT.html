📅 Future with Simple Present

The Simple Present tense is usually used to describe routines and facts. But it can also be used to talk about the future—especially for scheduled events, timetables, and fixed programs.

🕒 When to Use Simple Present for Future

We use the simple present for future actions when they are part of a fixed schedule or official timetable, such as transportation, school, or public events.

Examples:
Transport:
The train leaves at 6:00 a.m. tomorrow.
School:
Class starts at 8:30 a.m. on Monday.
Events:
The movie begins at 7 p.m. tonight.
Appointments:
My flight departs at noon.
