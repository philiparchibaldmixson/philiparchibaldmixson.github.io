⏳ Future Progressive – Actions in Progress

The Future Progressive tense (also called Future Continuous) is used to describe actions that will be in progress at a specific time in the future. It emphasizes the duration or ongoing nature of the activity.

🧠 Structure

Subject + will be + verb-ing

Examples: I will be working, She will be traveling, They will be studying

Examples of Future Progressive:
Specific Time:
At 8 p.m., I will be watching a movie.
Ongoing Action:
She will be working all afternoon.
Parallel Actions:
While you study, I will be cooking dinner.
Polite Question:
Will you be joining us for lunch?
