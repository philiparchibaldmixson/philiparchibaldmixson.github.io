📘 Future – BE + Infinitive

The structure BE + infinitive (e.g., "is to leave", "are to meet") is used to talk about official plans, formal arrangements, and instructions. It is common in news reports, announcements, and formal writing.

🧠 Structure

Subject + BE (am/is/are) + base verb

Examples: She is to attend the meeting. / We are to leave at 6 a.m.

Examples of BE + Infinitive:
Formal Plan:
The president is to visit Brazil next month.
Instruction:
You are to report to the office at 9 a.m.
Official Schedule:
The train is to depart at 7:30 sharp.
Expectation:
All students are to submit their forms by Friday.
