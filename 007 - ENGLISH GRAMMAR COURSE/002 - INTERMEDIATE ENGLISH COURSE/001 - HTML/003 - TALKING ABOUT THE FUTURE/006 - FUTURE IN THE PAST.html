🕰️ Future in the Past

Future in the Past is used to talk about something that was expected to happen after a certain point in the past. It expresses a future action from a past perspective.

🧠 Structure

Subject + was/were going to + base verb
Subject + would + base verb

Examples: I was going to call you. / She would travel to Spain.

Examples of Future in the Past:
Plan:
We were going to visit the museum, but it was closed.
Prediction:
I thought it would rain, but it didn’t.
Expectation:
She was going to start a new job last month.
Reported Speech:
He said he would help us with the project.

  • (I was going to / I will) call you yesterday.
  • (She would / She will) help us with the project.
  • (They were going to / They are going to) arrive at 8 p.m.
  • (We thought it would / We think it will) rain.
  • (He said he would / He says he will) come later.
