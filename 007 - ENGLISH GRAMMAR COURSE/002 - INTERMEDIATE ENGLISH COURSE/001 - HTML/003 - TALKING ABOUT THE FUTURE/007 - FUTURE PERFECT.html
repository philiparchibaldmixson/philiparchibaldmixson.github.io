⏱️ Future Perfect – Completed Actions in the Future

The Future Perfect tense is used to describe actions that will be completed before a specific time in the future. It helps us talk about what will have happened by a certain deadline or moment.

🧠 Structure

Subject + will have + past participle

Examples: I will have finished the report by 5 p.m. / They will have arrived before lunch.

Examples of Future Perfect:
Deadline:
By next week, we will have completed the project.
Expectation:
She will have graduated by June.
Travel:
They will have landed by the time we arrive.
Routine:
He will have eaten dinner before the meeting starts.
