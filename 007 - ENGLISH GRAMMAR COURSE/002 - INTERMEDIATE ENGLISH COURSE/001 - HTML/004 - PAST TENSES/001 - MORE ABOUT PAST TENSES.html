📚 More About Past Tenses

English has several past tenses that help us describe actions and situations that happened before now. Each tense has a specific use and structure. Let’s explore the four main past tenses:

✅ Past Simple

Used for completed actions in the past.

Example:
I visited my grandmother yesterday.

🔄 Past Continuous

Used for actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

Example:
She was reading a book when I arrived.

⏮️ Past Perfect

Used to show that one past action happened before another past action.

Example:
They had left before the party started.

⏳ Past Perfect Continuous

Used to show that a past action was ongoing before another past action.

Example:
I had been studying for hours before the exam began.
