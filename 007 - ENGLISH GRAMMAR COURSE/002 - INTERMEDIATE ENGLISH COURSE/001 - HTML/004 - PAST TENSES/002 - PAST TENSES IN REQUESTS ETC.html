🙏 Past Tenses in Requests and Politeness

In English, we often use past tenses to sound more polite, especially in requests, offers, and suggestions. This doesn’t mean the action happened in the past—it’s just a way to be more formal or respectful.

🧠 Common Structures

We use Past Simple and Past Continuous in polite expressions:

  • Could you…? → Could you help me?
  • Would you…? → Would you mind closing the door?
  • I was wondering if… → I was wondering if you could explain this.
  • Did you want…? → Did you want some coffee?
  • Were you hoping…? → Were you hoping to join us?
Examples of Polite Past Tense:
Request:
Could you help me with this task?
Offer:
Did you want some tea?
Suggestion:
Would you like to join us?
Polite Inquiry:
I was wondering if you had a moment.
