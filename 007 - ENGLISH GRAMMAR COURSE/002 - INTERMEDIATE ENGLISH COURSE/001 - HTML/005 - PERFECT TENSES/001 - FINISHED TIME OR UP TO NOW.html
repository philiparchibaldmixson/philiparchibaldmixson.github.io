⏳ Perfect Tenses – Finished Time or Up to Now?

Perfect tenses help us connect the past with the present. But how do we know when to use the Present Perfect or the Past Simple? It depends on whether the time is finished or still connected to now.

📌 Finished Time → Past Simple

Use the Past Simple when the time is finished (yesterday, last year, in 2010, etc.).

Example:
I visited Paris in 2019.

📌 Up to Now → Present Perfect

Use the Present Perfect when the time is not finished or when the exact time is not important.

Example:
I have visited Paris three times.
More Examples:
Past Simple:
She graduated in 2020. (finished time)
Present Perfect:
She has graduated. (no time mentioned, result is important now)

  • She called me an hour ago. → (Finished / Up to now)
  • They’ve worked here for ten years. → (Finished / Up to now)
