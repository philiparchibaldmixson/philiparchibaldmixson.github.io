🗞️ Perfect Tenses – News and Details

In news reporting, we often use the Present Perfect to announce recent events that are relevant now. Then we use the Past Simple to give specific details about what happened.

🧠 Structure

Present Perfect: Subject + has/have + past participle
Past Simple: Subject + verb in past form

Examples:
Headline (Present Perfect):
The police have arrested two suspects.
Details (Past Simple):
They found stolen items in the suspects' car.
Headline:
Scientists have discovered a new planet.
Details:
They used advanced telescopes to observe it last month.
