📘 Perfect Tenses – Simple Past vs Present Perfect

Both Simple Past and Present Perfect describe actions that happened in the past. But they are used in different contexts:

✅ Simple Past

Used for actions that happened at a specific time in the past. The time is finished.

Structure: Subject + verb in past form

Example:
I visited Rio last year.

🔄 Present Perfect

Used for actions that happened at an unspecified time or that are still relevant now. The time is not finished.

Structure: Subject + has/have + past participle

Example:
I have visited Rio several times.
Quick Comparison:
Simple Past:
She graduated in 2020.
Present Perfect:
She has graduated. (no time mentioned)
