⏮️ Perfect Tenses – More About the Past Perfect

The Past Perfect tense is used to show that one action happened before another action in the past. It helps us create a clear timeline of events and is often used with time expressions like before, after, by the time, and when.

🧠 Structure

Subject + had + past participle

Examples: I had finished dinner before they arrived. / She had left when I got there.

Examples of Past Perfect:
Sequence of Events:
They had already started the meeting when we arrived.
Cause and Effect:
He had forgotten his keys, so he couldn’t open the door.
Reported Experience:
I had never seen snow before that day.
Time Reference:
By the time she arrived, we had finished eating.
