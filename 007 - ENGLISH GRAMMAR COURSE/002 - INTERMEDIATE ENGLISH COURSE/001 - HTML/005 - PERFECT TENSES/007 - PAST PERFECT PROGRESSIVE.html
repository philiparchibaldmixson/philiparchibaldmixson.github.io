⏳ Perfect Tenses – Past Perfect Progressive

The Past Perfect Progressive tense is used to describe an action that was ongoing in the past and continued up to another point in the past. It emphasizes the duration or continuity of the activity.

🧠 Structure

Subject + had been + verb-ing

Examples: I had been studying for hours before the exam started. / They had been waiting for the bus when it finally arrived.

Examples of Past Perfect Progressive:
Duration:
She had been working at the company for five years before she quit.
Cause and Effect:
He had been running, so he was out of breath.
Interrupted Action:
We had been talking when the phone rang.
Background Activity:
I had been reading all afternoon before I took a break.
