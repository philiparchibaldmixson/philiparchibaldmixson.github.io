✨ Perfect Tenses – This Is the First Time

We use the phrase “This is the first time…” with the Present Perfect to describe experiences that are happening for the first time in someone’s life. It emphasizes that the action has never happened before now.

🧠 Structure

This is the first time + subject + has/have + past participle

Examples: This is the first time I have eaten sushi. / This is the first time she has traveled abroad.

Examples of First-Time Experiences:
Personal Experience:
This is the first time I have driven a car.
Travel:
This is the first time we have visited New York.
Food:
This is the first time he has tried Indian food.
Learning:
This is the first time she has studied French.
