🗣️ Modal Verbs – Had Better

Had better is used to give strong advice or warnings. It suggests that something is a good idea or necessary, and if not followed, there may be negative consequences.

🧠 Structure

Subject + had better + base verb

Examples: You had better study for the test. / We had better leave now.

Examples of Had Better:
Advice:
You had better take an umbrella. It might rain.
Warning:
He had better not be late again. The boss is angry.
Urgency:
We had better hurry or we’ll miss the train.
Negative Form:
You had better not forget her birthday.
