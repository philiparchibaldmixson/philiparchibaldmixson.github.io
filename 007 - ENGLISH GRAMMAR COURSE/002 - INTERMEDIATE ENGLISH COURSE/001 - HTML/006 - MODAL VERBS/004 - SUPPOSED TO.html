📌 Modal Verbs – Supposed To

Supposed to is used to talk about expectations, duties, or scheduled actions. It expresses what is expected or intended, even if it doesn’t actually happen.

🧠 Structure

Subject + be (am/is/are/was/were) + supposed to + base verb

Examples: I am supposed to meet her at 5. / They were supposed to call yesterday.

Examples of Supposed To:
Expectation:
You are supposed to wear a uniform at school.
Schedule:
The train is supposed to arrive at 8 a.m.
Obligation:
We are supposed to submit the form by Friday.
Past Expectation:
He was supposed to help us, but he didn’t show up.
