🔍 Modal Verbs – Must and Can't: Certainty

We use must and can't to express strong beliefs or logical conclusions about the present or past. These modal verbs show how certain we are about something.

✅ Must (Positive Certainty)

Must is used when we are sure something is true.

Structure: Subject + must + base verb

Example:
She must be tired. She’s been working all day.

❌ Can't (Negative Certainty)

Can't is used when we are sure something is not true.

Structure: Subject + can't + base verb

Example:
He can't be at home. I just saw him downtown.
More Examples:
Must:
This must be the right address. Everyone is here.
Can't:
You can't be serious. That’s impossible!
Must (Past):
She must have forgotten the meeting.
Can't (Past):
He can't have seen the message. He didn’t reply.
