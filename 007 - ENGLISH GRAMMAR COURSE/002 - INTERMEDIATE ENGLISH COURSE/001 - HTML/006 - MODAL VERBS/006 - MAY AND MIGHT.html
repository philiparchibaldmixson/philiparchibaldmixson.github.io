🌦️ Modal Verbs – May and Might

May and might are modal verbs used to express possibility. They are often interchangeable, but might can suggest a slightly lower probability or be more tentative.

🧠 Structure

Subject + may/might + base verb

Examples: It may rain later. / She might come to the party.

Examples of May and Might:
Possibility:
We may go to the beach tomorrow.
Tentative Suggestion:
He might join us later.
Uncertainty:
They may not know about the meeting.
Negative Form:
She might not come if it rains.
