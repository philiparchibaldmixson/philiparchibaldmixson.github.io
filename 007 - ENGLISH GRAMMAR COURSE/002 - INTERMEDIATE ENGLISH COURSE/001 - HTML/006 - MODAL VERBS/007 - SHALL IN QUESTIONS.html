🗨️ Modal Verbs – Shall in Questions

Shall is a modal verb used in questions to offer help, make suggestions, or ask for advice. It is more common in British English and sounds formal or polite.

🧠 Structure

Shall + subject + base verb

Examples: Shall we go now? / Shall I open the window?

Examples of Shall in Questions:
Offering Help:
Shall I carry your bag?
Making Suggestions:
Shall we take a break?
Asking for Advice:
What shall I do next?
Polite Offer:
Shall I call a taxi for you?
