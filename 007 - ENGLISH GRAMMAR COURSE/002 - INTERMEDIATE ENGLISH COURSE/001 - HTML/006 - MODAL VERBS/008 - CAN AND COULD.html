🧠 Modal Verbs – Can and Could (Ability)

Can and could are modal verbs used to express ability. We use them to talk about what someone is able to do in the present, past, or future.

✅ Can – Present and Future Ability

Can is used to express ability in the present or general future.

Structure: Subject + can + base verb

Present:
I can swim very well.
Future:
We can meet tomorrow if you’re free.

⏪ Could – Past Ability or Future Possibility

Could is used to express ability in the past or polite possibility in the future.

Structure: Subject + could + base verb

Past:
She could play the piano when she was five.
Future Possibility:
I could help you later if you need.
