🔁 Modal Verbs – Will and Would: Typical Behaviour

Will and would can be used to describe typical behavior or habits. These modal verbs help us talk about what people usually do or used to do in specific situations.

✅ Will – Present Typical Behaviour

Will is used to describe what someone usually does in the present or future.

Structure: Subject + will + base verb

Present Habit:
He will always arrive early to meetings.
Typical Reaction:
She will complain if the food is cold.

⏪ Would – Past Typical Behaviour

Would is used to describe what someone used to do regularly in the past.

Structure: Subject + would + base verb

Past Habit:
When we were kids, we would play outside until dark.
Repeated Action:
My grandfather would tell us stories every night.
