🧩 Perfect Modal Verbs – Should Have

Should have + past participle is used to express regret, criticism, or missed obligation about a past action. It means that something was expected or recommended, but it didn’t happen.

🧠 Structure

Subject + should have + past participle

Examples: I should have studied more. / They should have called earlier.

Examples of Should Have:
Regret:
I should have gone to the party. It looked fun.
Criticism:
You should have told me the truth.
Advice (in hindsight):
He should have taken the earlier train.
Missed Obligation:
We should have submitted the report yesterday.
