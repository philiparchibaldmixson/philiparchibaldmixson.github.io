🔍 Perfect Modal Verbs – May Have and Must Have

May have and must have are perfect modal verbs used to express possibility and certainty about past actions. They help us make logical guesses or assumptions about what happened.

🧠 Structure

Subject + may/must have + past participle

Examples: She may have forgotten the meeting. / He must have left already.

Examples of May Have and Must Have:
Possibility:
They may have missed the bus.
Certainty:
You must have seen the email. It was sent to everyone.
Guessing:
He may have gone to the store.
Logical Conclusion:
She must have studied hard. She got top marks.
