🔍 Perfect Modal Verbs – Could Have & Needn’t Have

Could have and needn’t have are perfect modal verbs used to talk about past possibilities and unnecessary actions. They help us reflect on what was possible or what wasn’t needed.

✅ Could Have – Past Possibility

Could have is used to express that something was possible in the past, even if it didn’t happen.

Structure: Subject + could have + past participle

Example:
She could have won the race, but she fell.

✅ Needn’t Have – Unnecessary Action

Needn’t have is used when someone did something, but it wasn’t necessary.

Structure: Subject + needn’t have + past participle

Example:
You needn’t have bought more milk. We already had plenty.
More Examples:
Could Have:
I could have gone to the concert, but I was tired.
Needn’t Have:
He needn’t have apologized. It wasn’t his fault.
