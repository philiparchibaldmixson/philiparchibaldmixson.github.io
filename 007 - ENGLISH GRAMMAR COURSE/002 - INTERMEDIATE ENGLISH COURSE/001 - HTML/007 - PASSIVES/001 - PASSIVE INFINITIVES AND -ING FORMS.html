🧠 Passive Infinitives and -ing Forms

Passive forms can be used with infinitives and -ing forms to focus on the action or the receiver of the action, rather than the doer.

✅ Passive Infinitives

Structure: to be + past participle

Used when something is expected, planned, or intended to be done.

Example:
The documents are to be signed by the manager.

✅ Passive -ing Forms

Structure: being + past participle

Used when describing ongoing actions in the passive voice.

Example:
She dislikes being interrupted during meetings.
More Examples:
Passive Infinitive:
The files need to be reviewed before Friday.
Passive -ing Form:
He enjoys being praised for his work.
