🔗 Passives – Prepositions with Passives

Some verbs are followed by prepositions, and when we use them in the passive voice, the preposition must still be included. These structures are common in English and are used to describe actions where the focus is on the receiver of the action.

✅ Common Verbs with Prepositions

Examples include: talk about, listen to, look at, laugh at, comment on, rely on, deal with, refer to, agree with, think about.

🧠 Structure

Active: Subject + verb + preposition + object

Passive: Object + be + past participle + preposition

Examples:
Active:
People talked about the issue.
Passive:
The issue was talked about by many people.
Passive:
The proposal was commented on by the committee.
Passive:
She was laughed at by her classmates.
