❓ Questions and Negatives – Question-word Subjects

In English, when the question word is the subject of the sentence, we do not use auxiliary verbs like do/does/did. The word order is the same as in affirmative sentences.

✅ Structure

Question-word (subject) + verb + object

Examples: Who called you? / What happened next?

Examples of Question-word Subjects:
Present Simple:
Who knows the answer?
Past Simple:
What caused the problem?
Present Continuous:
Who is making that noise?
Past Perfect:
Who had broken the window?
