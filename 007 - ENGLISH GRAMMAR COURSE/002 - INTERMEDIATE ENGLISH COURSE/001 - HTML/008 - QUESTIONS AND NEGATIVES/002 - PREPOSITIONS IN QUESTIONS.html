🔎 Questions and Negatives – Prepositions in Questions

When forming questions with verbs or expressions that require prepositions, the preposition can appear at the end of the question or before the question word. In spoken and informal English, it’s more common to place the preposition at the end.

✅ Structure

Informal: Question word + auxiliary + subject + verb + preposition

Formal: Preposition + question word + auxiliary + subject + verb

Examples of Prepositions in Questions:
Informal:
Who are you talking to?
Formal:
To whom are you talking?
Informal:
What are you looking at?
Formal:
At what are you looking?
