🧠 Infinitives and -ing Forms – More About Infinitives

Infinitives can express actions in general (to sit) or ongoing actions (to be sitting). These forms help us describe time, intention, and continuity.

✅ Basic Infinitive

to + base verb (e.g., to sit, to read, to work)

Used to express general or future actions.

Example:
He wants to sit near the window.

✅ Continuous Infinitive

to be + -ing form (e.g., to be sitting, to be reading)

Used to emphasize the action is/was in progress.

Example:
She seems to be sitting alone.
More Examples:
General Action:
They hope to travel next year.
Ongoing Action:
He claims to be working on the project.
