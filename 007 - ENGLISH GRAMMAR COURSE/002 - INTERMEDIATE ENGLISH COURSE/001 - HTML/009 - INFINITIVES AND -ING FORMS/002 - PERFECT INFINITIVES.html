⏳ Infinitives and -ing Forms – Perfect Infinitives

Perfect infinitives are used to refer to actions that happened before another action or to express unreal past situations, especially with modal verbs.

✅ Structure

to have + past participle (e.g., to have gone, to have seen, to have finished)

Examples of Perfect Infinitives:
With modal verbs:
You should have called earlier.
Unreal past:
He pretended to have forgotten the meeting.
Past reference:
She seems to have left already.
Regret or criticism:
They ought to have helped us.
