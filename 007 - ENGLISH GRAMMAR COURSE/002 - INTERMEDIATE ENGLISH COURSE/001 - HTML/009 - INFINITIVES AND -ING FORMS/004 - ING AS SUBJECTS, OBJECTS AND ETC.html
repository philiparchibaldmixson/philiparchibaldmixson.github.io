🌀 Infinitives and -ing Forms – -ing Forms as Subjects and Objects

The -ing form (also called a gerund) can be used as a subject or an object in a sentence. This is common in English when we talk about activities or general actions.

✅ -ing Form as Subject

When the -ing form comes at the beginning of a sentence, it acts as the subject.

Example:
Swimming is good for your health.

✅ -ing Form as Object

Many verbs are followed by an -ing form as their object.

Common verbs: enjoy, avoid, finish, suggest, admit, consider

Example:
She enjoys reading before bed.

✅ -ing Form After Prepositions

When a verb follows a preposition, it usually takes the -ing form.

Example:
He’s interested in learning Spanish.
More Examples:
Subject:
Jogging every morning keeps me fit.
Object:
They suggested taking a break.
After preposition:
I’m tired of waiting.
