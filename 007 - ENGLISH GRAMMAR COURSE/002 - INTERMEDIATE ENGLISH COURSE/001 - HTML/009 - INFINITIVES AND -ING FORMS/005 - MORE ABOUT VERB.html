🔄 Infinitives and -ing Forms – Verb + Infinitive or -ing

Some verbs are followed by an infinitive (to + base verb), some by an -ing form (verb + -ing), and some can take either with a change in meaning.

✅ Verb + Infinitive

Used to express intention, decision, or future action.

Common verbs: want, decide, hope, plan, agree

Example:
She decided to leave early.

✅ Verb + -ing Form

Used to express enjoyment, avoidance, or general activity.

Common verbs: enjoy, avoid, suggest, admit, finish

Example:
He enjoys reading novels.

✅ Verb + Infinitive or -ing (Change in Meaning)

Some verbs can take both forms, but the meaning changes.

  • Remember to do = don’t forget to do it
  • Remember doing = recall a past action
  • Stop to do = pause in order to do something
  • Stop doing = quit the activity
More Examples:
Infinitive:
They hope to win the match.
-ing form:
She suggested taking a break.
Change in meaning:
I stopped smoking. / I stopped to smoke.
