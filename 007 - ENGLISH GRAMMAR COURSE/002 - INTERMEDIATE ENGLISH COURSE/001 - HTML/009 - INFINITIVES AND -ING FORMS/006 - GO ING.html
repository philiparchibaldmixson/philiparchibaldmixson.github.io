🛍️ Infinitives and -ing Forms – Go + -ing (She’s gone shopping)

The structure go + -ing is used to talk about leisure activities, especially sports and hobbies. When we say “She’s gone shopping”, we mean she left to do the activity and is still doing it.

✅ Structure

go + -ing form → used with activities like swimming, shopping, jogging, dancing, etc.

have/has gone + -ing → used to show someone has left to do the activity and is still away.

✅ Common Expressions

  • She’s gone shopping. (She is shopping now.)
  • He’s gone jogging. (He is jogging now.)
  • They’ve gone swimming. (They are swimming now.)
  • We went hiking last weekend. (Past activity)
Examples:
Present Perfect:
She’s gone shopping. She’ll be back soon.
Past Simple:
We went hiking in the mountains.
Present Continuous:
He is going jogging right now.
Future:
They’re going to go swimming tomorrow.
