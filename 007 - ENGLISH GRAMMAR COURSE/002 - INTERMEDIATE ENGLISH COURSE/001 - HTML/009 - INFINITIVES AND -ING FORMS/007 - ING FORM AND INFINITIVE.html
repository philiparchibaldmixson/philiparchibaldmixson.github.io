🔁 Infinitives and -ing Forms – Both Possible

Some verbs can be followed by either the infinitive (to + base verb) or the -ing form, with little or no change in meaning. However, in some cases, the meaning may change slightly depending on the form used.

✅ Verbs That Accept Both Forms

  • begin
  • start
  • continue
  • like
  • love
  • hate
  • prefer

Examples: She started to cry. / She started crying.

✅ Meaning Differences (Sometimes)

  • like to do = think it’s a good idea
  • like doing = enjoy the activity
  • prefer to do = choice in a specific situation
  • prefer doing = general preference
Examples:
Infinitive:
I like to read before bed.
-ing form:
I like reading before bed.
Infinitive:
She prefers to walk to work.
-ing form:
She prefers walking to work.
