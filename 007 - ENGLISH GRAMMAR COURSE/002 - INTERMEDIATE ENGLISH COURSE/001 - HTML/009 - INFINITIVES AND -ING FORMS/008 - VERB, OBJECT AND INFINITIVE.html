🎯 Infinitives and -ing Forms – Verb + Object + Infinitive

Some verbs are followed by an object and then an infinitive (to + base verb). This structure is used when one person causes or expects another person to do something.

✅ Structure

Verb + object + to + base verb

Examples: She told him to wait. / They asked us to help.

✅ Common Verbs

  • ask
  • tell
  • want
  • advise
  • expect
  • invite
  • encourage
  • persuade
  • remind
  • force
Examples:
Present Simple:
I want you to come with me.
Past Simple:
She told him to leave early.
Future:
They will ask us to join the meeting.
Modal Verb:
You should remind her to call.
