😊 Infinitives and -ing Forms – Adjective + Infinitive or -ing

We often use adjectives followed by an infinitive (to + base verb) or an -ing form (verb + -ing) to express feelings, reactions, or opinions. The choice depends on the adjective and the meaning.

✅ Adjective + Infinitive

Used to describe reactions or opinions about actions or situations.

Examples: happy to help, surprised to hear, difficult to understand

✅ Adjective + -ing Form

Used when the adjective describes the subject doing the action.

Examples: busy studying, worth doing, afraid of flying

Examples:
Infinitive:
She was happy to see us.
Infinitive:
It’s easy to learn this app.
-ing Form:
He’s busy preparing for the test.
-ing Form:
This book is worth reading.
