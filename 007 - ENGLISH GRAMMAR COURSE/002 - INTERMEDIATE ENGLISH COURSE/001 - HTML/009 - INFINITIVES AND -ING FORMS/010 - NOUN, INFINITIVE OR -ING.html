📚 Infinitives and -ing Forms – Noun + Infinitive or -ing

Some nouns are commonly followed by an infinitive (to + base verb) or an -ing form (verb + -ing). The choice depends on the meaning and structure of the sentence.

✅ Noun + Infinitive

Used when the noun refers to a specific action or goal.

Examples: decision to leave, plan to travel, opportunity to learn

✅ Noun + -ing Form

Used when the noun refers to a general activity or process.

Examples: habit of smoking, difficulty in understanding, interest in learning

Examples:
Infinitive:
She made a decision to study abroad.
Infinitive:
We had an opportunity to meet the author.
-ing Form:
He has a habit of talking too much.
-ing Form:
They showed great interest in learning
