👤 Infinitives and -ing Forms – More About Noun/Pronoun + Infinitive

We often use a noun or pronoun + infinitive structure to show who is expected, allowed, or required to do something. This pattern is common after certain verbs, adjectives, and nouns.

✅ Structure

noun/pronoun + to + base verb

Examples: I want him to help. / It’s important for you to listen.

✅ Common Uses

  • After verbs: want, expect, ask, tell, advise
  • After adjectives: important, necessary, easy, difficult
  • After nouns: plan, decision, opportunity, request
Examples:
Verb + pronoun + infinitive:
She asked me to wait.
Adjective + for + noun + infinitive:
It’s important for students to study.
Noun + for + pronoun + infinitive:
There’s a chance for us to win.
Modal + pronoun + infinitive:
You should tell him to call.
