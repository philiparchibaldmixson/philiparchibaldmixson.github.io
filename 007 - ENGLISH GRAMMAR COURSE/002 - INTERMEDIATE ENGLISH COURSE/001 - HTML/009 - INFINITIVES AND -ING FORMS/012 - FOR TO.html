⏰ Infinitives and -ing Forms – For ... to ...

The structure for + noun/pronoun + to + base verb is used to show who is supposed to do something. It often follows adjectives, nouns, or expressions like “It’s time,” “It’s important,” or “It’s necessary.”

✅ Structure

for + noun/pronoun + to + verb

Examples: It’s time for the postman to come. / It’s important for you to listen.

✅ Common Uses

  • It’s time for someone to do something
  • It’s important/necessary for someone to do something
  • A good idea for someone to do something
  • Too early/late for someone to do something
Examples:
Time expression:
It’s time for the children to go to bed.
Importance:
It’s important for you to be honest.
Opportunity:
This is a chance for us to improve.
Too early:
It’s too early for them to leave.
