🌟 Infinitives and -ing Forms – More About Adjective + Infinitive

We often use adjective + infinitive to express feelings, opinions, or evaluations about actions. This structure helps describe how someone feels about doing something or how easy, difficult, or important an action is.

✅ Structure

adjective + to + base verb

Examples: happy to help, difficult to understand, ready to start

✅ Common Adjectives Used with Infinitives

  • happy, glad, pleased, excited
  • easy, hard, difficult, impossible
  • ready, willing, afraid, reluctant
  • important, necessary, helpful
Examples:
Feeling:
She was happy to see her friends again.
Evaluation:
This app is easy to use.
Willingness:
He’s ready to begin the project.
Necessity:
It’s important to follow the instructions.
