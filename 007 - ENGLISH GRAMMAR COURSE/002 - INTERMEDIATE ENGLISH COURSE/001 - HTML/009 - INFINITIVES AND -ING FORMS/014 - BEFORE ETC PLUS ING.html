🧩 Infinitives and -ing Forms – Before, After, Since, By, and For + -ing

When we use prepositions like before, after, since, by, and for, they are followed by the -ing form of a verb (also called a gerund). This is a common structure in English to show time, reason, or duration.

✅ Structure

preposition + verb-ing

✅ Common Uses

  • before + -ing: before doing something
  • after + -ing: after doing something
  • since + -ing: since doing something
  • by + -ing: method or means
  • for + -ing: purpose or reason
Examples:
Before:
Wash your hands before eating.
After:
She felt better after talking to a friend.
Since:
He’s been happier since moving to the countryside.
By:
You can improve by practicing every day.
For:
Thank you for helping me.
