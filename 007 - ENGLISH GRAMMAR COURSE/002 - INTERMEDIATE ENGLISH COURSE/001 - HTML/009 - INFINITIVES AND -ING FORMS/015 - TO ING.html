💬 Infinitives and -ing Forms – To + -ing

Some expressions end in to, but they are followed by the -ing form of a verb—not the infinitive. This happens because to is a preposition in these cases, not part of the infinitive.

✅ Structure

expression + to + verb-ing

Example: I look forward to seeing you.

✅ Common Expressions with To + -ing

  • look forward to
  • be used to
  • be committed to
  • be dedicated to
  • object to
  • be accustomed to
  • confess to
Examples:
Look forward to:
We look forward to meeting you soon.
Be used to:
She’s used to working late hours.
Be committed to:
They are committed to improving the service.
Object to:
He objected to being treated unfairly.
