🎉 Various Verb Structures – Exclamations

Exclamations are sentences that express strong emotion or surprise. In English, we often use special structures with verbs to form exclamations. These can include how, what, such, and so combined with adjectives, nouns, or verb phrases.

✅ Common Exclamation Structures

  • What + noun phrase: What a beautiful day!
  • How + adjective/adverb: How quickly he ran!
  • So + adjective: She is so talented!
  • Such + noun phrase: They are such good friends!
  • Exclamatory verb phrases: Isn’t it amazing! / Did he really say that!
Examples:
What + noun:
What a surprise to see you here!
How + adjective:
How wonderful this place is!
So + adjective:
She was so excited to meet them!
Such + noun:
They are such amazing performers!
Verb phrase:
Did you see that! Incredible!
