💥 Various Verb Structures – DO: Emphatic Auxiliary

We use do/does/did as an emphatic auxiliary to add emphasis to a verb in the present or past simple. This structure is used to show contrast, strong feelings, or to correct someone.

✅ Structure

do/does/did + base verb (without “to”)

Examples: I do like this movie. / She does want to come. / He did finish his homework.

✅ When to Use DO for Emphasis

  • To show contrast: He doesn’t like tea, but I do.
  • To correct someone: I did call you yesterday!
  • To express strong feelings: I do believe in you!
  • In formal writing or speech: We do appreciate your support.
Examples:
Present Simple:
I do enjoy reading at night.
Present Simple (third person):
She does want to join us.
Past Simple:
He did finish the report on time.
Correction:
I did send the email!
