🎯 Various Verb Structures – Emphasis with IT and WHAT

We use It and What in special sentence structures to emphasize a particular part of the sentence. These are called cleft sentences. They help us highlight new or important information.

✅ Structure with IT

It + be + emphasized part + that/who + rest of sentence

Example: It was John who broke the window.

✅ Structure with WHAT

What + clause + be + emphasized part

Example: What I need is a break.

✅ Common Uses

  • To emphasize the subject: It was Mary who called.
  • To emphasize the object: It was the book that he lost.
  • To emphasize the action or reason: What she did was amazing.
  • To clarify or contrast information.
Examples:
IT structure:
It was the teacher who gave us homework.
IT structure (object):
It was the keys that she lost.
WHAT structure:
What I love is traveling.
WHAT structure (action):
What he did was very brave.
