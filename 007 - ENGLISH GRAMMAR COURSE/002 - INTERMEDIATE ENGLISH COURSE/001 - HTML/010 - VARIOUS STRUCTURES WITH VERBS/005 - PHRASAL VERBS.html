🔗 Various Verb Structures – Phrasal Verbs

Phrasal verbs are combinations of a verb and one or more particles (prepositions or adverbs) that create a new meaning. They are common in spoken and informal English and often have idiomatic meanings.

✅ Structure

Verb + particle (e.g., give up, look after, take off)

✅ Types of Phrasal Verbs

  • Transitive (with object): She gave up smoking.
  • Intransitive (no object): The plane took off.
  • Separable: He turned off the light / He turned the light off.
  • Inseparable: We looked after the baby.
Examples:
Transitive:
She gave up chocolate last year.
Intransitive:
The train broke down suddenly.
Separable:
He turned off the TV / He turned the TV off.
Inseparable:
She looked after her little brother.
