📘 Articles – “The” in Generalisations

We use the definite article “the” to make generalisations about groups, species, inventions, or things that are understood as a whole category. This use is common in academic and formal writing.

✅ Structure

“The” + singular countable noun (representing a whole group)

“The” + adjective (to refer to a group of people)

✅ Common Uses

  • The lion is a dangerous animal. (refers to lions in general)
  • The computer has changed our lives. (refers to computers in general)
  • The rich should help the poor. (refers to groups of people)
  • The bicycle is a popular means of transport. (refers to bicycles in general)
Examples:
Generalisation – species:
The elephant is the largest land animal.
Generalisation – invention:
The smartphone has become essential.
Generalisation – group of people:
The elderly need special care.
Generalisation – object:
The book is a source of knowledge.
