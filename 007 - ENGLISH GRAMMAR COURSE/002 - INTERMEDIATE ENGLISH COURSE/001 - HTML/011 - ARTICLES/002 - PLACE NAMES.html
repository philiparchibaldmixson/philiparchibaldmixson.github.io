🌍 Articles – Place Names

Using articles with place names can be tricky. Some names take “the”, while others do not. The rules depend on the type of place and how the name is formed.

✅ When to Use “The”

  • With names that include common nouns: the Amazon River, the Sahara Desert
  • With plural place names: the Netherlands, the Philippines
  • With regions and areas: the Middle East, the Arctic
  • With organizations and buildings: the United Nations, the Eiffel Tower

❌ When Not to Use “The”

  • With most countries: Brazil, Japan
  • With cities and towns: London, Itamaraju
  • With continents and mountains: Asia, Mount Everest
  • With lakes and islands (usually): Lake Victoria, Sicily
Examples:
With “the”:
We visited the Grand Canyon last summer.
Without “the”:
She lives in Canada.
Plural country:
He’s from the United States.
Region:
Climate change affects the Arctic deeply.
