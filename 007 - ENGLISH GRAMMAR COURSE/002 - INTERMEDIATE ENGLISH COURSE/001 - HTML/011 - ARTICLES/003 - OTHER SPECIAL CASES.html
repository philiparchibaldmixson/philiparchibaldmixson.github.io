📑 Articles – Other Special Cases

Some article uses in English don’t follow the usual rules. These special cases often depend on fixed expressions, idioms, or specific contexts. Learning them helps improve fluency and accuracy.

✅ Common Special Cases

  • With meals: We usually say “have breakfast” (no article), but “the breakfast was delicious” (specific).
  • With institutions: “go to school” (general purpose), but “visit the school” (specific building).
  • With transport: “by car” (no article), but “the car is outside” (specific).
  • With places: “in prison” (as a prisoner), but “visit the prison” (as a visitor).
  • With time expressions: “in the morning”, “at night” (no article), “in the evening”.
Examples:
Meals:
We had breakfast at 8 a.m.
Institution:
She goes to school every day.
Transport:
He came by bus.
Place:
He’s in hospital after the accident.
Time:
We’ll meet in the afternoon.
