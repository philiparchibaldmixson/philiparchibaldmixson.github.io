🧮 Determiners – Some, Any, or No Article

We use some and any to talk about an unspecified quantity. Sometimes, we use no article when speaking generally or about things in plural or uncountable form.

✅ Usage of “Some”

  • Used in positive statements: I have some friends.
  • Used in offers and requests: Would you like some tea?

✅ Usage of “Any”

  • Used in negative statements: I don’t have any money.
  • Used in questions: Do you have any sugar?

✅ No Article

  • Used with plural or uncountable nouns when speaking generally: Books are useful. / Water is essential.
  • Used with meals, places, and transport in general: We had lunch. She goes to school by bus.
Examples:
Some:
I bought some apples at the market.
Any:
Do you have any questions?
No article:
Books can teach us many things.
Negative with “any”:
She didn’t bring any food.
