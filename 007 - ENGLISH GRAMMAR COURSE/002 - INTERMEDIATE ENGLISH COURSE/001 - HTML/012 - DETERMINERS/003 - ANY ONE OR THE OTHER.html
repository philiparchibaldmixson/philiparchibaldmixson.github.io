🔄 Determiners – “Any” = One or the Other (It’s Not Important Which)

Sometimes, we use “any” to mean “it doesn’t matter which one” or “one or the other”. This is different from using “any” in negative sentences or questions. It’s often used in offers, instructions, or when the choice is not important.

✅ Structure

any + singular countable noun or any + plural/uncountable noun

✅ Common Uses

  • Take any seat. (It doesn’t matter which seat.)
  • You can call me any time. (It doesn’t matter when.)
  • Choose any color you like.
  • Press any key to continue.
Examples:
Instruction:
You can sit in any chair.
Offer:
Take any cookie from the plate.
Choice:
Choose any topic.
Time:
You can come any day
