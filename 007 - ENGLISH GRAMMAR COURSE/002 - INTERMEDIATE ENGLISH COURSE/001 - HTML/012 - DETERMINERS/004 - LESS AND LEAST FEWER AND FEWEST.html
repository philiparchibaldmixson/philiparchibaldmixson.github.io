📉 Determiners – Less, Least, Fewer, Fewest

We use less and least with uncountable nouns, and fewer and fewest with countable nouns. These determiners help us compare quantities and express smaller amounts or numbers.

✅ Usage Summary

  • Less – smaller amount (uncountable): I have less time today.
  • Least – the smallest amount (uncountable): He has the least experience.
  • Fewer – smaller number (countable): There are fewer students this year.
  • Fewest – the smallest number (countable): She made the fewest mistakes.
Examples:
Less (uncountable):
We have less water than before.
Least (uncountable):
He showed the least interest in the topic.
Fewer (countable):
There are fewer cars on the road today.
Fewest (countable):
This team made the fewest errors.
