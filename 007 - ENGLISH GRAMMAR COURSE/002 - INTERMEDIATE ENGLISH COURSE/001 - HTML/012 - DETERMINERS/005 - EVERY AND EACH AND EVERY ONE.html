🔢 Determiners – Every, Each, and Every One

Every, each, and every one are used to refer to individual members of a group. They are similar, but have different emphasis and usage depending on context.

✅ Usage Summary

  • Every – refers to all members of a group as a whole. Used with singular countable nouns: Every student passed the test.
  • Each – emphasizes individual members separately. Also used with singular countable nouns: Each student received a certificate.
  • Every one – more emphatic and often followed by “of”: Every one of them was present.
Examples:
Every:
Every child needs love and care.
Each:
Each participant introduced themselves.
Every one:
Every one of the books was returned.
Each (emphasis on individual):
Each apple was carefully selected.
