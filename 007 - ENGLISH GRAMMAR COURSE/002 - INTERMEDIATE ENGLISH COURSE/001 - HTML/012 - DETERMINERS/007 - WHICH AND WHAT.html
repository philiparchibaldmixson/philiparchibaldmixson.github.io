❓ Determiners – Which? and What?

Which and what are question words used as determiners before nouns. They help us ask for specific or general information about people or things. They are followed by a noun or noun phrase.

✅ Usage Summary

  • Which – used when the options are limited or known: Which color do you prefer?
  • What – used when the options are open or unknown: What subject do you like?
  • Both are followed by a singular or plural noun: Which books? What time?

✅ Examples

Examples:
Which (limited options):
Which dress are you going to wear?
What (open options):
What movie do you want to watch?
Which (plural):
Which books did you borrow?
What (profession):
What job do you have?
