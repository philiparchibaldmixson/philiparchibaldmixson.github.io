🔄 Determiners – Other, Others, and Another

Other, others, and another are used to refer to additional people or things. They help us talk about alternatives or different members of a group.

✅ Usage Summary

  • Other – used before plural or uncountable nouns: other people, other information
  • Others – a pronoun that replaces “other + noun”: Some students arrived early; others were late.
  • Another – means “one more” or “a different one”; used with singular countable nouns: another book, another idea
Examples:
Other:
Do you have other questions about the topic?
Others:
Some people left early, but others stayed until the end.
Another:
Would you like another cup of coffee?
Other (uncountable):
We need other information before deciding.
