🧮 Determiners and “of” – Most People vs Most of Us

We use most to talk about the majority of a group. When followed by a noun, it refers to a general group. When followed by “of”, it refers to a specific group or pronoun.

✅ Usage Summary

  • Most + noun – general reference: Most people enjoy music.
  • Most of + determiner/pronoun – specific reference: Most of us were tired.
  • Use most of with: the, my, these, those, us, them, etc.
Examples:
General group:
Most children like sweets.
Specific group:
Most of the children were excited.
Pronoun:
Most of us agreed with the idea.
Demonstrative:
Most of these books are new.
