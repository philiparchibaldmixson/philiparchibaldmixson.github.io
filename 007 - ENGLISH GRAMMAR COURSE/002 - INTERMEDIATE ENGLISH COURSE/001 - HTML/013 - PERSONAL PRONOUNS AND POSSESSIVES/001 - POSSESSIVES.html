🧍 Personal Pronouns and Possessives – Possessives

Possessive determiners and possessive pronouns show ownership or relationship. They are used to indicate that something belongs to someone.

✅ Possessive Determiners

  • Used before a noun: my, your, his, her, its, our, their
  • Example: This is my book.

✅ Possessive Pronouns

  • Used without a noun: mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs
  • Example: This book is mine.

✅ Comparison Table

  • I → my / mine
  • You → your / yours
  • He → his / his
  • She → her / hers
  • It → its / its
  • We → our / ours
  • They → their / theirs
Examples:
Possessive determiner:
That is her car.
Possessive pronoun:
That car is hers.
Plural determiner:
We love our house.
Plural pronoun:
The house is ours.
