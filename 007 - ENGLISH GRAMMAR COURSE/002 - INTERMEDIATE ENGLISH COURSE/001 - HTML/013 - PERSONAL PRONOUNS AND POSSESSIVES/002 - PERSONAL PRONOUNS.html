🙋 Personal Pronouns and Possessives – Personal Pronouns

Personal pronouns are used to replace nouns and refer to people or things. They help us avoid repetition and make sentences clearer. They change depending on the subject or object of the sentence.

✅ Subject Pronouns

  • Used as the subject of a sentence: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
  • Example: She is reading a book.

✅ Object Pronouns

  • Used as the object of a verb or preposition: me, you, him, her, it, us, them
  • Example: I saw him at the park.

✅ Comparison Table

  • I → me
  • You → you
  • He → him
  • She → her
  • It → it
  • We → us
  • They → them
Examples:
Subject pronoun:
They are going to the cinema.
Object pronoun:
I called her yesterday.
Subject pronoun:
We love learning languages.
Object pronoun:
She invited us to the party.
