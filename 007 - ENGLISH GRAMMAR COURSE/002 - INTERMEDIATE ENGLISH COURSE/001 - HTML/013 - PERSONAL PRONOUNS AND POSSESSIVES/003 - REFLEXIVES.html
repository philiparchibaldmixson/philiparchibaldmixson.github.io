🔁 Personal Pronouns and Possessives – Reflexive Pronouns

Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and the object of a sentence are the same person. They show that someone does something to themselves.

✅ Reflexive Pronouns List

  • I → myself
  • You → yourself
  • He → himself
  • She → herself
  • It → itself
  • We → ourselves
  • You (plural) → yourselves
  • They → themselves

✅ Common Uses

  • To show self-action: She taught herself to play the guitar.
  • To emphasize: I made this myself.
  • After prepositions: He was proud of himself.
Examples:
Self-action:
She taught herself to play the guitar.
Emphasis:
I built this table myself.
Preposition:
He looked at himself in the mirror.
Plural:
We prepared ourselves for the test.
