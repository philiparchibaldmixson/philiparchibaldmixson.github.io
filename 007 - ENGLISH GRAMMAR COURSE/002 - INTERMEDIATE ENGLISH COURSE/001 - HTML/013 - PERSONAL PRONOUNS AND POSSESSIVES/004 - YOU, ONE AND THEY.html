🗣️ Personal Pronouns – You, One, and They (Generic Use)

Sometimes we use you, one, or they to talk about people in general. These pronouns don’t refer to a specific person, but to anyone or everyone.

✅ Usage Summary

  • You – informal and common in speech: You can't learn French in a month.
  • One – formal and used in writing: One must be careful when crossing the street.
  • They – used when the subject is unknown or general: They say it’s going to rain.

✅ Notes

  • You is often used in advice, instructions, or general truths.
  • One is more formal and less common in conversation.
  • They is used when the speaker doesn’t know exactly who.
Examples:
General truth:
You can't learn French in a month.
Formal advice:
One should always be polite.
Unknown source:
They say the movie is excellent.
Instruction:
You press this button to start.
