📦 Nouns – More About Countable and Uncountable Nouns

Nouns can be countable or uncountable. Understanding the difference helps us use correct articles, quantifiers, and verb agreement.

✅ Countable Nouns

  • Things we can count: one apple, two apples
  • Have singular and plural forms
  • Use a/an, many, few, several, a number of
  • Examples: book, chair, idea, student

✅ Uncountable Nouns

  • Things we cannot count individually: milk, information, advice
  • No plural form
  • Use some, much, little, a bit of, a piece of
  • Examples: water, furniture, knowledge, rice

✅ Some Nouns Can Be Both

  • Chicken (meat – uncountable / animal – countable)
  • Paper (material – uncountable / document – countable)
Examples:
Countable:
I have three books in my bag.
Uncountable:
She gave me some advice.
Both:
I bought a paper this morning. (newspaper)
Both:
We need some paper for the printer. (material)
