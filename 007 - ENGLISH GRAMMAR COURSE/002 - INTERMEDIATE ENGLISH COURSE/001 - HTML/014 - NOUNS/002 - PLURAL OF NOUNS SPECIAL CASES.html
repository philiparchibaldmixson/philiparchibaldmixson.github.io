🧠 Nouns – Plural of Nouns: Special Cases

Some English nouns have irregular plural forms or do not change at all in the plural. These special cases must be memorized and used correctly in writing and speech.

✅ Special Cases

  • Aircraft – same in singular and plural: One aircraft / Two aircraft
  • Sheep – same in singular and plural: One sheep / Ten sheep
  • Arms – always plural when referring to weapons: They carried arms

✅ Notes

  • Use context and verb agreement to identify singular or plural.
  • Arm (body part) is regular: one arm / two arms
  • Arms (weapons) is always plural and uncountable in this sense.
Examples:
Aircraft:
Several aircraft landed safely.
Sheep:
The farmer owns fifty sheep.
Arms (weapons):
The rebels were carrying arms.
Arm (body part):
He broke one of his arms.
