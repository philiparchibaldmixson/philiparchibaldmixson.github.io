🍰 Nouns – One and Ones

We use one and ones to avoid repeating a noun when it’s clear from context. They act as substitutes and help make speech and writing smoother.

✅ One

  • Used to replace a singular countable noun
  • Example: I’ll take a big one with cream. (referring to a cake)

✅ Ones

  • Used to replace a plural countable noun
  • Example: I prefer the red ones. (referring to apples)

✅ Notes

  • We often use adjectives before “one” or “ones”: a small one, the old ones
  • We don’t use “one” or “ones” for uncountable nouns
  • We can use “this one,” “that one,” “these ones,” “those ones” for emphasis
Examples:
Singular:
I’ll have a big one with cream.
Plural:
She chose the shiny ones.
Demonstrative:
I don’t like those ones.
Adjective + one:
Let’s buy a cheaper one.
