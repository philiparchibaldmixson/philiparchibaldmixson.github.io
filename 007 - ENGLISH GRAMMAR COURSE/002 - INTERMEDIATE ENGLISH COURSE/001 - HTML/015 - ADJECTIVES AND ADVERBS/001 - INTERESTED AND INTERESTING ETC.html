🎭 Adjectives and Adverbs – Interested vs. Interesting

Some adjectives ending in -ed and -ing can be confusing. They describe different things:

✅ -ed Adjectives

  • Describe how someone feels
  • Examples: interested, bored, excited, surprised
  • Example: I’m interested in art.

✅ -ing Adjectives

  • Describe the thing that causes the feeling
  • Examples: interesting, boring, exciting, surprising
  • Example: That book is interesting.

✅ Common Pairs

  • interested / interesting
  • bored / boring
  • excited / exciting
  • tired / tiring
  • confused / confusing
Examples:
Feeling:
She was interested in the topic.
Cause:
The topic was interesting.
Feeling:
I felt bored during the lecture.
Cause:
The lecture was boring.
