🏃 Adjectives and Adverbs – Adverbs with the Verb

Adverbs describe how, when, where, or to what degree an action happens. When used with verbs, they give more detail about the action.

✅ Common Adverb Types with Verbs

  • Manner – how something happens: quickly, slowly, carefully
  • Time – when something happens: now, soon, yesterday
  • Place – where something happens: here, there, outside
  • Frequency – how often: always, often, never
  • Degree – to what extent: completely, almost, very

✅ Position of Adverbs

  • Usually after the verb or object: She speaks clearly.
  • Sometimes before the verb: He always arrives early.
  • Some adverbs can move: Suddenly, he stood up.
Examples:
Manner:
She danced gracefully.
Time:
We will leave soon.
Place:
He went outside.
Frequency:
They always eat dinner together.
Degree:
She completely forgot the meeting.
