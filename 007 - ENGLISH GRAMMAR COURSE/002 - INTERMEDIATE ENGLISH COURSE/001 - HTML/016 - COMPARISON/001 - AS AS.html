📏 Comparison – As ... As

The structure as ... as is used to show equality or similarity. It can be used with adjectives, adverbs, or quantity expressions like as many, as much, as often.

✅ Structure

  • as + adjective/adverb + as → She is as tall as her brother.
  • as + many/much + noun + as → We invited as many people as possible.
  • as + adverb + as → He runs as fast as his friend.

✅ Notes

  • Use many with countable nouns: as many books as
  • Use much with uncountable nouns: as much water as
  • Use possible to express maximum effort: as quickly as possible
Examples:
Adjective:
She is as smart as her sister.
Adverb:
He works as hard as anyone.
Quantity:
We invited as many people as possible.
Uncountable:
Drink as much water as you need.
Effort:
Try to finish as quickly as possible.
