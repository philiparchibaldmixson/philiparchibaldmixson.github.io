📈 Comparison – More on Comparatives

We use comparatives to show how things change or to compare two things. In this lesson, we focus on two special patterns: repeated comparatives and “the more…, the better” structure.

✅ Repeated Comparatives

  • Structure: comparative + and + comparative
  • Used to show gradual change or increase
  • Examples: taller and taller, more and more difficult, better and better

✅ The More…, The Better…

  • Structure: the + comparative…, the + comparative…
  • Used to show cause and effect or parallel increase
  • Examples: The more you study, the better your results.
Examples:
Repeated comparative:
The baby is getting bigger and bigger every day.
Repeated comparative:
This book is becoming more and more interesting.
The more…, the better:
The more you practice, the better you become.
The more…, the more…:
The more he eats, the more weight he gains.
The less…, the less…:
The less you sleep, the less energy you have.
