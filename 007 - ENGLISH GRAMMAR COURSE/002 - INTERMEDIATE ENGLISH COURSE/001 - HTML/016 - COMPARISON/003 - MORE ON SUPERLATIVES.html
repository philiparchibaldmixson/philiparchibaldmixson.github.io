🏅 Comparison – More About Superlatives: “The Best Player of Us All”

Superlatives show the highest or lowest degree of a quality. When comparing within a group, we often use phrases like “of us all” or “in the team” to clarify the group.

✅ Structure

  • the + superlative + noun + of/in + group
  • Examples: the best player of us all, the smartest student in the class
  • Use “of us all” for personal groups; “in” for places or categories

✅ Common Superlatives

  • good → the best
  • bad → the worst
  • talented → the most talented
  • fast → the fastest
  • strong → the strongest
Examples:
Group comparison:
He is the best player of us all.
Location comparison:
She is the fastest runner in the school.
Category comparison:
That was the most difficult question in the exam.
Personal group:
You are the kindest of us all.
Superlative with “ever”:
It’s the best gift I’ve ever received.
