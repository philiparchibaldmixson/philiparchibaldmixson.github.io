🔍 Comparison – Like, As, So, and Such

These words are used to compare, describe, or emphasize qualities. They have different meanings and structures depending on how they are used.

✅ Like vs. As

  • Like → used to compare things or people: She sings like a professional.
  • As → used to describe roles or functions: He works as a teacher.
  • As + adjective + as → used for equality: She is as tall as her brother.

✅ So vs. Such

  • So + adjective/adverb: She is so kind.
  • Such + (a/an) + adjective + noun: He is such a kind person.
  • Use “that” to show result: She was so tired that she fell asleep.
Examples:
Like:
He dances like a professional.
As:
She works as a nurse.
So:
It was so cold that we stayed inside.
Such:
They are such good friends.
As…as:
She is as smart as her brother.
