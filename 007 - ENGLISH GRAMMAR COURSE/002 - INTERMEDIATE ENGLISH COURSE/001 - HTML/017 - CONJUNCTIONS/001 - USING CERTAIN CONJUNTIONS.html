🔗 Conjunctions – So That, As Long As, Until, etc.

Conjunctions connect clauses and show relationships like purpose, condition, or time. These conjunctions are useful for expressing goals, limits, and dependencies.

✅ So That

  • Shows purpose or result
  • Example: He studied hard so that he could pass the exam.

✅ As Long As

  • Shows condition or duration
  • Example: You can stay here as long as you are quiet.

✅ Until

  • Shows time limit or endpoint
  • Example: We waited until the rain stopped.

✅ Other Useful Conjunctions

  • Unless – condition: Unless it rains, we’ll go out.
  • Before – time: Finish your homework before dinner.
  • After – time: We’ll leave after the meeting ends.
Examples:
Purpose:
She left early so that she could catch the train.
Condition:
You can play as long as you finish your homework.
Time:
He worked until midnight.
Negative condition:
Unless you hurry, we’ll miss the bus.
Sequence:
We’ll eat after they arrive.
