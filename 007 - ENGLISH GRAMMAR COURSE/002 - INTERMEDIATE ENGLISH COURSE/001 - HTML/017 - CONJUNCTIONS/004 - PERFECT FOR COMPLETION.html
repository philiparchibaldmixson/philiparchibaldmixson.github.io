⏳ Conjunctions – Perfect for Completion: “When I've Finished”

Some conjunctions are ideal for expressing completion or the end of an action. One of the most common is when, especially in phrases like “When I’ve finished”. This structure helps us show that one action will happen after another is completed.

✅ Structure

  • When + present perfect → shows completion before another action
  • Example: When I’ve finished, I’ll call you.
  • Other conjunctions for completion: once, as soon as, after

✅ Usage Tips

  • Use the present perfect for the completed action: When I’ve finished
  • Use the future or present tense for the next action: I’ll call you
  • This structure is common in planning, instructions, and storytelling
Examples:
Completion:
When I’ve finished my homework, I’ll watch TV.
Sequence:
When she’s done with the report, she’ll send it.
Instruction:
When you’ve completed the form, click “Submit.”
Planning:
When we’ve packed everything, we’ll leave for the airport.
