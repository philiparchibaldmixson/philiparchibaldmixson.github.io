⏱️ Conjunctions – Tenses with Since and For

Since and for are conjunctions and prepositions used to express duration or starting points in time. They are commonly used with the present perfect and present perfect continuous tenses.

✅ Since

  • Used to show the starting point of an action
  • Followed by a specific time or event: since 2010, since we were students
  • Common with present perfect: I’ve known her since college.

✅ For

  • Used to show the duration of an action
  • Followed by a period of time: for two years, for a long time
  • Common with present perfect: We’ve lived here for ten years.

✅ Tense Patterns

  • Present perfect: have/has + past participle
  • Present perfect continuous: have/has been + verb-ing
  • Use with actions that started in the past and continue now
Examples:
Since:
We’ve been friends since we were students.
For:
I’ve worked here for five years.
Present perfect:
She has lived in Brazil since 2015.
Present perfect continuous:
They have been studying for hours.
