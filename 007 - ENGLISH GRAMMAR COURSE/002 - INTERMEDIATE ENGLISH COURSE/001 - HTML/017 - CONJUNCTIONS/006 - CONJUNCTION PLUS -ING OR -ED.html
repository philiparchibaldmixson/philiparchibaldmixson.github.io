🔗 Conjunctions – Conjunction + -ing or -ed

We can use conjunctions followed by -ing or -ed forms to create shorter, smoother sentences. These structures are common in spoken and written English and help express time, cause, or condition.

✅ Conjunction + -ing

  • Used when the subject of both clauses is the same
  • Common conjunctions: after, before, while, since
  • Example: After talking to you, I felt better.

✅ Conjunction + -ed

  • Used with passive meaning or completed action
  • Common conjunctions: until, once, when
  • Example: Until cooked, the meat is not safe to eat.

✅ Notes

  • These forms are often used at the beginning of a sentence
  • They reduce repetition and make writing more fluent
  • Make sure the subject is clear and logical
Examples:
-ing form:
After talking to you, I changed my mind.
-ing form:
Before leaving, she locked the door.
-ed form:
Until cooked, the rice will remain hard.
-ed form:
Once finished, the project will be reviewed.
