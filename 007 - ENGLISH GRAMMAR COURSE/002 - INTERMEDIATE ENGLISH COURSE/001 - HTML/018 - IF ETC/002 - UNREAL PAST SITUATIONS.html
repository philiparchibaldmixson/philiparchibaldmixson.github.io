🕰️ Conditionals – Unreal Past Situations

We use the third conditional to talk about unreal or hypothetical situations in the past. These are things that didn’t happen, but we imagine what would have happened if they had.

✅ Structure

  • If + past perfect, would have + past participle
  • Example: If Jane hadn’t helped me, I would have failed.
  • We can also use could have or might have for possibility

✅ Meaning

  • Used to express regret, relief, or imagined outcomes
  • Describes a condition that didn’t happen and its possible result
  • Example: If I had studied, I would have passed. (But I didn’t study)
Examples:
Regret:
If Jane hadn’t helped me, I would have failed.
Relief:
If we hadn’t left early, we would have missed the train.
Possibility:
If he had asked, I could have helped.
Imagined outcome:
If they had known, they might have come.
