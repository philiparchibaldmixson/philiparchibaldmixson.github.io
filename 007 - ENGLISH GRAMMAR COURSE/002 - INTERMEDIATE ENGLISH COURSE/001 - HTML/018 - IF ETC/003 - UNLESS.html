📌 Conditionals – Unless

Unless is a conjunction used to mean “if not”. It introduces a condition that must be avoided or prevented for the main action to happen. It’s often used in informal speech and writing.

✅ Structure

  • Main clause + unless + condition
  • Example: Come tonight unless I phone. (Come if I don’t phone)
  • “Unless” replaces “if not” in conditional sentences

✅ Meaning

  • “Unless” = “if not”
  • Used to express exceptions or negative conditions
  • Example: Unless it rains, we’ll go hiking. (We’ll go hiking if it doesn’t rain)
Examples:
Simple condition:
Come tonight unless I phone. (Come if I don’t phone)
Negative condition:
Unless you hurry, we’ll miss the bus.
Exception:
I’ll go unless I feel sick.
Instruction:
Don’t call unless it’s urgent.
