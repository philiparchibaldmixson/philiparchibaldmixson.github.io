☔ Conditionals – In Case

In case is used to express precaution or preparation for something that might happen. It introduces a possible future situation and explains what we do to be ready for it.

✅ Structure

  • Action + in case + present simple
  • Example: I’m taking my umbrella in case it rains.
  • We use the present tense after “in case” even when talking about the future

✅ Meaning

  • “In case” = because something might happen
  • Used to show preparation or caution
  • Example: Take your phone in case you get lost.
Examples:
Precaution:
I’m taking my umbrella in case it rains.
Preparation:
Bring some snacks in case you get hungry.
Safety:
Write down the address in case you forget it.
Travel:
Take your passport in case they ask for ID.
