⏰ It’s Time and I’d Rather – Unreal Tenses

It’s time and I’d rather are used to express suggestions, preferences, or criticism. They are often followed by past tense verbs, even though the meaning refers to the present or future. This use of the past tense shows distance or unreality.

✅ It’s Time

  • It’s time + subject + past simple
  • Example: It’s time you had a haircut. (You need a haircut now)
  • Used to suggest that something should happen now or soon

✅ I’d Rather

  • I’d rather + subject + past simple
  • Example: I’d rather you stayed home. (I prefer that you stay home)
  • Used to express preference about someone else’s action

✅ Notes

  • Past simple is used for present or future meaning
  • We use “I’d rather + base verb” when talking about ourselves: I’d rather go now.
  • We use “I’d rather + subject + past” when talking about others: I’d rather he didn’t come.
Examples:
It’s time:
It’s time you had a haircut.
It’s time:
It’s time we left for the airport.
I’d rather (others):
I’d rather you didn’t tell anyone.
I’d rather (self):
I’d rather stay home tonight.
