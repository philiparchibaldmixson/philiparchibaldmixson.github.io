🧬 Relatives – Whose

Whose is a relative pronoun used to show possession. It connects two clauses and replaces a possessive structure like “the girl’s beauty” with “a girl whose beauty…”.

✅ Structure

  • Noun + whose + noun + verb
  • Example: A girl whose beauty amazed everyone.
  • “Whose” replaces possessive forms like “his”, “her”, “their”, “the boy’s”, etc.

✅ Usage

  • Used to describe people, animals, or things with something belonging to them
  • Can be used in defining and non-defining relative clauses
  • Examples: The man whose car was stolen / A book whose cover is torn
Examples:
Person:
She is a girl whose beauty is admired by all.
Object:
I found a book whose pages were missing.
Animal:
That’s a dog whose owner travels a lot.
Group:
They are students whose results impressed the teacher.
