📍 Prepositions – Some Common Choices

Prepositions are small words that show relationships between other words in a sentence. They often indicate time, place, direction, cause, or manner. Choosing the correct preposition depends on the verb, noun, or adjective it follows.

✅ Common Preposition Patterns

  • Good at – She’s good at painting.
  • Interested in – I’m interested in history.
  • Afraid of – He’s afraid of spiders.
  • Depend on – It depends on the weather.
  • Listen to – We listened to music.

✅ Notes

  • Some verbs always take specific prepositions
  • Some adjectives are followed by prepositions
  • Changing the preposition can change the meaning
Examples:
Verb + preposition:
She apologized for being late.
Adjective + preposition:
He’s proud of his work.
Noun + preposition:
There’s a reason for everything.
Fixed expression:
She’s on time every day.
