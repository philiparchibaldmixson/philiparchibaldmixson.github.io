👀 Prepositions – Verbs with Prepositions

Some verbs are followed by specific prepositions. These combinations form fixed patterns that must be memorized. For example, look at means to direct your eyes toward something or someone.

✅ Structure

  • Verb + preposition + object
  • Example: Look at her.
  • “Look” needs the preposition “at” when referring to what someone sees

✅ Common Verb + Preposition Combinations

  • Look at – Look at the sky.
  • Talk to – Talk to your teacher.
  • Listen to – Listen to the music.
  • Wait for – Wait for the bus.
  • Think about – Think about your future.
Examples:
Visual focus:
Look at her. She’s waving!
Conversation:
He talked to his manager about the issue.
Attention:
Please listen to the instructions carefully.
Waiting:
We waited for the train for an hour.
