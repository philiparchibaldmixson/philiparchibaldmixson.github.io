📌 Prepositions – Nouns with Prepositions

Some nouns are commonly followed by specific prepositions. These combinations form fixed expressions that help clarify meaning. For example, “lack of sleep” means not having enough sleep.

✅ Structure

  • Noun + preposition + object
  • Example: He suffers from a lack of sleep.
  • “Lack” is followed by “of” to show what is missing

✅ Common Noun + Preposition Combinations

  • Advantage of – The advantage of exercise is better health.
  • Reason for – What’s the reason for your absence?
  • Cause of – Stress is a cause of illness.
  • Effect on – Pollution has an effect on the environment.
  • Lack of – Lack of sleep affects concentration.
Examples:
Missing something:
He complained about his lack of sleep.
Cause:
The cause of the fire is unknown.
Effect:
Noise has a negative effect on children.
Reason:
There’s no reason for panic.
