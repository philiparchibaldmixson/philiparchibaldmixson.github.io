💧 Prepositions – Adjectives with Prepositions

Some adjectives are followed by specific prepositions. These combinations are fixed and must be learned. For example, “full of” is used to describe something that contains a lot of something else.

✅ Structure

  • Adjective + preposition + noun
  • Example: The bottle is full of water.
  • “Full” is followed by “of” to show what fills the object

✅ Common Adjective + Preposition Combinations

  • Full of – The room was full of people.
  • Afraid of – She’s afraid of spiders.
  • Good at – He’s good at math.
  • Interested in – I’m interested in music.
  • Famous for – Paris is famous for its art.
Examples:
Quantity:
The glass is full of water.
Emotion:
She’s afraid of heights.
Skill:
He’s good at drawing.
Interest:
They’re interested in science.
