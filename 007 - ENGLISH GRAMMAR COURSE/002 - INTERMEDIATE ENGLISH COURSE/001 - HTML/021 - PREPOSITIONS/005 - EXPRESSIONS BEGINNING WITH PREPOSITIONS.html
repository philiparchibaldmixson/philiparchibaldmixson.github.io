🎉 Prepositions – Expressions Beginning with Prepositions

Some expressions begin with prepositions and are followed by a noun or noun phrase. These expressions are used to describe time, place, condition, or situation. For example, “at a party” describes location or event.

✅ Structure

  • Preposition + noun/noun phrase
  • Example: We met at a party.
  • “At” shows the location or occasion

✅ Common Expressions

  • At a party – I saw her at a party.
  • In a hurry – He left in a hurry.
  • On time – She always arrives on time.
  • By mistake – I sent the email by mistake.
  • Under pressure – He works well under pressure.
Examples:
Location:
We met at a party last weekend.
Time:
She arrived on time for the meeting.
Manner:
He left in a hurry.
Accident:
I deleted the file by mistake.
