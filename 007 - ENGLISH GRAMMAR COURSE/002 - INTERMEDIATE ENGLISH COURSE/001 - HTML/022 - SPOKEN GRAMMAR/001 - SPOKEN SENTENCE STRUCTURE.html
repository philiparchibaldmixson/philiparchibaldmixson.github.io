🗣️ Spoken Grammar – Spoken Sentence Structure

In spoken English, sentence structure is often more flexible and less formal than in written English. One common pattern is placing the subject or object after the comment, like “It’s difficult, the exam.” This structure is used for emphasis or clarification.

✅ Structure

  • Comment + noun (subject/object)
  • Example: It’s difficult, the exam.
  • Used to highlight or clarify what you're talking about

✅ Usage

  • Common in everyday speech
  • Often used with intonation and pauses
  • Helps focus attention or express emotion
Examples:
Emphasis:
It’s difficult, the exam.
Clarification:
It’s amazing, that view.
Emotion:
It’s annoying, the noise outside.
Focus:
It’s expensive, that restaurant.
