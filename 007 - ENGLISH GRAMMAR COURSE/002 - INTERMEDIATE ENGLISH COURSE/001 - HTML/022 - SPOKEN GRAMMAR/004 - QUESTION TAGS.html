❓ Spoken Grammar – More About Question Tags

Question tags are short questions added to the end of a statement. They are used to check information, show interest, or invite agreement. In spoken English, we sometimes use plural pronouns like “they” with negative subjects like “nobody”.

✅ Structure

  • Statement + comma + auxiliary verb + pronoun + ?
  • Example: Nobody phoned, did they?
  • “Nobody” is singular, but we use “they” in the tag

✅ Usage

  • Used to confirm or check information
  • Common in casual conversation
  • “They” is used for indefinite or unknown people
Examples:
Negative subject:
Nobody phoned, did they?
Indefinite subject:
Someone called, didn’t they?
Nothing happened:
Nothing went wrong, did it?
Everybody left:
Everybody left, didn’t they?
