Advanced Grammar – DO for Emphasis

Using DO for Emphasis

The auxiliary verb do can be used to add emphasis to a statement, especially in affirmative sentences. This is common in spoken English and in writing when we want to strongly affirm or contrast something.

Key Point: “Do” is used with the base form of the verb and adds emotional force or contrast.

Examples of Emphatic DO

Affirming Truth

I do like this movie!
She does know the answer.
We did finish the project on time.

Contrasting a Negative

He said I didn’t help, but I did help!
They think she doesn’t care, but she does care.

Responding with Emotion

I do want to go!
We did try our best.
Tip: Use “do/does/did” before the base verb to emphasize. Avoid overusing it in formal writing.
