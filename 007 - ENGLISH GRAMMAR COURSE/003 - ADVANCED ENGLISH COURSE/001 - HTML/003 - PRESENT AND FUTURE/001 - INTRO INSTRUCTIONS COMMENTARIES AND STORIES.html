Present and Future

Advanced Grammar – Instructions, Commentaries, and Stories

Introduction to Present and Future Usage

English uses the present tense in many situations beyond describing current actions. It can express instructions, commentaries, and even future events. Understanding these uses helps you sound more natural and fluent in both speech and writing.

Key Point: The present tense is flexible and often used for future meaning, especially in informal or narrative contexts.

Using Present Tense for Instructions

Instructions often use the present simple to describe steps or actions clearly and directly.

Examples

You open the app and select your profile.
First, you mix the ingredients. Then, you bake for 30 minutes.
You press the green button to start.

Using Present Tense for Commentaries

In sports, news, or live events, the present tense is used to describe actions as they happen—even if they’re slightly in the past.

Examples

He passes the ball to the striker—goal!
She walks onto the stage and begins her speech.
The crowd cheers as the team enters the field.

Using Present Tense in Stories

Writers sometimes use the present tense to make stories feel more vivid and immediate. This is called the “narrative present.”

Examples

I walk into the room and see everyone staring at me.
He opens the letter and reads the shocking news.
She runs down the street, laughing as the rain pours.
Tip: Using the present tense in stories creates a sense of drama and closeness for the reader.
