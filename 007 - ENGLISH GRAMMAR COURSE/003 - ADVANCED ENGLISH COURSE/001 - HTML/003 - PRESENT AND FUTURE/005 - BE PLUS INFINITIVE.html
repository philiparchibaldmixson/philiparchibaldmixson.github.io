Advanced Grammar – BE + Infinitive

BE + Infinitive Structure

The structure be + to + base verb is used to express formal future arrangements, instructions, or expectations. It is common in official contexts, news reports, and formal writing.

Key Point: This form is more formal than “going to” or “will” and often implies obligation or scheduled action.

Examples of BE + Infinitive

Formal Arrangements

The president is to visit Brazil next month.
The train is to depart at 6:00 a.m.

Instructions and Orders

You are to report to the manager immediately.
All guests are to leave by midnight.

Expectations and Plans

I am to start my new role on Monday.
They are to complete the task before Friday.
