Advanced Grammar – Future Progressive

What Is the Future Progressive?

The future progressive (also called future continuous) describes actions that will be in progress at a specific time in the future. It is formed with will be + verb-ing.

Key Point: Use the future progressive to emphasize duration, ongoing activity, or expected interruptions.

Examples of Future Progressive

Ongoing Future Actions

This time tomorrow, I’ll be flying to New York.
She’ll be working late tonight.

Polite or Indirect Questions

Will you be joining us for dinner?
Will they be attending the meeting?

Expected Future Events

We’ll be launching the new product next month.
He’ll be staying with us over the weekend.
