Advanced Grammar – Future Perfect

What Is the Future Perfect?

The future perfect tense describes an action that will be completed before a specific time in the future. It is formed with will have + past participle.

Key Point: Use the future perfect to show that something will be finished before another future event or time.

Examples of Future Perfect

Completed Actions Before a Future Time

By 8 p.m., I will have finished my homework.
She will have left by the time you arrive.

Expectations or Predictions

They will have completed the project before the deadline.
We will have traveled to five countries by the end of the year.
