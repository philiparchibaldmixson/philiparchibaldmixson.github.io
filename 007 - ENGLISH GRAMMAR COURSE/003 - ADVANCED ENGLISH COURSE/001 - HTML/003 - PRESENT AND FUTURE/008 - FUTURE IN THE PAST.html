Present and Future

Advanced Grammar – Future in the Past

What Is Future in the Past?

Future in the past describes a future event from the perspective of a past moment. It’s often used in storytelling, reported speech, or when reflecting on plans or expectations that existed earlier.

Structure: would + base verb / was/were going to + base verb

Examples of Future in the Past

Using “would”

She said she would call me later.
I knew they would be late.

Using “was/were going to”

I was going to tell you, but I forgot.
They were going to travel abroad last year.

In Narratives and Reflections

We thought the meeting would start at 10.
He was going to propose, but changed his mind.
