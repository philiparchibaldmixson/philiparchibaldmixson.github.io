Simple Past vs. Past Progressive

The simple past describes completed actions at a specific time in the past. The past progressive (was/were + verb-ing) describes actions that were in progress at a particular moment or when another action occurred.

Key Point: Use the past progressive for background actions and the simple past for shorter, interrupting actions.

Examples and Comparison

Simple Past

I watched a movie last night.
She called me yesterday.

Past Progressive

I was watching a movie when she called.
They were studying all evening.

Combined Use

While I was walking to work, it started to rain.
He was cooking dinner when the phone rang.
