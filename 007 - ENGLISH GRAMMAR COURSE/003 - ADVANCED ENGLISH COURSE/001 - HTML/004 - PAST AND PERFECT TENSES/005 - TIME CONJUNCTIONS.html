Understanding the Past Perfect with Time Conjunctions

The past perfect is used to show that one past action happened before another. Time conjunctions like before, after, when, by the time, and as soon as help clarify the sequence of events.

Structure: had + past participle (used with another past action or time reference)

Examples with Time Conjunctions

Before / After

She had left before I arrived.
After he had finished dinner, he went for a walk.

When / As Soon As

When I had completed the task, I took a break.
As soon as they had landed, they called home.

By the Time

By the time we got there, the show had started.
He had already left by the time I called.
