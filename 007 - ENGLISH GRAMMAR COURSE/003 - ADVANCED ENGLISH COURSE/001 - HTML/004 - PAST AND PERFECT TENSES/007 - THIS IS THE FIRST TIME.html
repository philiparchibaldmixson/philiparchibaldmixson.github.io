Past and Perfect Tenses

Advanced Grammar – “This is the First Time…”

Using “This is the first time…”

The phrase “This is the first time…” is followed by the present perfect to describe a new experience or event that has just happened. It emphasizes that something has never occurred before now.

Structure: This is the first time + subject + have/has + past participle

Examples of “This is the first time…”

New Experiences

This is the first time I’ve tried sushi.
This is the first time she’s traveled abroad.

Formal or Written Contexts

This is the first time the company has launched a product in Asia.
This is the first time we’ve received such feedback.

Past Reference (Optional)

In past contexts, use “It was the first time…” followed by the past perfect.

It was the first time I had spoken in public.
It was the first time they had met each other.
