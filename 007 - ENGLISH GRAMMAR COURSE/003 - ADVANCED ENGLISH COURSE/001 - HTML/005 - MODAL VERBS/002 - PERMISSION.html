Modal Verbs for Permission

Modal verbs like can, could, may, and might are used to ask for or give permission. They vary in formality and certainty.

Structure: modal + base verb (e.g., “May I leave?”)

Usage and Examples

Can – Informal Permission

Can I borrow your pen?
You can go now.

Could – Polite or Past Permission

Could I ask you a question?
When I was a child, I could stay up late.

May – Formal or Polite Permission

May I come in?
You may leave early today.

Might – Tentative or Uncertain Permission

You might be allowed to attend the meeting.
Might I suggest an alternative?
Tip: “May” is more formal than “can.” “Could” is more polite. “Might” is less certain.
