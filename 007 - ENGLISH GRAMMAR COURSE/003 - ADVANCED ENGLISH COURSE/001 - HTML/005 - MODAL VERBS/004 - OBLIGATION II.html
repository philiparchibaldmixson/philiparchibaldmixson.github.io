Advanced Grammar – Obligation: Should and Ought To

Modal Verbs for Advice and Obligation

Should and ought to express advice, recommendation, or moral obligation. They are often interchangeable, though “ought to” is slightly more formal or traditional.

Structure: should + base verb / ought to + base verb

Usage and Examples

Should – Common Advice or Expectation

You should drink more water.
He should be here by now.

Ought To – Formal or Moral Advice

You ought to apologize for that.
We ought to respect others’ opinions.

Negative Forms

You shouldn’t eat so much sugar.
He ought not to speak that way.
Tip: “Should” is more common in speech. “Ought to” adds emphasis or formality.
