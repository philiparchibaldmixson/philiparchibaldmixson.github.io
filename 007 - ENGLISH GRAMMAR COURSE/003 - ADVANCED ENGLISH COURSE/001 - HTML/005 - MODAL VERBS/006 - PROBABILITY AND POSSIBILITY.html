Modal Verbs for Probability and Possibility

Modal verbs like may, might, can, and could express different degrees of possibility. They help us talk about uncertain situations, potential outcomes, and hypothetical ideas.

Structure: modal + base verb (e.g., “It may rain.”)

Usage and Examples

May – Formal or Likely Possibility

It may rain later today.
She may join us for dinner.

Might – Less Certain or Tentative Possibility

He might come to the party.
We might need more time.

Can – General Possibility or Ability

Accidents can happen anytime.
This app can help you learn faster.

Could – Hypothetical or Past Possibility

It could be dangerous if we’re not careful.
She could have missed the train.
Tip: “May” and “might” are used for present/future possibility. “Can” shows general possibility. “Could” adds uncertainty or refers to the past.
