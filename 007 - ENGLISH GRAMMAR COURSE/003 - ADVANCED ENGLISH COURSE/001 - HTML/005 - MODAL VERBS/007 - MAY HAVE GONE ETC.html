Modal Verbs

Advanced Grammar – Perfect Modals: May Have Gone, Should Have Told, etc.

What Are Perfect Modals?

Perfect modals are used to express possibility, certainty, obligation, or regret about past actions. They follow the structure: modal + have + past participle.

Structure: may/might/could/should/must + have + past participle

Usage and Examples

May/Might/Could Have – Past Possibility

She may have gone to the store.
He might have forgotten the meeting.
They could have missed the train.

Should Have – Missed Obligation or Advice

You should have told me earlier.
We should have checked the weather.

Must Have – Strong Deduction About the Past

He must have left already.
They must have misunderstood the instructions.
Tip: Use perfect modals to reflect on past events with possibility, advice, or deduction.
