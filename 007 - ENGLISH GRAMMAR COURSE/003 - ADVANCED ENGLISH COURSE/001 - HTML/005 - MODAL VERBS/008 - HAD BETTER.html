Modal Verbs

Advanced Grammar – Had Better

Understanding “Had Better”

Had better is used to give strong advice or warnings. It implies that something bad might happen if the advice is not followed. It is more urgent than “should” or “ought to.”

Structure: subject + had better + base verb

Usage and Examples

Advice with Urgency

You had better take an umbrella—it looks like rain.
She had better apologize before it’s too late.

Warnings

They had better not be late again.
You’d better be careful with that glass.

Contractions

You’d better leave now.
He’d better not forget his passport.
Tip: “Had better” is followed by the base verb and is not used in the past tense, despite “had.”
