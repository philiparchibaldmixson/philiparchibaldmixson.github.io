Modal Verbs

Advanced Grammar – Be Supposed To

Understanding “Be Supposed To”

Be supposed to is used to express expectation, obligation, or something that is intended or expected to happen. It often implies that something is required or planned, even if it doesn’t happen.

Structure: subject + be (am/is/are/was/were) + supposed to + base verb

Usage and Examples

Expectation or Obligation

You’re supposed to submit the report by Friday.
She’s supposed to be at the meeting now.

Past Reference

We were supposed to leave at 8 a.m.
He was supposed to call me yesterday.

Negative Form

You’re not supposed to park here.
They weren’t supposed to know about the surprise.
Tip: Use “be supposed to” to talk about rules, plans, or expectations—especially when they’re not followed.
