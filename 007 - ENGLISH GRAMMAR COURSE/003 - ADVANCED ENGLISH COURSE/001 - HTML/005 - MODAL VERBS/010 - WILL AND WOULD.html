Modal Verbs

Advanced Grammar – Will and Would: Willingness and Typical Behaviour

Understanding “Will” and “Would”

Will and would are modal verbs used to express willingness, habits, and typical behaviour. “Will” refers to present or future actions, while “would” is used for past habits or polite requests.

Structure: subject + will/would + base verb

Usage and Examples

Willingness

I’ll help you with your homework.
She would always offer to drive us home.

Typical Behaviour or Habits

He will talk for hours about history.
When we were kids, we would play outside until dark.

Polite Requests or Offers

Would you like some coffee?
I would be happy to assist you.
Tip: Use “will” for current/future actions and “would” for past habits or polite expressions.
