Modal Verbs

Advanced Grammar – Used To

Understanding “Used To”

Used to is a modal-like expression used to describe past habits, states, or repeated actions that no longer happen. It emphasizes contrast between the past and the present.

Structure: subject + used to + base verb

Usage and Examples

Past Habits or Routines

I used to play the piano when I was younger.
She used to walk to school every day.

Past States

We used to live in São Paulo.
He used to be very shy.

Negative and Questions

I didn’t use to like coffee.
Did you use to travel a lot?
Tip: “Used to” is only for the past. For present habits, use “usually” or the present simple.
