Modal Verbs

Advanced Grammar – Need

Understanding “Need” as a Modal Verb

Need can function as both a regular verb and a modal verb. As a modal, it is mainly used in negative sentences and questions to express necessity or obligation, especially in British English.

Structure (modal): need + base verb (without “to”)
Structure (regular): need + to + base verb

Usage and Examples

Modal “Need” – Negative and Questions

You needn’t worry about the exam.
Need I finish it today?

Regular “Need” – Affirmative Sentences

You need to submit the form by Friday.
She needs to take her medicine.

Past Form

You needn’t have called me—I was already on my way.
He didn’t need to bring anything.
Tip: Use “needn’t” for unnecessary actions. Use “need to” for general obligation.
