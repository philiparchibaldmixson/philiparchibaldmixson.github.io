Advanced Passive Structures

Beyond basic and complex passives, English includes other advanced passive forms that add flexibility and precision to your writing. These include passive infinitives, passive gerunds, causative passives, and prepositional passives.

Types and Examples

Passive Infinitives

The report needs to be completed by Friday.
She wants to be invited to the event.

Passive Gerunds

Being chosen for the team was a great honor.
He mentioned being promoted last year.

Causative Passive (have/get something done)

We had the house painted last week.
She got her hair done for the wedding.

Prepositional Passive

The candidate was voted for by the majority.
This topic is often talked about in class.
Tip: These passive forms allow you to express actions indirectly, formally, or with emphasis on the receiver.
