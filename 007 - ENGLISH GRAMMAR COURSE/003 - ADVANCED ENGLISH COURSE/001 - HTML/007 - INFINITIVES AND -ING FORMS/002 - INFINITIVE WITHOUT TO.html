Infinitives and -ing Forms

Advanced Grammar – Infinitive Without To

What Is the Infinitive Without “To”?

The bare infinitive (infinitive without “to”) is the base form of the verb used after certain verbs, modals, and expressions. It’s common in causative, perception, and modal structures.

Structure: verb + base verb (without “to”)
Examples: She made me cry. / You must go.

Common Uses of Bare Infinitives

After Modal Verbs

You should study more.
He might arrive late.

After Verbs of Perception (see, hear, feel, watch, etc.)

I saw her leave the room.
We heard them sing.

After “make” and “let”

She made me laugh.
Let him speak.
Tip: Use the bare infinitive after modals, causatives, and perception verbs to sound natural and fluent.
