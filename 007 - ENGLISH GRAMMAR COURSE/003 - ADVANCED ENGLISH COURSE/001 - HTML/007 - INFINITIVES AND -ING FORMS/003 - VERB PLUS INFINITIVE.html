Infinitives and -ing Forms

Advanced Grammar – Verb + Infinitive

What Is “Verb + Infinitive”?

Many verbs are followed by the infinitive form (to + base verb). This structure is used to express intention, desire, decision, or plans. It’s common in formal and academic writing.

Structure: verb + to + base verb
Examples: She decided to leave. / They hope to win.

Common Verbs Followed by Infinitives

Verbs of Intention or Decision

He decided to study abroad.
We plan to visit Italy next year.

Verbs of Desire or Preference

She wants to learn Spanish.
I’d prefer to stay home tonight.

Verbs of Promise or Refusal

He promised to help us.
They refused to cooperate.
Tip: Use “verb + infinitive” to express future actions, intentions, or polite offers.
