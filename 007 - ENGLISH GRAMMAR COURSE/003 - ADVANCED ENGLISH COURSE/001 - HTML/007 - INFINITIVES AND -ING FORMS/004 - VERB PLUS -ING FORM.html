Infinitives and -ing Forms

Advanced Grammar – Verb + -ing Form

What Is “Verb + -ing Form”?

Many verbs are followed by the -ing form (gerund). This structure is used to express enjoyment, avoidance, or mental processes. It’s common in everyday and academic English.

Structure: verb + -ing form
Examples: She enjoys reading. / They admitted cheating.

Common Verbs Followed by -ing Forms

Verbs of Enjoyment or Preference

He enjoys swimming in the ocean.
I love cooking for friends.

Verbs of Avoidance or Denial

She avoided talking about the issue.
They denied breaking the rules.

Verbs of Mental Process or Suggestion

We considered moving to another city.
He suggested waiting until tomorrow.
Tip: Use “verb + -ing” to describe ongoing actions, preferences, or reflections.
