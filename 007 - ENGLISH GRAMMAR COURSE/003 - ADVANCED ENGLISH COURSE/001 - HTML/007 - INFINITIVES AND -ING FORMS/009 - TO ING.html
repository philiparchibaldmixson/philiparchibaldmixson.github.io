Infinitives and -ing Forms

Advanced Grammar – To + -ing

Understanding “To” Followed by -ing

Although “to” is usually followed by the base form of a verb (infinitive), it can also be followed by an -ing form when “to” is a preposition. This happens after certain verbs, adjectives, and expressions.

Structure: verb/expression + to (preposition) + -ing
Examples: I look forward to meeting you. / They admitted to breaking the rule.

Common Expressions with “To” + -ing

Verbs Followed by “To” as a Preposition

He confessed to cheating on the test.
They objected to working overtime.

Adjectives and Nouns Followed by “To”

She’s committed to improving her skills.
Their dedication to helping others is admirable.

Fixed Expressions

I’m used to waking up early.
We’re looking forward to seeing you.
Tip: If “to” is a preposition, it must be followed by a noun or -ing form—not an infinitive.
