Advanced Grammar – Determiners with -ing Form

Understanding Determiners with -ing Forms

When a verb in the -ing form acts as a noun (a gerund), it can be preceded by a determiner or possessive adjective (e.g., my, his, her, their). This structure emphasizes who performs the action and is common in formal writing.

Structure: determiner/possessive + verb-ing
Examples: I appreciate your helping me. / His singing impressed everyone.

Usage and Examples

Possessive + -ing Form

I didn’t like his shouting during the meeting.
We appreciated their arriving early.

Emphasizing the Doer of the Action

Her dancing was graceful.
My speaking out caused tension.

Formal and Academic Use

The teacher praised the students’ writing.
We discussed his leaving the company.
Tip: Use possessive + -ing to highlight the person responsible for the action, especially in formal contexts.
