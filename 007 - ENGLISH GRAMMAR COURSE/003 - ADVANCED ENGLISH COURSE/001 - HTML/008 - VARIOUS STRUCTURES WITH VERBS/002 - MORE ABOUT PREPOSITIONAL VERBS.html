Advanced Grammar – More About Prepositional Verbs

What Are Prepositional Verbs?

Prepositional verbs are verbs that are followed by a specific preposition and then an object. The preposition is essential to the meaning of the verb and cannot be omitted. These structures are common in both formal and informal English.

Structure: verb + preposition + object
Examples: She applied for the job. / He believes in hard work.

Common Prepositional Verbs

Verbs with Fixed Prepositions

We’re waiting for the bus.
She apologized for being late.

Verbs with Abstract Objects

He believes in honesty.
They care about the environment.

Verbs with People as Objects

I spoke to the manager.
She depends on her team.
Tip: Don’t separate the verb from its preposition. The preposition is part of the verb’s meaning.
