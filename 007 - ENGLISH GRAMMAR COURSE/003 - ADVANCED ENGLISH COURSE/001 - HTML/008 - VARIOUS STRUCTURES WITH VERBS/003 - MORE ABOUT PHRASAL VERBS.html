Advanced Grammar – More About Phrasal Verbs

What Are Phrasal Verbs?

Phrasal verbs are combinations of a verb and one or more particles (prepositions or adverbs) that together create a meaning different from the individual words. They are common in spoken and informal English, but many are also used in formal contexts.

Structure: verb + particle(s) [+ object]
Examples: She gave up. / He put off the meeting.

Types of Phrasal Verbs

Transitive and Separable

They turned off the lights. / They turned the lights off.
She called off the meeting. / She called the meeting off.

Transitive and Inseparable

We’re looking into the problem. (not: ~looking the problem into~)
He ran into an old friend.

Intransitive Phrasal Verbs

The plane took off.
She woke up late.
Tip: Some phrasal verbs are idiomatic and their meanings can’t be guessed from the individual words.
