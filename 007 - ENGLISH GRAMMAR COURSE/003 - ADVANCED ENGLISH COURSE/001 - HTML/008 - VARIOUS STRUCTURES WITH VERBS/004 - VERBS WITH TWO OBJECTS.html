Advanced Grammar – Verbs with Two Objects

What Are Verbs with Two Objects?

Some verbs can take two objects: a direct object (the thing given) and an indirect object (the person receiving it). These verbs often describe actions of giving, showing, telling, or sending.

Structure 1: verb + indirect object + direct object
Structure 2: verb + direct object + to/for + indirect object
Examples: She gave me a gift. / She gave a gift to me.

Common Verbs with Two Objects

Verbs of Giving and Sending

He sent her a message.
She gave the book to her friend.

Verbs of Showing and Telling

They showed us the new design.
He told the story to the children.

Verbs of Offering and Lending

I offered him a ride.
She lent her notes to me.
Tip: Use either structure depending on emphasis or clarity. Both are grammatically correct.
