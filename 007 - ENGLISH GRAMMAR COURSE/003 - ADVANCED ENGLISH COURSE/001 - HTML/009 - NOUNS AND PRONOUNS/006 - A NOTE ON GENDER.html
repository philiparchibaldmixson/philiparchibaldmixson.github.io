Advanced Grammar – A Note on Gender: He, She, or It?

Understanding Gender in Pronouns

English pronouns reflect gender in the third person singular: he (male), she (female), and it (things, animals, or abstract ideas). Choosing the correct pronoun depends on the noun’s nature and context. In modern usage, gender-neutral pronouns like they are also common.

He: used for male people
She: used for female people
It: used for objects, animals (unless gender is known), and abstract nouns
They: used for plural or gender-neutral reference

Examples and Usage

People

John is a doctor. He works at the hospital.
Maria is my friend. She lives in Lisbon.

Objects and Animals

The book is on the table. It is new.
The dog is barking. It wants food. (or He/She if gender is known)

Gender-Neutral and Formal Use

Someone left their umbrella. (gender unknown)
Each student must submit their work. (inclusive language)
Tip: Use “they” for inclusivity or when gender is unknown. Use “it” only for non-human references.
