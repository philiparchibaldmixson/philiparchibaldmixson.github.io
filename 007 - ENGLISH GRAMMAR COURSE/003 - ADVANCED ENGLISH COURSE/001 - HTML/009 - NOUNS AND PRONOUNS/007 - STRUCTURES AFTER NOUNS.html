Advanced Grammar – Structures After Nouns

Understanding Structures After Nouns

In advanced English, nouns are often followed by additional structures that clarify, define, or expand their meaning. These include prepositional phrases, relative clauses, infinitive phrases, and appositives. These structures help build more precise and informative sentences.

Examples: the book on the shelf, the person who helped me, the opportunity to learn, my friend Maria

Types of Post-Noun Structures

Prepositional Phrases

The keys on the table are mine.
She wore a necklace with a silver pendant.

Relative Clauses

The student who won the award is absent.
I met a woman whose car was stolen.

Infinitive Phrases

We have a lot of work to finish.
He found a place to stay.

Appositives

My brother, a skilled musician, teaches piano.
The capital, Brasília, is located in central Brazil.
Tip: These structures follow the noun and enrich your sentence with detail, purpose, or identification.
