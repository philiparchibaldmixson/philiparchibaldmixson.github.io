Nouns and Pronouns

Advanced Grammar – Personal Pronouns

What Are Personal Pronouns?

Personal pronouns replace specific nouns and refer to people or things. They vary by person (first, second, third), number (singular, plural), and case (subject, object). Mastering personal pronouns helps avoid repetition and improves sentence flow.

Subject pronouns: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
Object pronouns: me, you, him, her, it, us, them

Examples and Usage

Subject Pronouns

She is a doctor.
They work in the same office.

Object Pronouns

I saw him at the store.
Can you help us?

Pronoun Agreement

Maria and I went shopping. We bought some clothes.
John called Lisa. He asked her to join the meeting.
Tip: Use subject pronouns before verbs and object pronouns after verbs or prepositions.
