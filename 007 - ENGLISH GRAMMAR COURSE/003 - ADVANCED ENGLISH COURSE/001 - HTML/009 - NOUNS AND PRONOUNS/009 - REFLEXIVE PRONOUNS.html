Advanced Grammar – Reflexive Pronouns and Each Other / One Another

Understanding Reflexive and Reciprocal Pronouns

Reflexive pronouns refer back to the subject of the sentence and are used when the subject and object are the same. Reciprocal pronouns like each other and one another describe mutual actions or feelings between two or more people.

Reflexive pronouns: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves
Reciprocal pronouns: each other, one another

Examples and Usage

Reflexive Pronouns

She taught herself to play the piano.
We enjoyed ourselves at the party.

Reciprocal Pronouns

They hugged each other after the match.
The teammates congratulated one another.

Common Errors

Incorrect: He looked at him. → Correct: He looked at himself.
Incorrect: They helped them. → Correct: They helped each other.
Tip: Use reflexive pronouns when the subject and object are the same. Use reciprocal pronouns for mutual actions.
