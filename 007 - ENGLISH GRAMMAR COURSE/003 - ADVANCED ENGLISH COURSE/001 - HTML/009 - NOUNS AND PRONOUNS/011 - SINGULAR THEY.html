Advanced Grammar – Singular They

Understanding Singular “They”

Singular “they” is used to refer to a person when the gender is unknown, irrelevant, or nonbinary. It is grammatically accepted and widely used in both formal and informal contexts. It helps avoid gender bias and ensures inclusivity.

Examples: If someone calls, tell them I’ll call back. / Each student should submit their work.

Examples and Usage

Unknown Gender

Someone left their umbrella here.
If a person wants to succeed, they must work hard.

Nonbinary Identity

Alex said they would arrive at noon.
Jordan prefers they/them pronouns.

Generic Reference

Every employee must check their email daily.
No one wants to feel like they’re being ignored.
Tip: Use “they” with singular meaning to promote clarity, neutrality, and respect for identity.
