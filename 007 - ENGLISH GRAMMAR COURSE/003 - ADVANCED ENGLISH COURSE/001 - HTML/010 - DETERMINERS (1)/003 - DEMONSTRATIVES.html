Determiners

Advanced Grammar – Demonstratives

What Are Demonstratives?

Demonstratives are determiners that point to specific things in relation to the speaker’s position. They include this, that, these, and those. They help identify nouns in terms of distance (near or far) and number (singular or plural).

Near: this (singular), these (plural)
Far: that (singular), those (plural)

Examples and Usage

Singular Demonstratives

This book is mine. (near)
That car belongs to my neighbor. (far)

Plural Demonstratives

These cookies are delicious. (near)
Those mountains look beautiful. (far)

With Abstract or Time Reference

This idea could work. (current topic)
That was a great moment. (past event)
Tip: Use demonstratives to clarify which noun you’re referring to and to add emphasis or contrast.
