Advanced Grammar – Possessives: My, Mine, and Others

Understanding Possessive Determiners and Pronouns

Possessive determiners (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) come before a noun to show ownership. Possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs) replace the noun entirely. These forms must agree in number and person with the noun they refer to.

Determiner: This is my book.
Pronoun: This book is mine.

Examples and Usage

Possessive Determiners

Her phone is on the table.
Our team won the match.

Possessive Pronouns

That jacket is mine.
The decision was theirs.

Common Errors

Incorrect: This is mine book. → Correct: This is my book.
Incorrect: That is her’s. → Correct: That is hers.
Tip: Use possessive determiners before nouns and possessive pronouns to replace nouns. Never mix them.
