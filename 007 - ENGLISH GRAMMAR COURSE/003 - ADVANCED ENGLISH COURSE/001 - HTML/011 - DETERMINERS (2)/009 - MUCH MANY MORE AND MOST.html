Determiners

Advanced Grammar – Much, Many, More, and Most

Understanding “Much,” “Many,” “More,” and “Most”

These determiners express quantity and comparison. Much is used with uncountable nouns, while many is used with plural countable nouns. More and most are comparative and superlative forms used with both types of nouns.

Much: much water, much time
Many: many books, many people
More: more sugar, more ideas
Most: most money, most students

Examples and Usage

Using “Much” and “Many”

There isn’t much milk left.
She has many friends in the city.

Using “More”

We need more information.
He has more books than I do.

Using “Most”

Most people agree with the idea.
She spent most of her time studying.
Tip: Use “much/many” for quantity, “more” for comparison, and “most” for majority or emphasis.
