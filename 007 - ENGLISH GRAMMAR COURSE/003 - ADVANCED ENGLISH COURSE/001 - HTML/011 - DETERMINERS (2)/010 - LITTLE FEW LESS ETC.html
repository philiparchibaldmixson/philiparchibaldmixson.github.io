Determiners

Advanced Grammar – Little, Few, Less, Fewer, Least, Fewest

Understanding These Determiners

These determiners express small quantity or degree. Little and less are used with uncountable nouns. Few and fewer are used with countable nouns. Least and fewest are their superlative forms.

Uncountable: little, less, least
Countable: few, fewer, fewest

Examples and Usage

Little / Less / Least (Uncountable)

There is little hope left.
We have less time than expected.
She showed the least interest in the topic.

Few / Fewer / Fewest (Countable)

Few people understood the question.
There were fewer errors this time.
He made the fewest mistakes in the class.
Tip: Use “little/less/least” with things you can’t count (e.g., water, time), and “few/fewer/fewest” with things you can count (e.g., books, students).
