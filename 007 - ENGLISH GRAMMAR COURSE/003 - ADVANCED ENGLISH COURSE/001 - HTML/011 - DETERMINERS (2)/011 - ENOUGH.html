Determiners

Advanced Grammar – Enough

Understanding “Enough”

Enough is a determiner used to express the right amount or degree. It can be used with countable and uncountable nouns, adjectives, and adverbs. Its position in the sentence depends on what it modifies.

Examples: We have enough chairs. / She isn’t tall enough. / He spoke loudly enough.

Examples and Usage

With Nouns

There isn’t enough water.
Do we have enough volunteers?

With Adjectives

She’s strong enough to lift it.
Is the room warm enough?

With Adverbs

He ran fast enough to win.
They didn’t work hard enough.
Tip: Use “enough” before nouns, but after adjectives and adverbs.
