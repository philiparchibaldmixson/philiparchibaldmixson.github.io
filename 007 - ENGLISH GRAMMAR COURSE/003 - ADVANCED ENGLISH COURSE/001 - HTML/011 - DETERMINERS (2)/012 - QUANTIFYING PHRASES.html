Advanced Grammar – Quantifying Phrases

Understanding Quantifying Phrases

Quantifying phrases are expressions that indicate quantity or amount. They often combine determiners with prepositions or modifiers to give more precise information. These phrases can be used with countable or uncountable nouns depending on their structure.

Examples: a lot of, plenty of, a number of, a great deal of, a bit of, loads of

Examples and Usage

With Countable Nouns

A number of students were absent.
Lots of books were donated.

With Uncountable Nouns

There was a great deal of interest in the topic.
We have plenty of time.

Informal and Formal Use

She has loads of energy. (informal)
A considerable amount of effort was made. (formal)
Tip: Match the quantifying phrase to the noun type and context (formal vs informal).
