Advanced Grammar – Of with Quantifiers

Understanding “Of” with Quantifiers

Quantifiers like some, many, most, none, all, and each are often followed by of when they precede a determiner (e.g., the, my, these). This structure helps specify the group being referred to.

Examples: Some of the students, All of my friends, None of those options

Examples and Usage

With “the,” “my,” “these,” etc.

Some of the books were missing.
All of my ideas were rejected.
None of those answers is correct.

Without “of” (general reference)

Some students were late. (general)
Most people agree. (general)
Tip: Use “of” when the quantifier is followed by a determiner. Drop “of” for general references.
