Adjectives & Adverbs

Advanced Grammar – Introduction and Adjective or Adverb?

Introduction to Adjectives, Adverbs, and Comparison

Adjectives describe or modify nouns and pronouns. Adverbs describe or modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Both can be used to compare qualities or actions using comparative and superlative forms.

Adjective: She is a quick learner.
Adverb: She learns quickly.

Adjective or Adverb?

Adjective Usage

Adjectives usually come before a noun or after linking verbs (be, seem, become, etc.).

The test was difficult. (after linking verb)
He gave a brilliant answer. (before noun)

Adverb Usage

Adverbs often end in -ly and describe how something is done. They modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.

She answered quickly. (modifies verb)
He is extremely talented. (modifies adjective)
Tip: Ask “what kind?” for adjectives and “how?” or “to what extent?” for adverbs.
