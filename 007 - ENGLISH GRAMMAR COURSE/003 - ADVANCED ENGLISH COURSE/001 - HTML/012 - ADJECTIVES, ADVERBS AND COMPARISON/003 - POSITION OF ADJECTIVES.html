Advanced Grammar – Position of Adjectives

Understanding the Position of Adjectives

Adjectives usually appear in two main positions: before a noun (attributive position) or after a linking verb (predicative position). Some adjectives can only be used in one position, while most can be used in both.

Attributive: The happy child ran outside.
Predicative: The child is happy.

Examples and Usage

Attributive Position (Before Noun)

She wore a beautiful dress.
They live in a quiet neighborhood.

Predicative Position (After Linking Verb)

The dress is beautiful.
The neighborhood seems quiet.

Adjectives with Restricted Position

The president elect will speak. (only attributive)
She is asleep. (only predicative)
Tip: Most adjectives can be used in both positions, but some are restricted to one. Learn the exceptions!
