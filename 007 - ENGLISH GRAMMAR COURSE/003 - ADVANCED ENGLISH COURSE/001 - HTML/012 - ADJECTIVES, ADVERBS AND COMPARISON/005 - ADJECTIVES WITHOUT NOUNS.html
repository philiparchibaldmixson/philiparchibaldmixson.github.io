Advanced Grammar – Adjectives Without Nouns

Understanding Adjectives Without Nouns

Adjectives are usually used with nouns, but in some cases, they can stand alone and act as nouns. These adjectives refer to a group or category of people or things and are often preceded by “the”.

Examples: the rich, the poor, the elderly, the unknown, the injured

These expressions are plural and refer to people in general. They are used as collective nouns and take plural verbs.

Examples and Usage

Common Adjectives Used Without Nouns

The rich often live in luxury.
The elderly need special care.
The injured were taken to the hospital.

Abstract or General Use

The unknown can be frightening.
The unexpected happened during the meeting.
Tip: These adjectives act as nouns and refer to a group or concept. They are always preceded by “the.”
