Advanced Grammar – Structures After Adjectives

Understanding Structures After Adjectives

Adjectives are often followed by specific grammatical structures. These include:

  • Adjective + to-infinitive: She is happy to help.
  • Adjective + that-clause: I’m glad that you came.
  • Adjective + preposition + noun/gerund: He’s interested in learning.
These patterns help express reactions, attitudes, and relationships between ideas.

Examples and Usage

Adjective + to-infinitive

She was eager to begin.
It’s difficult to explain.

Adjective + that-clause

I’m surprised that he left early.
We’re happy that you joined us.

Adjective + preposition + noun/gerund

He’s afraid of flying.
They’re excited about the trip.
Tip: Learn which adjectives pair with which structures. Some adjectives only work with one type.
