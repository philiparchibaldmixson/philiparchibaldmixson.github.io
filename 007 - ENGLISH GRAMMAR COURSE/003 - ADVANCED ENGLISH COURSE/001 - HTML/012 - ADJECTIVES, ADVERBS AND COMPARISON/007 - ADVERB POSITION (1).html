Advanced Grammar – Adverb Position

Understanding Adverb Position

Adverbs can appear in different positions depending on their type and function. The three main positions are:

  • Front position: Before the subject (e.g., Suddenly, he stood up.)
  • Mid position: Between the subject and main verb (e.g., He always arrives early.)
  • End position: After the verb or object (e.g., She spoke clearly.)
The position depends on the type of adverb: frequency, manner, time, place, degree, etc.

Examples and Usage

Frequency Adverbs (usually mid position)

He often goes to the gym.
They rarely complain.

Manner Adverbs (usually end position)

She danced gracefully.
He spoke loudly.

Time and Place Adverbs (usually end or front position)

Tomorrow, we will leave early.
We met at the park.
Tip: Frequency adverbs go before the main verb but after “be.” Manner, time, and place adverbs often go at the end.
