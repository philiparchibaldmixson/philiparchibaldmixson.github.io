Advanced Grammar – Adverb Position: With the Verb

Adverb Placement with Verbs

Adverbs can appear in different positions depending on the verb type. With simple verbs, adverbs of frequency (e.g., always, never, often) usually go before the main verb. With auxiliary verbs (e.g., have, be, will), they go after the first auxiliary.

Examples:
She always arrives early. (simple verb)
He has never been late. (auxiliary verb)
They will probably come tomorrow. (modal verb)

Examples and Usage

With Simple Verbs

I usually walk to work.
She often reads before bed.

With Auxiliary Verbs

He has always wanted to travel.
They are frequently late.

With Modal Verbs

You should definitely try it.
We might never know the truth.
Tip: Place the adverb after the first verb in verb phrases. Avoid splitting verb + object unnecessarily.
