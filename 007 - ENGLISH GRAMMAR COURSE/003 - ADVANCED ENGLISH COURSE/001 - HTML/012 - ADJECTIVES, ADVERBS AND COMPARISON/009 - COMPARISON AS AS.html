Advanced Grammar – As ... As

Understanding “As ... As” Comparisons

The structure as + adjective/adverb + as is used to show equality or lack of equality between two things. It can be used in affirmative, negative, and interrogative sentences.

Examples:
She is as tall as her brother. (equal)
He isn’t as fast as his teammate. (not equal)
Is this book as interesting as the last one? (question)

Examples and Usage

Affirmative Comparison

This car is as expensive as that one.
She works as efficiently as her colleague.

Negative Comparison

He isn’t as confident as he used to be.
The movie wasn’t as exciting as expected.

Interrogative Comparison

Is this solution as effective as the previous one?
Do they perform as well as the other team?
Tip: Use “as ... as” to compare qualities or actions. Add “not” for negative comparisons.
