Superlatives

Advanced Grammar – More About Superlatives

Understanding Superlatives in Depth

Superlatives are used to show that something has the highest or lowest degree of a quality within a group. They are often formed by adding -est to short adjectives or using most before longer adjectives.

Examples:
She is the fastest runner in the team.
That was the most impressive

Examples and Usage

Regular Superlatives

This is the tallest building in the city.
He’s the youngest player on the team.

Using “Most” with Long Adjectives

She gave the most thoughtful answer.
It’s the most comfortable chair I’ve ever sat in.

Irregular Superlatives

This is the best cake I’ve ever tasted. (good → best)
That was the worst decision. (bad → worst)
Tip: Use “the” before superlatives. Don’t use “most” and “-est” together (e.g., ✗ the most fastest).
