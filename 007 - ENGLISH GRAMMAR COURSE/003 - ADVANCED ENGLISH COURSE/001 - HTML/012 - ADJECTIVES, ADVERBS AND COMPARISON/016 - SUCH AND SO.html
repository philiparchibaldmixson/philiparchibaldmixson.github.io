Advanced Grammar – Such and So

Understanding “Such” and “So”

“Such” and “so” are intensifiers used to emphasize qualities. They are used differently depending on the structure of the sentence.

SUCH + (adjective) + noun: It was such a beautiful day.
SO + adjective/adverb: The day was so beautiful.

Examples and Usage

Using “Such”

They are such talented musicians.
It was such an interesting book.
She’s such a kind person.

Using “So”

The music was so loud.
He ran so quickly.
I’m so proud of you.
Tip: Use “such” before a noun (with or without an adjective). Use “so” before an adjective or adverb alone.
