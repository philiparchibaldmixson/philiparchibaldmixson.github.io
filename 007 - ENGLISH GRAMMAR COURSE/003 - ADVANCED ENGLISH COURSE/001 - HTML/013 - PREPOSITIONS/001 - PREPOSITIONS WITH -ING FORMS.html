Advanced Grammar – Prepositions with -ing Forms

Understanding Prepositions with -ing Forms

When a verb follows a preposition, it must take the -ing form (gerund). This rule applies to all prepositions, including compound ones like “in spite of,” “without,” and “after.”

Examples:
She’s interested in learning Spanish.
He left without saying goodbye.
After finishing the project, we celebrated.

Examples and Usage

Common Prepositions + -ing

They succeeded in completing the task.
We talked about moving abroad.
She apologized for being late.

Compound Prepositions + -ing

In spite of feeling tired, he kept working.
Thanks to studying hard, she passed the exam.
By practicing daily, he improved quickly.
Tip: Never use the base form or infinitive after a preposition. Always use the -ing form.
