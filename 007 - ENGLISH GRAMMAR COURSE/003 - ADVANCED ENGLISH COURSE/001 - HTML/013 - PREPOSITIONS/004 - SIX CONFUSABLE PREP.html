Advanced Grammar – Six Confusable Prepositions

Understanding Confusable Prepositions

These six prepositions are often confused due to overlapping meanings or similar contexts. Knowing when and how to use them correctly improves clarity and precision.

Focus: Between vs. Among, By vs. Until, During vs. For

Examples and Usage

Between vs. Among

The money was divided between two people. (used with two)
The money was shared among the team members. (used with more than two)

By vs. Until

Finish the report by Friday. (deadline)
Stay here until Friday. (duration)

During vs. For

She slept during the movie. (when something happens)
She slept for two hours. (how long something lasts)
