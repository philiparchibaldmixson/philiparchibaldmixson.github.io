Advanced Grammar – Six Confusable Prepositions

Understanding Confusable Prepositions

These six prepositions are often confused due to similar meanings or overlapping usage. Knowing their distinctions helps clarify relationships, agents, and spatial references.

Focus: With vs. By, With vs. By in Passive Clauses, Besides vs. Except, Opposite vs. In Front Of

Examples and Usage

With vs. By

He painted the wall with a brush. (instrument)
The wall was painted by the artist. (agent)

With vs. By in Passive Clauses

The cake was decorated with strawberries. (material)
The cake was decorated by the chef. (person who did it)

Besides vs. Except

Besides John, everyone attended. (John + others)
Everyone attended except John. (everyone but John)

Opposite vs. In Front Of

The bank is opposite the supermarket. (facing across)
He parked in front of the bank. (directly before it)
