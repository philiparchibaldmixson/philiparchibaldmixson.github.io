Advanced Grammar – AND and OR in Clauses and Tenses

Understanding AND and OR

“And” and “or” are coordinating conjunctions used to connect words, phrases, or clauses. They affect meaning, verb agreement, and sometimes tense consistency.

AND adds or combines ideas.
OR offers alternatives or choices.

Examples and Usage

AND – Combining Clauses and Actions

She studied hard and passed the exam.
He writes novels and teaches literature.

OR – Offering Alternatives

You can call me or send an email.
Will you stay or leave?

Verb Agreement and Tense Consistency

Neither the manager nor the assistants were available. (plural agreement)
She will study or she will travel. (parallel future tense)
Tip: Use parallel structure with “and” and “or” to maintain clarity and grammatical accuracy.
