Advanced Grammar – Double Conjunctions: BOTH ... AND / (N)EITHER ... (N)OR

Understanding Double Conjunctions

Double conjunctions are used to link two parallel elements in a sentence. They emphasize inclusion or exclusion and require parallel grammatical structure.

BOTH ... AND adds two elements.
EITHER ... OR offers two alternatives.
NEITHER ... NOR excludes both options.

Examples and Usage

BOTH ... AND

Both the manager and the assistant attended the meeting.
She is both intelligent and creative.

EITHER ... OR

You can either call me or send a message.
Either the teacher or the students made the mistake.

NEITHER ... NOR

Neither the printer nor the scanner is working.
He speaks neither Spanish nor Portuguese.
Tip: Maintain parallel structure after each conjunction. Subject-verb agreement depends on the second element.
