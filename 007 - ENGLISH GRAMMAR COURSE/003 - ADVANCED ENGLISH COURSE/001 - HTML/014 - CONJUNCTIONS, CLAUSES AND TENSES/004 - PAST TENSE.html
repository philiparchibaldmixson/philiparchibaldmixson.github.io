Advanced Grammar – Past Tense with Present or Future Meaning

What Does It Mean?

In English, the past tense is sometimes used to express present politeness, hypothetical situations, or future plans

Examples:
I wanted to ask you something. (present polite)
If I went She said she was leaving

Examples and Usage

Politeness with Past Tense

I was wondering if you could help me.
Did you want to join us?

Hypothetical Clauses

If I knew the answer, I’d tell you.
If she studied more, she would pass.

Reported Speech with Future Meaning

He said he was going to call me later.
They told us they were leaving next week.
Tip: Past tense doesn’t always refer to past time. Context and clause type determine its meaning.
