Understanding Prepositions in Relative Clauses

In adjective (relative) clauses, prepositions can appear in two positions: before the relative pronoun or at the end

Formal: The person to whom I spoke was helpful.
Informal: The person who I spoke to was helpful.

Examples and Usage

Preposition at the End (Informal)

The book that I was looking for is here.
The person who I talked to was kind.

Preposition Before the Pronoun (Formal)

The book for which I was looking is here.
The person to whom I talked was kind.
Tip: Use “whom” or “which” after prepositions in formal writing. Use “who” or “that” with prepositions at the end in informal speech.
