Advanced Grammar – Verbs in That-Clauses: Should

Understanding “Should” in That-Clauses

In formal English, certain verbs followed by “that” can use should + base verb to express advice, necessity, or urgency. This structure is an alternative to the subjunctive and is common in British English.

Common verbs: suggest, recommend, insist, demand, propose, request
Structure: Subject + verb + that + subject + should + base verb

Examples and Usage

Should in That-Clauses

She suggested that he should study more.
They recommended that we should leave early.
The manager insisted that the report should be revised.

Negative and Passive Forms

He demanded that the rule should not apply to him.
It was proposed that the meeting should be postponed.
Tip: “Should” adds clarity and formality. It’s optional in American English but preferred in British usage.
