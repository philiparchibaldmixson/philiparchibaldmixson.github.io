Advanced Grammar – More About Question-Word Clauses

Understanding Question-Word Clauses

Question-word clauses are noun clauses introduced by words like what, who, where, why, how, and when. They are used to report questions or express uncertainty, and they follow statement word order (not question word order).

Structure: Subject + verb + question word + subject + verb
Important: Do not use auxiliary inversion (e.g., “do”, “are”) in noun clauses.

Examples and Usage

Question-Word Clauses as Objects

I don’t know where she went.
He asked what they wanted.
We’re not sure why he left early.

Question-Word Clauses as Subjects

What she said surprised everyone.
How they solved the problem was impressive.
Tip: Use statement word order: “what she said” (not “what did she say”).
