Advanced Grammar – Preparatory It

Understanding Preparatory “It”

In formal English, we often use preparatory it to introduce a noun clause that acts as the subject. This structure helps improve sentence flow and emphasize the main idea.

Structure: It + be + adjective/noun + that-clause
Function: The noun clause is postponed and introduced by “it”

Examples and Usage

Preparatory It with Adjectives

It is important that you arrive on time.
It is surprising that she won the prize.

Preparatory It with Nouns

It’s a fact that he lied.
It’s a shame that they canceled the event.
Tip: Use preparatory “it” to shift long or complex noun clauses to the end of the sentence.
