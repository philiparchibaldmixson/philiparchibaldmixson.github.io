Adverb Clauses

Advanced Grammar – After + -ing, On + -ing, etc.

Understanding Preposition + -ing Clauses

Some adverb clauses can be reduced using prepositions followed by a gerund (-ing form). These structures are concise and often used in formal or academic writing. They express time, cause, condition, or sequence.

Common patterns: after + -ing, before + -ing, on + -ing, by + -ing, without + -ing
Function: Show time, method, condition, or contrast

Examples and Usage

Time and Sequence

After finishing the report, she left the office.
On entering the room, he noticed the silence.

Cause, Condition, and Contrast

By working hard, he achieved success.
Without knowing the truth, they made a decision.
Tip: These structures are not full clauses—they don’t have a subject. The subject must match the main clause.
