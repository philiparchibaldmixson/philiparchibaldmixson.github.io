Advanced Grammar – Information Structure: Getting the Right Subject

Choosing the Right Subject

In advanced writing, the subject of a sentence plays a key role in organizing information. Choosing the right subject helps maintain flow, clarity, and emphasis. Writers often select subjects based on what is already known (given) or what they want to highlight (new).

Key principle: Choose a subject that connects smoothly with the previous sentence or sets up the main idea clearly.

Examples and Usage

Maintaining Topic Continuity

The new policy was introduced last week. It aims to reduce costs. ✅
The new policy was introduced last week. Managers aim to reduce costs. ❌

Highlighting the Main Idea

What surprised everyone was the speed of the decision.
The speed of the decision surprised everyone.

Avoiding Subject Shifts

The team completed the project. They celebrated afterward. ✅
The team completed the project. The celebration was loud. ❌ (shifted focus)
Tip: Use pronouns, cleft sentences, or passive voice strategically to keep the subject aligned with the topic.

📝 Practice Exercises

Exercise 1: Identify the better subject

  • The report was submitted yesterday. __________ will be reviewed tomorrow. (It / The committee)
  • We discussed the proposal. __________ was controversial. (It / The meeting)
  • The results were unexpected. __________ shocked the team. (They / The announcement)
  • She gave a speech. __________ inspired many. (It / The audience)
