Advanced Grammar – Special Word Order: Fronting

What Is Fronting?

Fronting is a technique where a word or phrase that usually comes later in a sentence is moved to the beginning for emphasis, contrast, or cohesion. It’s common in formal writing and speech when the writer wants to highlight a particular element.

Structure: Fronted element + comma + main clause
Purpose: Emphasis, contrast, cohesion, or stylistic variation

Examples and Usage

Fronting for Emphasis

At the end of the street stood a tall oak tree.
What he wanted most was peace.

Fronting for Contrast or Focus

This book I’ve read, but that one I haven’t.
Never have I seen such chaos.

Fronting for Cohesion

As for the budget, it will be reviewed tomorrow.
Regarding your request, we’ll respond shortly.
Tip: Fronted elements are often followed by a comma. Use fronting sparingly for stylistic effect.
