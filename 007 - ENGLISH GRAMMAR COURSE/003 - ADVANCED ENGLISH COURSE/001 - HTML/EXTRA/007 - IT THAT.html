Advanced Grammar – Emphasis: It...That Structure

What Is the “It...That” Structure?

The “It...that” structure is a cleft sentence used to emphasize a specific part of a sentence. It allows writers to highlight subjects, objects, or adverbials by placing them between “It is/was” and “that.” This structure is common in formal writing and speech.

Structure: It + be verb + emphasized element + that + rest of sentence
Purpose: Emphasis, contrast, clarification

Examples and Usage

Emphasizing the Subject

It was John who solved the problem.
It is Maria who leads the team.

Emphasizing the Object

It was the report that caused confusion.
It is this book that I recommend.

Emphasizing Time or Place

It was yesterday that they arrived.
It is in Brazil that the event will be held.
Tip: Use “It...that” to draw attention to important details or clarify ambiguous sentences.
