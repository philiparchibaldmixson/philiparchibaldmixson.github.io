Grammar Beyond the Sentence

Advanced Grammar – Complex Noun Phrases in Writing

What Are Complex Noun Phrases?

A complex noun phrase is a noun plus all the words that modify it. In academic and professional writing, these phrases often include adjectives, prepositional phrases, relative clauses, and participles. They allow writers to pack more information into fewer words, but they can be difficult to read or write clearly.

Structure: Noun + modifiers (adjectives, prepositional phrases, clauses)
Purpose: Precision, density, formal tone

Examples and Usage

With Prepositional Phrases

The results of the recent survey on consumer behavior were surprising.
A study from the University of São Paulo revealed new trends.

With Relative Clauses

The proposal that was submitted last week received approval.
The students who participated in the experiment showed improvement.

With Participles and Adjectives

The rising cost of imported goods affects local businesses.
The exhausted team continued working late into the night.
Tip: To understand or revise complex noun phrases, identify the core noun and then simplify or reorder modifiers.
