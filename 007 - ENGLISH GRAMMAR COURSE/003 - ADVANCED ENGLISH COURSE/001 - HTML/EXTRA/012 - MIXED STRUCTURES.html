Grammar Beyond the Sentence

Advanced Grammar – Mixed Structures

What Are Mixed Structures?

Mixed structures occur when parts of a sentence don’t match grammatically or logically. This often happens when writers combine sentence patterns incorrectly, shift subjects or tenses mid-sentence, or leave out necessary connectors. These errors can confuse readers and weaken writing clarity.

Common causes: sentence blending, faulty comparisons, unclear references, mismatched clauses

Examples and Corrections

Faulty Sentence Blending

While reading the article, the phone rang. ❌
While I was reading the article, the phone rang. ✅

Unclear or Illogical Subject

To improve results, the experiment was repeated. ❌
To improve results, the researchers repeated the experiment. ✅

Mixed Comparison or Structure

She enjoys hiking more than her brother. ❌ (ambiguous)
She enjoys hiking more than her brother does. ✅
Tip: Read aloud and check for logical flow. Make sure each part of the sentence connects clearly and grammatically.
