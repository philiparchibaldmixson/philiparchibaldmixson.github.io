Advanced Grammar – Three Kinds of Spoken Question

What Are the Three Kinds?

Spoken English uses three main types of questions to manage interaction and express attitude. These are:

1. Real Questions: Ask for information
2. Checking Questions: Confirm or clarify
3. Rhetorical Questions: Express emotion or make a point

Examples and Usage

Real Questions

“Where are you going?”
“What time does it start?”

Checking Questions

“You’re coming with us, right?”
“She’s already left, hasn’t she?”

Rhetorical Questions

“Why would anyone do that?”
“Isn’t it obvious?”
Tip: Intonation and context help listeners understand the speaker’s intent. Rhetorical questions often don’t expect an answer.
